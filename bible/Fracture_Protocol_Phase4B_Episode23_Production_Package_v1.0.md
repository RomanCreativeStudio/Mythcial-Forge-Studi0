## 3. Scene Breakdown

- Purpose: Open directly on the disaster beginning to unfold.
- Location: A populated civilian area within an already-Locked location category (Lower Sector or Mid Zone, World Bible §3) — no new named district or landmark.
- Characters present: Unnamed civilians; unnamed response personnel from multiple institutions.
- Emotional state: Escalating, uncertain — the moment before consequence becomes irreversible.
- Conflict: The Doctrine's reach crosses from persuasion and expansion into an act that can't be walked back.
- Visual requirements: Restrained staging, consistent with Episode 11's own tonal standard — clear and unambiguous without being graphic; no new effect or technology introduced.

- Production needs: No new location asset beyond an already-established category; no new named character asset built for this document (names reserved for script stage).

**Scene 2 — Too Late From Every Direction**
- Purpose: Show each faction's partial response arriving without coordination.
- Location: Same as Scene 1, and the institutional spaces established in Episode 22.

- Emotional state: Urgent, isolated — each institution genuinely trying, none succeeding, none aware of what the others know.
- Conflict: Political division and institutional delay — the same systemic failure that caused the Sol Collapse — prevents any single actor from arriving in time or with sufficient capability.
- Visual requirements: Cross-cutting between the three institutional responses, using already-established environments from Episodes 13–22; no new visual technology.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should convey genuine effort undercut by structural failure, not incompetence or malice.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 3 — What Is Lost**
- Purpose: Land the disaster's human cost.
- Location: Same as Scene 1.
- Characters present: Multiple named-but-not-core-cast civilians (specific names reserved for the script-writing stage, per this document's own primary scope note); unnamed response personnel.
- Emotional state: Grief landing directly, without narrative cushioning.
- Conflict: The loss itself — rendered with clarity and restraint, never staged for spectacle, consistent with Episode 11's own tonal standard.

- Audio requirements: A drop into near-silence at the moment of loss, consistent with Episode 11's own audio standard — no swelling score.
- Production needs: No new location or effect asset; named-civilian character assets deferred to script stage, flagged for future Asset Registry entries once names are assigned.

**Scene 4 — No Side Free of This**
- Purpose: Show each faction confronting that its own answer was not sufficient.
- Location: The institutional spaces established across Episodes 13–22.
- Characters present: Unnamed Light Order, Night Haven, and Ascendant Network personnel, each within their own institution.
- Emotional state: Sober, shaken — not blame directed outward, but each institution facing its own share of the failure.
- Conflict: None external — the scene exists to demonstrate, through each faction's own reaction, that no single answer proved sufficient (Phase 4A.2, Episode 23, Character Progression).
- Visual requirements: Same environment categories as prior institutional scenes; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should stay grave and restrained — no character delivers the episode's thesis as spoken commentary; the proof is structural, shown through consequence, not argued aloud.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 5 — Kael Witnesses**
- Purpose: Give Kael direct proximity to the loss's scale, without making it a personal-flaw beat.
- Location: Same as Scene 1, in the aftermath, or Ward Station Seven receiving the report.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed response personnel.
- Emotional state: Kael — sobered by the scale, understanding it as civilizational rather than personal, distinct from Episode 11's intimate loss.
- Conflict: None external — this is a witnessing beat, not a confrontation; per this document's own scope, nothing here tests or resolves his burden-alone flaw, which remains Episode 21's territory, not this episode's.
- Visual requirements: Same environment categories; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Minimal dialogue, carried by restraint — intent only.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 6 — Uncushioned**
- Purpose: Close the episode without resolution, per its own explicit Ending Beat.
- Location: Any of the episode's established environments.
- Characters present: Institutional presence only, no single named core-cast focus required.
- Emotional state: Grave, unresolved — the loss stands without comfort or immediate lesson.
- Conflict: None — the scene exists specifically to withhold resolution, mirroring Episode 11's own closing standard at a larger scale.
- Visual requirements: Quiet, still framing; no visual metaphor implying closure.
