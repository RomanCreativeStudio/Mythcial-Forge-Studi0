## 1. Purpose

This document permanently defines how visual manifestations are established for entities whose narrative identity remains intentionally unknown — in this saga, exclusively the Guardian. It provides production authority only. Consistent with the Founder Directive's own explicit prohibition, it does not and must not define: origin, creator, true nature, purpose, destiny, relationship to future revelations, or the Guardian's true relationship to Kael, Titan, the Fracture, or humanity beyond what Phase 1C's Character Bible and Relationship Dynamics already establish. Every one of those remains permanently unresolved, exactly as Phase 5A.2 already locks in place.

---

## 2. Visual Manifestation Rule (Locked)

A visual manifestation exists solely because production requires something to animate. Visual appearance never implies narrative explanation. Appearance is not identity. Form is not origin. Silhouette is not purpose. Movement is not revelation.

The audience may see the Guardian. The audience must never understand the Guardian simply because it is visible.

**This principle is the design foundation for every decision in Section 4, below**: every visual choice made here is deliberately ordinary and unremarkable, precisely because an ordinary, unremarkable appearance is the one design direction that shows the Guardian without answering anything about it. A distinctive, exotic, or symbolically loaded design would itself function as a narrative claim; a deliberately generic one does not.

## 4. Visual Authority

Production-visible qualities only. No lore is defined in this section.

### Overall Silhouette
Humanoid-proportioned, adult-human scale. Deliberately generic: no wings, halo, visible mechanical framework, elongation, or other non-human anatomical marker that would function as an implicit answer to the origin mystery. The silhouette must read, at a glance, as an ordinary person occupying the frame.

### Visual Scale
Fixed at ordinary adult-human scale in every scene. No scale shifts for dramatic or spectacle purposes (no towering, no shrinking, no scale-based "reveal" beat) — spectacle-through-scale would itself be a form of unveiling, which Section 2's Visual Manifestation Rule forbids.

### Body Proportions
Ordinary humanoid proportions throughout. No stylization that reads as explicitly robotic, ethereal, crystalline, or otherwise inhuman.

### Surface Treatment
Matte, non-reflective, non-luminous, non-translucent. No particle effects, no energy patterning, no metallic sheen, no glow of any kind. At the level of surface rendering, the Guardian must be visually indistinguishable from an ordinary human presence under the same lighting.

### Motion Language
Measured, economical, unhurried. No sudden, erratic, or mechanically precise movement. Directly extends the Locked Episode 8 production note that "the Guardian's slightly longer pause before speaking is the only permitted signal of its underlying weight, nothing more overt" — the same restraint governs its physical motion, not only its speech.

### Facial Language
Calm and settled. Legible enough to support the already-Locked 10-expression Expression Library (Character Production Package, Section 5), but deliberately unremarkable — no non-human eyes, no visible mechanism, no uncanny stillness played for effect, and no features distinctive enough to suggest a specific ethnicity, culture, or era that would itself imply an origin claim.

### Presence
Ambient and settled by default, never intrusive, never announced by the frame — directly extending Episode 4's Locked Story Purpose note, "establish the Guardian as ambient civilizational presence before any personal contact." It occupies space; it does not command it.

### Lighting Behavior
Obeys ordinary practical lighting exactly as any human character does: receives and casts shadow, is subject to falloff and occlusion, and is never separately lit, rim-lit, or highlighted relative to its surroundings. **Explicit cross-check against an existing Locked rule, not a new invention:** the Guardian is never rendered in the reserved cyan/icy-blue palette (`mythic-forge-art-bible/color-language.md`, Master System Prompt Section 4 authority — reserved exclusively for Fracture anomalies, Kael's eye-glow, Cipher-work, and Breach residue, "never used decoratively"). This directly formalizes the already-Locked Episode 4 production note that "the Guardian's presence is not marked by any lighting change."

### Environment Interaction
Physically integrated into every environment it appears in — no floating, no independent light source, no environmental distortion around it. Interacts with set geometry exactly as a human character would (can be seated, can stand beside furniture, can share a doorway or frame edge).

### Distance Behavior
No forced-perspective or scale trickery at any camera distance. Wide shots and close-ups both treat it with the same physical consistency as any character — consistent with Visual Scale, above.

### Camera Behavior
Never given a "reveal" treatment. No rack-focus unveiling, no dramatic re-framing cut tied to its appearance in a scene. Directly formalizes the Locked Episode 4 production note: "A held two-shot once the Guardian speaks — no cutting away to 'reveal' it dramatically; its presence is simply established, not unveiled." Held two-shots are the default camera grammar for any scene pairing it with another character.

### Color Philosophy
Neutral, unmarked tones. No new signature color is assigned to the Guardian by this document — deliberately withheld, since assigning one would itself function as a visual answer about its nature (a violation of Section 2's Visual Manifestation Rule). The one binding rule is negative, not positive: never the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue (see Lighting Behavior, above).

### Symbolic Design Language
No iconography that reads as explicitly religious, mythological, or overtly mechanical/technological — avoiding visual tropes ("angelic," "machine-god," "ancient astronaut," etc.) that would function as an implicit answer to the origin mystery. Symbolic restraint is itself the symbolic design language.

### Visual Consistency Rules
Every rule above must render identically across all episodes and all promotional/marketing assets — the same standard already Locked for Kael's eye-glow specification. No episode-specific, mood-specific, or "Signal-state"-linked redesign exists for the Guardian, since no such state is Locked for it (unlike Kael's Signal-Sensitivity-linked eye-glow, which remains exclusively his).
