## 1. Visual Identity Definition

### A. Facial Structure

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Face shape | Softer, naturalistically rounded-to-oval facial geometry — approachable rather than sharp or angular. |
| Facial proportions | Ordinary, naturalistic human proportions; no idealized model-perfect symmetry and no exaggerated anime-stylized feature scaling (oversized eyes, tiny nose/mouth) — compatible with the semi-realistic anime cinematic hybrid register's own existing rule against flat/exaggerated stylization (`forbidden-elements.md`; `visual-development-guide.md`). |
| Distinctive facial characteristics | Warmth communicated through a softer, less tension-carrying brow and eye-area than Kael's, and a more naturally relaxed default mouth-line reflecting quiet approachability; subtle naturalistic asymmetry, not mirror-perfect. |
| Natural age appearance | Visibly 20 — youthful early-adult maturity appropriate to hands-on labor and a lived, displaced backstory; not adolescent-coded, not aged or weathered beyond her years. |
| Realistic human variation | Naturalistic, not hyper-symmetric or idealized — directly restates Phase 6B.2 item 5's "no stylized idealization" and item 6's "trustworthy and steady rather than dramatic." |

**Differentiation from Kael, explicit and checkable:** Kael's Locked facial structure is "open, direct, sincere features; youthful (age 19) but visibly carrying strain and maturity" (Phase 6B.2 item 6, Kael entry) — a face shaped by Cadet training and combat proximity. Mira's is warm/approachable/steady rather than strain-carrying, softer rather than sharp, civilian rather than military-adjacent. Both entries are quoted, not altered — Kael's own document is not touched by this Specification.

### B. Hair Design

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Hair color range | Within the natural Spanish/Iberian ancestral range — predominantly dark brown to black, the statistically most common range across Iberia, with lighter brown genuinely possible and not excluded. No single value is Locked; concept-art finalization selects within this range. |
| Hair texture | Any naturalistic human hair texture (straight through wavy) is compatible; no single texture is mandated. |
| Hair length | Open — either practically tied-back medium-to-long hair, or a practical short-to-medium cut, both satisfy Phase 6B.2 item 8's "kept out of the way... e.g., tied back" (an example, not the only compliant styling). No single length is mandated. |
| Typical hairstyle | Practical, functional — tied back (braid, ponytail, or bun) when working, or a practical short/medium cut; never elaborate, ornamental, or vanity-styled (Phase 6B.2 item 8, restated). |
| Practicality for reconstruction work | Must never obstruct hands-on work or vision; functional presentation always takes priority over any styling choice. |

### C. Eye Design

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Natural eye color range | Within natural human variation compatible with Spanish/Iberian ancestry — commonly dark brown, with lighter brown or hazel also common, and green or lighter eyes genuinely possible (more associated with Northern Iberia) though less common. No single value is Locked; concept-art finalization selects within this range, consistent with Phase 6B.2 item 7's "warm, steady" direction. |
| Eye shape | Naturalistic, expressive human eye shape — no exaggerated anime-stylized oversized or simplified eye geometry, consistent with the semi-realistic register's own detailed, naturalistic facial rendering. |
| Emotional visual quality | Warm, steady, expressive — capable of conveying quiet resilience and restrained emotion (per her Locked "carried, not cured" internal-conflict guidance, Voice Requirements), never glowing. |

**Absolute requirements, restated from already-Locked Do Not Do rules, not new:** human eyes only. No glow. No Signal association of any kind. No cyan/icy-blue visual language under any lighting condition. No artificial effects. (Phase 6B.2 item 7, item 20; `color-language.md`'s reservation of cyan/icy-blue exclusively to Kael's Signal-linked phenomena.)

### D. Skin Tone / Complexion Range

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Heritage-informed natural range | Spans the genuinely wide natural range within Spanish/Iberian ancestry — from fair/light tones (more associated with Northern Iberia) through olive/tan tones (more associated with Southern Iberia and the wider Mediterranean). No single tone is Locked; any point within this real, respectful range is compliant. |
| Complexion characteristics | Ordinary civilian texture with plausible outdoor/fieldwork weathering consistent with reconstruction labor — not idealized flawlessness (Phase 6B.2 item 9, restated, now paired explicitly with the ancestral range). |
| Visual consistency rule | Whichever specific tone is selected at first Concept Art finalization becomes the binding reference for all subsequent assets, per Phase 6B.2 item 22's general Visual Consistency Rule — the range is open only until that first selection, not permanently open per-asset. |

### E. Body Silhouette

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Height range | Approximately 163–170 cm (5'4"–5'7") — Locked, Phase 6B.2 item 4, restated unchanged. |
| Build | Ordinary, capable civilian build — no stylized idealization (Phase 6B.2 item 5, restated). |
| Physical presence | Practical civilian-worker silhouette — unglamorous, functional, immediately legible as someone who works with her hands rather than someone dressed for spectacle (Phase 6B.2 item 3, restated). |
| Posture | Open and grounded by default; visibly closes or guards slightly in scenes touching her displacement backstory or trust-related tension (Phase 6B.2 item 12, restated). |
| General silhouette | Practical civilian-worker silhouette overall, defined by civilian work-wear (Phase 6B.3 §3, cross-referenced, not altered), not a tactical or military garment category. |

**Differentiation from Kael, explicit and checkable:** Kael's Locked build is "lean-athletic, Cadet-trained... athletic-functional proportions" (Phase 6B.2 items 3–5, Kael entry) in a dark techwear/tactical silhouette. Mira's is an "ordinary, capable civilian build" in a civilian work-wear silhouette — a genuinely different build type and garment category, not merely a recolor of the same shape. Kael's own document is not touched by this Specification.

### F. Signature Identity Features

| Feature | Category | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Hands** | Work-worn detail (primary) | Her single strongest, highest-priority signature feature — Locked, Phase 6B.2 item 10/15 ("her equivalent of Kael's eye-glow"). Visibly marked by hands-on reconstruction work: calluses, small working scars, practical unpolished nails. Must never be rendered clean, unmarked, or gloved by default. |
| Practical hairstyling | Hairstyle element (secondary) | Consistently practical, never elaborate — a recognizable silhouette element precisely because of its plainness, distinct from any character with a more deliberate/styled look. |
| Grounded, quiet expression quality | Expression quality (secondary) | Warm/steady default, restrained under tension — a recognizable emotional throughline, deliberately distinct from Kael's more intense, strain-carrying default expression. |
| Work-worn costume details | Personal visual motif (secondary, cross-referenced only) | Her Locked wear-pattern costume detail (Phase 6B.3 §3 — visible material wear, practical field/home repairs, functional utility pockets) reinforces her "career history" identity visually. **Restated by cross-reference only — not created or altered by this document; Phase 6B.3 remains the sole authority for costume content.** |

**Confirmed not introduced, per this section's explicit requirement:** no powers, no technology markings, no faction symbols, no supernatural elements of any kind appear anywhere in this Specification.
