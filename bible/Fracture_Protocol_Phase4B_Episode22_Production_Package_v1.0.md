## 1. Episode Identity

- **Episode number:** 22
- **Episode title:** Placeholder only, not final — "Partial Information" (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22 seed entry).
- **Core purpose:** Open Arc Transition Two — establish the threshold the Doctrine's reach is about to cross (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Purpose).
- **Narrative objective:** Show, through institutional structure rather than personal drama, why three factions each holding a piece of the picture still fail to prevent what's coming (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Central Conflict).
- **Emotional objective:** Mounting institutional dread — the audience seeing the mechanism of failure assembling in real time, unable to stop it from the inside.
- **Audience experience goal:** This episode should feel like watching separate rooms each almost understand the full picture, and never quite share it with each other in time.

---

## 2. Story Structure

- **Opening hook:** Three factions — Light Order, Night Haven, and the Ascendant Doctrine — each holding partial information or partial capability, none yet acting decisively (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Beginning State).
- **Setup:** Each faction's partial picture is shown independently, without any of them yet recognizing the full shape of what's forming.
- **Rising conflict:** The Doctrine's reach crosses a threshold — from persuasion and expansion into an act that can't be walked back, echoing Sol-Collapse-scale stakes (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Central Conflict; Phase 2A.2, Section 5).
- **Character turning point:** None — this episode is structural and institutional, establishing the mechanism of the coming loss (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Character Progression).
- **Major reveal or progression:** The three-faction rivalry's cost becomes concrete and imminent rather than abstract (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, World Progression).
- **Climax:** The moment it becomes clear, to the audience if not yet fully to any single faction, that no one is positioned to stop what's coming.
- **Closing emotional beat:** The disaster is now inevitable, though not yet landed (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Ending Beat).
- **Cliffhanger / Transition:** Into the loss itself (Phase 4A.2, Episode 22, Transition; Episode 23's own seed entry).

## 3. Scene Breakdown

*Five scenes proposed, sized for Studio OS's Mainline Episode runtime target. This episode is deliberately structural, cutting between institutions rather than following a single character's throughline.*

**Scene 1 — What Light Order Sees**
- Purpose: Establish Light Order's partial picture.
- Location: Ward Station Seven, an intelligence/briefing context, reused from the season's established asset base.
- Characters present: Kael, present as a witness within his institution; unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th leadership.
- Emotional state: Grave, procedural — the institution sees a piece of the threat, not the whole.
- Conflict: Light Order holds partial information about the Doctrine's movements, insufficient alone to act decisively.
- Visual requirements: Reused Ward Station Seven briefing environment.
- Audio requirements: Procedural dialogue intent conveying urgency without full clarity — intent only.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 2 — What Night Haven Knows**
- Purpose: Establish Night Haven's own, separate partial picture.
- Location: An institutional space consistent with Night Haven's established Episode 14 presence.
- Characters present: Unnamed Night Haven representatives — no new named individual beyond what Episode 14 already established.
- Emotional state: Guarded, independent — Night Haven's own information is real but incomplete, and it does not share it with Light Order.
- Conflict: Night Haven holds a different partial piece of the same threat, kept separate by the same rivalry the season has tracked since Episode 13.
- Visual requirements: Same environment category as Episode 14; no new location, no new Night Haven character design.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should convey competence and independence, not malice — the withholding is structural rivalry, not sabotage.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 3 — What the Doctrine Is Approaching**
- Purpose: Establish the Doctrine's own movement toward the threshold, without specifying its content.
- Location: An institutional or Doctrine-adjacent space, consistent with Episode 15's world-scale treatment.
- Characters present: Unnamed Ascendant Network operatives, rendered institutionally — no named individual beyond Cassian, who does not appear this episode.
- Emotional state: Purposeful, unremarked by the Doctrine itself as anything but continuation of its existing trajectory.
- Conflict: The Doctrine's reach is approaching the threshold from persuasion and expansion into an act that can't be walked back — rendered only at the level of trajectory, not specific event (Phase 2A.2, Section 5).
- Visual requirements: Same environment category established for Ascendant Network content since Episode 15; no new location or iconography beyond what's already Locked.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should stay strictly at the level of trajectory and momentum — no invented specific act, location, or casualty detail.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 4 — Rivalry, Not Coordination**
- Purpose: Render the institutional failure mechanism directly.
- Location: Cutting between Light Order and Night Haven's separate institutional spaces, or a shared but non-communicating moment.
- Characters present: Unnamed Light Order and Night Haven personnel, each unaware of what the other knows.
- Emotional state: Procedural on both sides — no dramatic confrontation, simply parallel institutions that do not talk to each other.
- Conflict: The three factions' rivalry — not malice — is what prevents any single actor from acting decisively, per the seed entry's own Beginning State.
- Visual requirements: Same environment categories as Scenes 1–2; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should make the gap between what each side knows legible to the audience without either side voicing awareness of the other's information.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 5 — Not Yet Landed**
- Purpose: Close the episode on the disaster's inevitability, without dramatizing it.
- Location: Ward Station Seven or a similarly institutional closing space.
- Characters present: Kael, present as witness; unnamed Ward Division personnel.
- Emotional state: Grave, unresolved — a mounting sense that something is coming that no one is positioned to stop.
- Conflict: None new — the scene exists to let the mechanism's inevitability register before Episode 23 lands its consequence.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; no new location.
- Audio requirements: The scene should end without resolution, carrying dread rather than closure into Episode 23.
- Production needs: No new asset.
