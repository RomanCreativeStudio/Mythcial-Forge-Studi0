## Production Notes and Scope Flags (read before the sections below)

- **The follower and incident remain unnamed and unspecified.** This is the single most important production constraint in this episode — no name, no specific act, no specific victim is invented; the incident is described only by its consequence and its relationship to Cassian's own stated limits.
- **This is not Cassian's own action.** It happens in his name, without his sanction, and shakes him precisely because it does — consistent with his Locked status as a philosophical position, not a villain.
- **The Doctrine's internal true-believer/extremist tension is already Locked, not new.** The Faction Bible's own "Supporting Cast Framework" already establishes that some followers exceed Cassian's stated moral limits; this episode makes that latent tension visible and consequential, not invents it.
- **No other core-cast character appears.** Consistent with the seed entry's own scope, this remains a Cassian-centered episode.

## 1. Episode Identity

- **Episode number:** 30
- **Episode title:** Placeholder only, not final — "Without His Compassion" (Phase 4A.3, Episode 30 seed entry).
- **Core purpose:** Deliver the moment a follower applies Cassian's logic without his compassion (Phase 4A.3, Episode 30, Purpose).
- **Narrative objective:** Show Cassian confronting, directly, that his own movement has caused the harm he built it to prevent (Phase 4A.3, Episode 30, Ending Beat).
- **Emotional objective:** Horror and reckoning — not at an external enemy, but at what has been done in his own name.
- **Audience experience goal:** The audience should feel the specific, painful gap between what Cassian believes and what his belief, unchecked by his own compassion, has produced.

## 2. Story Structure

- **Opening hook:** Cassian's doubt, visible since Episode 28 and deepened in Episode 29, not yet acted upon by anyone but him.
- **Setup:** News or evidence of an incident reaches Cassian — an unnamed extremist follower, consistent only with the already-Locked "Supporting Cast Framework" characterization of Doctrine extremists who exceed his own stated moral limits.
- **Rising conflict:** The follower causes exactly the harm Cassian originally set out to prevent (Phase 2C, Cassian §3, §5). The specific incident is deliberately left unspecified in this episode, consistent with established practice.
- **Character turning point:** This is not Cassian's own action — it happens in his name, without his sanction, and shakes him precisely because it does.
- **Major reveal or progression:** The Doctrine's own internal true-believer/extremist tension, already Locked in the Faction Bible, becomes visible and consequential rather than latent.
- **Climax:** Cassian confronting, directly, that his own movement has caused the harm he built it to prevent.
- **Closing emotional beat:** Cassian confronts, directly, that his own movement has caused the harm he built it to prevent (Phase 4A.3, Episode 30, Ending Beat).
- **Cliffhanger / Transition:** Into Cassian visibly shaken (Phase 4A.3, Episode 30, Transition) — not developed here, named only as the structural handoff to Episode 31.

## 3. Scene Breakdown

*Four scenes proposed, matching this season's precedent for a Cassian-centered episode with one external event — comparable in scale to Episode 17's origin-episode structure. Sized for Studio OS's Mainline Episode runtime target.*

**Scene 1 — Word Reaches Him**
- Purpose: Establish that news or evidence of the incident reaches Cassian.
- Location: Cassian's private interior (Episodes 17/24/25/28).
- Characters present: Cassian; unnamed Doctrine personnel delivering the news.
- Emotional state: Cassian — composed, then abruptly still as the information lands.
- Conflict: The incident is reported, not dramatized in specific detail — consistent with this episode's own deliberate unspecification.
- Visual requirements: Same environment as Episodes 17/24/25/28; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should convey the report factually, without inventing named victims or a specific mechanism.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 2 — What It Cost**
- Purpose: Render the consequence's weight without specifying its mechanism.
- Location: Same environment, continuous.
- Characters present: Cassian, alone.
- Emotional state: Horrified, in a controlled way — not collapsing, but unable to look away from what has happened.
- Conflict: The follower's actions exceed the Doctrine's own stated moral limits (Faction Bible, "Supporting Cast Framework") — harm Cassian's own philosophy was meant to prevent, caused by someone claiming to act on it.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; no new location, no specific incident imagery invented.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent, if any, should carry restrained horror.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 3 — Not His Hand**
- Purpose: Establish clearly that this was not Cassian's own action or sanction.
- Location: Same environment, continuous.
- Characters present: Cassian; unnamed Doctrine personnel.
- Emotional state: Cassian — asserting, to himself as much as anyone, that this was not what he intended or ordered.
- Conflict: The gap between Cassian's own stated moral limits and what was done in his name becomes explicit and undeniable.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should keep this scoped to Cassian's own culpability question, not a wider faction conflict scene.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 4 — What His Own Movement Did**
- Purpose: Close the episode on Cassian's direct confrontation with the harm caused in his name.
- Location: Same environment or a natural transition within it.
- Characters present: Cassian, alone.
- Emotional state: Reckoning — the composed philosopher confronting, for the first time, the concrete cost of his own certainty.
- Conflict: None new — Cassian confronts, directly, that his own movement has caused the harm he built it to prevent, per the seed entry's own explicit Ending Beat.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; no new location.
- Audio requirements: The episode should close on unresolved reckoning, not despair or resolution — his shaken state carries into Episode 31.
- Production needs: No new asset.
