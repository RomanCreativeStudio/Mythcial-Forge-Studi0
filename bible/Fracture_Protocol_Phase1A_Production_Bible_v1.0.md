## 1. Core Premise

*Compiled synthesis of explicitly Founder-confirmed facts below — not a Founder-authored logline, and should be refined once one exists.* Following humanity's discovery of an ancient artificial intelligence known as **the Guardian**, the interplanetary community entered the historical era known as **The Fracture Protocol** — an ideological fracture between two rival factions, **Light Order** and **Night Haven**, each shaping humanity's institutions, militaries, and public opinion in pursuit of its own vision of the future the Guardian refuses to choose on humanity's behalf. **Kael**, of the **Light 98th Contubernium**, stands at the center of that unfolding conflict.

## 2. Story Identity

- **Genre:** Science fiction. Confirmed, Locked, non-negotiable absent an explicit future Founder Override: *"The Fracture Protocol remains a sci-fi flagship IP... No genre conversion is authorized."*
- **Creative Register:** storytelling inspired by the scale, emotion, and world-building found in manhwa and manga — explicitly scoped to *storytelling approach only*: cinematic presentation, emotional storytelling, character-driven narratives, progression systems, deep world-building, and memorable characters. This is a presentation/craft influence, not a genre instruction.

The flagship conflict is the ideological struggle between **Light Order** and **Night Haven** (Sections 8–9).

## 5. Historical Foundation

Humanity's discovery of an ancient, highly advanced artificial intelligence — the Guardian (Section 7) — set in motion the chain of events historically known as **The Fracture Protocol** (Section 6).

## 6. The Fracture Protocol

**The Fracture Protocol** is the official historical name for the chain of events that followed humanity's discovery of the Guardian.

**Terminology note, flagged rather than silently resolved:** this is a *second, distinct* use of the term "Fracture Protocol" alongside two that already exist in Locked canon — the franchise's universe-level title (Master System Prompt Section 3: *"Universe name: THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL. This is the only valid universe-level identity"*) and the World Bible's use of "the Fracture Protocol" as the name of Authority's containment protocol for the single megacity (World Bible Section 2: *"the protocol that keeps the city stable *is* the Fracture Protocol"*). A title that is also an in-universe historical term is a common and often elegant convention, not inherently a conflict — but with three candidate referents now attached to one name (franchise title / megacity containment protocol / interplanetary historical era), reconciling them is a genuine open question, not something resolved by this compilation. Not altered here per the "resolve wording inconsistencies only, do not introduce new lore" instruction.

## 7. The Guardian

An ancient, highly advanced artificial intelligence discovered by humanity. Its purpose is to guide humanity toward a better future, but it refuses to force humanity's choices.

**Additional confirmed facts (added 2026-07-10, from the Phase 1B civilization material):**
- The Guardian cannot be controlled through force.
- It evaluates individuals according to principles rather than political affiliation.
- Its knowledge exceeds every artificial intelligence humanity has ever created.
- Although immensely powerful, humanity still does not fully understand its true origins or long-term purpose.

**Core Guardian Principle:**
> "Humanity cannot be led into a future it has not chosen."

**Relationship to the existing Authority System, flagged rather than silently resolved:** the World Bible (Section 4, Locked) already establishes **the Authority System** as *"the single governing force of the megacity — regulated, emotionless, always active... cannot be removed, destroyed, or ignored."* Authority's described character (controlling, regulated, treats residents as registered digital identity) reads in direct tension with the Guardian's described character (refuses to force choices, evaluates by principle rather than control). Whether these are the same entity under two names, two distinct AIs at different scales (city-level vs. civilization-level), or a deliberate thematic contrast the story is built to explore is not stated in any directive received so far. This document does not invent an answer — "Authority System" is not one of this compilation's requested topics, and connecting the two would be inventing new lore, not compiling given lore. Flagged here as the clearest candidate for "a genuine contradiction," per this task's own instruction to surface one if found.
