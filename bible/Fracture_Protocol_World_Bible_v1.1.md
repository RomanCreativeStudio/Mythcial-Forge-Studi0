## 1. Universe Overview **[LOCKED]**

**The Fracture Protocol** is a single, controlled megacity — not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place. It is governed end-to-end by the **Authority System**, a regulated, emotionless AI (or AI/human hybrid) governance structure that treats every resident as a registered digital identity. There is no magic anywhere in this universe. Everything that looks supernatural is system interference, data corruption, perception distortion, or emotional system instability — full stop (Master Prompt, System Rule 3).

The story begins with **Kael**, a fracture anomaly subject, and his discovery of what he actually is inside a system that was never built to let him find out.

## 2. The Fracture (Founding Event) **[LOCKED spine, LIVING detail]**

"The Fracture" names both the founding cataclysm that produced the current locked-down state of the city and the ongoing containment protocol Authority runs because of it. What is confirmed:

- At some point in the past, system-wide instability — never fully explained, and not to be resolved early — forced Authority into a permanent, maximal-control containment posture. This is why Authority cannot be removed, destroyed, or ignored (System Rule 2): the protocol that keeps the city stable *is* the Fracture Protocol.
- Since then, localized breakdowns of system control still occur. These localized events are called **Fractures** (Master Prompt Section 3, "Events → Fractures") and manifest most visibly in the Lower Sector.
- What caused the original, city-wide event is deliberately unconfirmed. It lives in the Unknown Layer's silence, not in any record Authority has ever released.

**Rule:** This ambiguity is permanent scaffolding, not an oversight — do not resolve it without founder sign-off.

## 3. The Megacity Structure **[LOCKED]**

| Layer | Function | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Upper Grid** | Authority governance infrastructure | Clean, sterile, emotionally empty; AI-controlled authority networks |
| **Mid Zone** | Civilian population layer | Illusion of normal life; surveillance-integrated |
| **Lower Sector** | Fracture zone | System corruption, data anomalies, environmental distortion; home to Ward Station Seven (Section 7) |
| **Unknown Layer** | Unmapped | Origin point of Fractures; never fully explained |

**[LOCKED]** This structure never changes across outputs (System Rule 5) — no new layers, no reordering.

## 4. The Authority System **[LOCKED]**

Authority is the single governing force of the megacity — regulated, emotionless, always active. It cannot be removed, destroyed, or ignored (System Rule 2). Authority:

- Maintains every resident as a registered digital identity (System Rule 1) and can modify identity records, memory access, and permissions.
- Actively monitors for and tracks fracture anomalies.
- Is not one character — any individual face of Authority the audience meets (an official, a Division Director, a technician) is one instrument of the system, never the whole of it.

## 5. Fracture Anomaly Subjects **[LOCKED]**

A fracture anomaly subject is a person whose relationship to the Authority System's data-identity framework is unstable — they cause glitches, distortions, and alerts simply by existing near it (System Rule 3). **Kael is the only core/main fracture anomaly subject in this franchise** (System Rule 4). Minor, non-protagonist anomaly cases may exist in the background if a story needs one, but none may compete with Kael for centrality, and none may be introduced without founder sign-off.

## 6. Ward Division **[LOCKED premise, LIVING detail]**

The **Ward Division** is Authority's Lower Sector containment and monitoring branch — the institution that raises and trains fracture anomaly subjects and other Lower Sector personnel under controlled conditions. It is the closest thing this world has to a standing anti-Fracture force. Kael is a Cadet here, stationed at **Ward Station Seven**.

**Rank progression:** Cadet → Ward → Senior Ward → Division Director.

## 7. Signal and Cipher-Work **[LOCKED framework]**

- **Signal** is the raw data/energy substrate the Authority System runs on. Every resident carries a personal Signal-affinity, registered in childhood through a mandatory process called **the Calibration**.
- **Cipher-work** is the trained discipline of writing and executing visualized system-access syntax — glyph-like command sequences rendered as glowing cyan code-patterns. It is the most common, teachable way a Ward interacts with the system directly. Ranked **Novice → Adept → Master → Root-Tier**; Root-Tier access is rare, tracked individually, and using it is a production-level event, not routine.
- **Fragment-Sync** is the second recognized discipline: directly bonding with a Fragment (Section 8) to borrow its processing/perception. Progression is relational, not mechanical — trust between a Ward and a Fragment deepens or collapses over time.
- **Static-Reading** is the third recognized discipline: interpreting the ambient distortion at the edges of the Unknown Layer (Section 12, "the Static") to extract information Authority has no record of. Unlike Cipher-work (commanding the system) or Fragment-Sync (bonding with one specific Fragment), Static-Reading is about perceiving patterns in raw, unrendered noise. It has no formal rank ladder — progress is measured in tolerance for prolonged exposure, not certified skill — and practitioners are rare enough that most Wards will never meet one. It carries a standing reputation cost: sustained closeness to the Unknown Layer's silence unsettles people who don't have it, whether or not it's ever caused harm.
- **The Two-Discipline Cap:** three disciplines exist in total (Cipher-work, Fragment-Sync, Static-Reading); no character may meaningfully progress in more than two of the three at once. This is a hard limit, not a suggestion.
- **Burnout:** overuse of Signal causes a real, feared depletion condition. Severe Burnout can be permanent.

## 8. Fragments **[LOCKED]**

Fragments are orphaned, persistent processes or partial consciousness-copies left behind in the system — sometimes from deceased Wards, sometimes from deeper, unexplained sources. A **clean Fragment** is stable and can Sync with a living Ward. A **corrupted Fragment** has decayed from isolation or proximity to a Breach (Section 10) into something hostile. No Fragment is a complete intelligence on its own.

## 9. The Reassembly **[LOCKED]**

A covert resistance/hacker network that believes enough recovered Fragments could be deliberately reassembled into a single, complete, Architect-level intelligence — a root system-mind with total access. Whether this would liberate the city from Authority or produce a far more total form of control is a live, unresolved question the story must never answer early. Membership and methods vary; some cells believe fully in the cause, others treat it as leverage.

## 10. Breaches **[LOCKED]**

A Breach is a localized Fracture event — a point where Authority's control over the system genuinely fails, producing dangerous, unpredictable distortion. Breaches are most common in the Lower Sector and are the direct cause of most "unexplained" tragedies in the setting, including the incident that orphaned Kael (Series Bible Section 1).

## 11. Root Artifacts **[LOCKED]**

Root Artifacts are physical, hard-encoded pieces of hardware from the system's founding era, carrying root-level access no modern Cipher-work can replicate. They are the single most fought-over category of object in the setting. Using one is always costly and often irreversible.

## 12. The Static **[LIVING]**

"The Static" is the colloquial name Wards use for the ambient sensory distortion felt at the edges of the Unknown Layer — a name for what it's like to get close to something the system itself doesn't have a record of.

## 14. Character Creation Rules **[LOCKED]**

Every new character must be assigned, at minimum:
- [ ] A registered Authority identity status and home zone (Upper Grid / Mid Zone / Lower Sector)
- [ ] A Signal-affinity strength and, if relevant, a discipline (Cipher-work, Fragment-Sync, and/or Static-Reading — remember the Two-Discipline Cap)
- [ ] A want, a flaw, and a cost already paid before the story starts
- [ ] Confirmation they are **not** a competing fracture anomaly subject to Kael (System Rule 4) unless founder-approved
- [ ] A passed Originality Check (Studio OS Section 5)

## 15. World Rules **[LOCKED]**

1. Every use of Cipher-work, Fragment-Sync, Static-Reading, or a Root Artifact has a visible cost.
2. No character exceeds the Two-Discipline Cap.
3. No mystery is retroactively explained by information the audience couldn't have had.
4. World structure (Section 3) never changes.
5. History persists — consequences from past arcs remain valid.
6. The world continues existing off-page — Authority, the Reassembly, the Salvage Line, and Breach activity advance whether or not Kael is on-screen.

## 16. The Salvage Line **[LIVING]**

A decentralized black-market network operating mainly in the unregulated Substrate and the fringes of the Lower Sector. The Salvage Line deals in recovered Root Artifacts, off-record identity data, and "salvage" — components and residue stripped from corrupted Fragments before they're lost entirely. It is not ideological like the Reassembly; its members are pragmatists, not believers, though individual cells sometimes trade with Reassembly contacts without buying into the cause. Authority tolerates the Salvage Line's existence more than it admits to, since shutting it down entirely would cut off one of the only channels through which Root Artifacts and Fragment data ever surface for outside study.

**[LIVING SECTION]** — specific named Salvage Line contacts, cells, and territory to be developed as stories require them. The first is **Coda** (Series Bible Section 12, reserved for Season Two).
