## 2. Narrative Purpose

The season's baseline "ordinary world" — the Lower Sector is where Kael's routine patrol life is established in the pilot ("nothing about this reads as significant, that is the point"), and where the season's escalating crisis content is staged across Episodes 2, 3, 8, and the sustained Episodes 9–11 field-operation arc. Its narrative function shifts with its dressing (routine patrol → crisis-site → displaced-civilian aftermath → informal/off-duty → scaled field operation), but the underlying physical asset is the same layer-identity location throughout, per Episode 9's own explicit lineage citation to Episode 2's dressing.
