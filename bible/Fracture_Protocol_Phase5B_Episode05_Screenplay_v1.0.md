### SCENE 3 — WHAT HAPPENED

- **Scene Number:** 3
- **Scene Title:** What Happened
- **INT./EXT.:** Same
- **Location:** Same as Scenes 1–2
- **Time of Day:** Continuous
- **Story Purpose:** Mira tells Kael about her displacement.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Mira steady, telling her own story on her own terms; Kael listening fully.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, Mira.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Mira.
- **Required Audio:** No flashback dramatization; her telling carries the scene.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 2; no reenactment sequence anywhere in this scene.

**ACTION**

No flashback. No reenacted history. Mira tells it, present-tense, in her own words, and the camera stays with her telling — her face, her hands, her own pacing of it. Kael listens completely; he does not reach for her, does not interrupt, does not try to soften anything before she's finished.

**DIALOGUE**

MIRA
I grew up on the Moon. Mid Zone family, nothing remarkable. When the Doctrine moved into our district, they didn't come in with weapons first — they came in with an offer. Protection, if we stopped asking questions about who was in charge.
*(beat, steady)*
My family said no. A lot of families said no. That's who got displaced.

KAEL
*(quiet, not interrupting)*
...

MIRA
The Light 98th came. Eventually. Not in time for everything — but in time for enough of us that I got to become someone instead of just a name on a casualty count.
*(a small, real note of pride)*
I rebuilt houses before I rebuilt myself. Reconstruction work made more sense to me than anything else did, after. Still does.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Per Phase 2E's own requirement, this must never read as a passive account of victimhood — Mira's own agency, competence, and eventual reconstruction identity are as much the content of this account as the loss is. She is the one narrating her own history; nothing here positions her as a spectacle.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Sustained close coverage on Mira throughout her telling; Kael appears only in reaction shots that never pull focus from her.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Same quiet, focused treatment as Scene 2.

**SOUND NOTES**

Minimal — her voice carries the scene almost entirely alone.

**TRANSITION**

Cut as she continues, toward the specific fact she hasn't yet said.

### SCENE 4 — TITAN'S NAME

- **Scene Number:** 4
- **Scene Title:** Titan's Name
- **INT./EXT.:** Same
- **Location:** Same as Scenes 1–3
- **Time of Day:** Continuous
- **Story Purpose:** Land the episode's specific revelation — Titan's personal connection.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Mira matter-of-fact; Kael visibly recontextualizing.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, Mira.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Mira.
- **Required Audio:** Mira stays in control of this disclosure too.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 3; Titan does not appear on screen.

**ACTION**

Mira delivers the specific fact plainly — not as a twist, not as an accusation, a fact she settled with long ago.

**DIALOGUE**

MIRA
*(plain, at peace with it)*
Titan was part of the response that reached us. Not the reason it was late. Just — there, when it finally came. I've known that for years. It's not something I carry anger about. It's just true.

KAEL
*(quiet, visibly recalculating something)*
I didn't know that.

MIRA
*(gently, not performing forgiveness for him)*
It's not a story about him being at fault. It's just part of my story that happens to touch his.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Mira does not perform forgiveness or anger for Kael's benefit — the fact is simply stated. Kael's reaction should register as personal recontextualization of Titan's myth, restrained, not shock or blame directed at Mira.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Held on Kael's face as the fact lands — readable, restrained, not overplayed; this episode does not process it fully.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Unchanged from Scene 3.

**SOUND NOTES**

Silence around the disclosure; no musical underline.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to the conversation settling.
