## 1. Purpose

Phase 6K exists to govern the eventual production of concept art for Kael, Cassian, Mira, the Guardian, all confirmed Environments, FPP-TECH-001, and both confirmed Visual Effects assets. Concept art is the first actual visual output in this production's Production Authority Hierarchy (level 7) — everything Phase 6B through 6H built was governance, inventory, and reference-package material only. Phase 6K.0 establishes only the rules that will govern concept art work. **It authorizes no concept art itself, invents no design, and expands no canon.**

---

## 2. Concept Art Authority

Concept art may be produced only for an asset already confirmed in an Approved Production Package:
1. **Character Production Packages** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6B_Character_*_v1.0.md`, including 6B.1/6B.2/6B.3) — for Kael, Cassian, Mira, and the Guardian only. Titan is explicitly excluded (no physical asset required, per Phase 6B.0).
2. **Environment Production Packages** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6C_Environment_*_v1.0.md`, including the 6C.5–6C.9 reconciliation lineage) — for all 15 confirmed environments.
3. **The Prop & Technology Production Package** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6D2_Prop_Technology_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`) — for FPP-TECH-001 only, the sole confirmed asset.
4. **The Storyboard Production Packages** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6E2_Storyboard_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`) — governing camera/composition context where concept art directly informs a specific shot.
5. **The Visual Effects Production Packages** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6H2_VFX_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`) — for Kael's eye-glow and the Breach-adjacent disturbance lineage only.

No concept art may be produced for any subject not confirmed in one of these five source families. An asset with no citable source here does not exist for concept art purposes — it must be flagged as missing, never invented.

**Flagged ambiguity, not resolved here:** `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A5_Asset_Production_Standards_v1.0.md`'s own Asset Authority section states every production asset must originate "only from: Approved Screenplays, Approved Asset Registry, Approved Production Roadmap," explicitly listing "Production Packages" among sources assets must **never** originate from ("those documents explain intent... Approved Screenplays govern production"). This appears to sit in direct tension with both (a) this directive's own explicit "Use only" instruction, which names Character/Environment/Prop/Storyboard/VFX Production Packages as required sources for this governance document, and (b) the actual operating practice of every prior Phase 6 discipline (6E through 6J), each of which cited prior disciplines' Production Packages as primary authority without exception. This document follows this directive's own explicit instruction for its own scope (Concept Art Authority, above, cites Production Packages directly, as directed); the broader tension with Phase 6A.5's literal text is flagged here for Founder awareness and future reconciliation, not resolved or overridden globally.

---

## 3. Visual Design Constraint Authority

Where concept art is produced, its visual character is bounded by:
- Each character's own Locked visual canon (Phase 6B.1/6B.2: silhouette, proportions, facial structure, hair, skin, movement, posture) and Costume/Wardrobe canon (Phase 6B.3).
- Each environment's own Color Philosophy and Lighting Rules/Lighting Philosophy field, including any Permanent Lighting/Production Canon (e.g., Night Haven's Phase 6C.7 canon, Cassian's Private Interior's Phase 6C.6 canon).
- The Prop & Technology Production Package's own Production Requirements for FPP-TECH-001 — form factor and interface behavior remain unspecified and **may not be designed by concept art** absent a further Founder Directive resolving that gap.
- The Locked permanent three-color system (`color-language.md`, `global-consistency-rules.md`) and each Visual Effects Production Package's own Effect Authority — cyan/icy-blue remains reserved exclusively for Fracture-anomaly phenomena, never assigned decoratively to any concept art subject.
- `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A5_Asset_Production_Standards_v1.0.md`'s own Character/Environment/Prop Standards sections, which already define the deliverable structure concept art must eventually satisfy per asset type (Character: Turnaround, Expressions, Poses, Scale/Color/Material/Lighting/Animation Reference; Environment: Master View, Layout, Scale, Lighting, Material Reference, Reuse Notes, Module Breakdown; Prop: Orthographic Views, Scale, Material Notes, Episode Usage, Reuse Opportunities).

**No color palette, rendering style, or art-direction approach is created or selected by this document.** Per Phase 6A.5's own Color Management and Resolution Standards sections, a single consistent color-management profile, resolution standard, and file-format standard are already mandated to exist but are explicitly not yet Locked for Concept Art specifically — Phase 6A.5's own text states these must be "flagged as an open item wherever no Locked value yet exists, rather than invented here." This document reconfirms that flag rather than closing it: **no rendering style (flat-vector, painterly, photorealistic, or any other) is specified anywhere in this production's existing documentation for character, environment, prop, or VFX concept art**, and none is invented here.

## 4. Approval Authority

A piece of concept art becomes Approved only via the same discipline every other asset type in this production has followed:
- Technical Review, Alignment Audit, Founder Review, and Founder Approval, per `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A5_Asset_Production_Standards_v1.0.md`'s own Asset Review Rule.
- Progression through the full Asset Lifecycle already Locked at Phase 6A.5 (Requested → In Progress → Review → Revision → Founder Review → Approved → Locked → Archived) — no stage may be skipped.
- Registration in `documentation/Asset_Registry.md` immediately upon Approval, per the Registry's own standing rule that no asset is "real" until logged there.

No concept art may be treated as canon, referenced by any downstream discipline, or used to inform any other asset until it has passed this full Approval chain.

---

## 5. Continuity Rules

Every piece of Approved concept art must remain consistent with:
- Its own prior Approved iterations for the same subject — never independently redesigned at each new instance without an explicit Revision.
- Every governing source cited under Visual Design Constraint Authority (Section 3), without exception.
- The Guardian's own Locked Mystery Protection Rule (Phase 6B.1) — concept art may never depict, imply, or gesture toward any of the Guardian's nine protected narrative facts.
- Mira's Episode 33 mechanism protection (Phase 5A.2) — concept art touching that episode's content must not imply any specific unspecified mechanism.

Any apparent inconsistency between a proposed piece of concept art and its own governing sources must be flagged for Founder decision under the Production Conflict Resolution rule (Section 7, below), never silently resolved by concept-art-stage judgment.

---

## 6. Revision Rules

Any revision to an Approved piece of concept art must:
- Reopen the existing entry, never overwrite it silently — preserving all prior Founder Approval as historical record, per the reopen-and-append method already established throughout this production.
- State its Reason, Affected Episodes, and Affected Screenplays, and undergo Regression Review, per Phase 6A.5's own Asset Revision Rule.
- Cite the specific new source that justifies the revision (a corrected screenplay, an updated Character/Environment/Prop/VFX Production Package, or a new Founder Directive).
- Carry its own new Founder Review, Alignment Audit, and Founder Approval before superseding the prior version.
- Never delete a prior version's record — mark it Superseded, consistent with the standing "nothing disappears without a record" practice.

---

## Version Control

Every Concept Art production document, and every individual concept art asset once produced, follows the same versioning discipline standing across this entire production: `v1.0` on first compilation/production, incremented major versions on Founder-directed content changes, changelog entries (or Version Standard fields, per Phase 6A.5) preserved in full at every version, and git commit/push performed separately for content-compilation versus Founder-approval steps wherever both occur in the same session.

---

## 7. Production Conflict Resolution

If any Concept Art work finds:
- A contradiction between two Locked Canon sources,
- A contradiction between a proposed design and its own governing Character, Environment, Prop, Storyboard, or Visual Effects Production Package,
- A design element with no citable source anywhere in Concept Art, Visual Design Constraint, or Approval Authority (Sections 2–4), or
- Any indication of repository corruption or a missing Locked authority,

production on that specific piece of concept art stops, and the conflict is flagged for Founder decision — exactly as every prior Phase 6 Production Conflict Rule already requires in its own domain. No conflict of this kind may be silently resolved by concept-art-stage inference, convenience, or aesthetic preference. This includes, without exception, any apparent creative need for a visual detail (a specific color value, a rendering technique, a form factor, an architectural feature) the governing sources do not confirm — such a need is always flagged, never self-authorized.

## 8. Cross-Reference Authority

This framework sits entirely atop already-Locked authority and creates no independent visual specification of its own. Every rule above traces directly to one of: `Fracture_Protocol_Phase60_Production_Pipeline_Governance_v1.0.md`, `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A5_Asset_Production_Standards_v1.0.md`, the four Character Production Packages, the 15 Environment Production Packages, the Prop & Technology Production Package, the Storyboard Production Packages, or the Visual Effects Production Packages. Where this framework is silent, the governing source is one of these documents, never this document's own independent judgment.

**Flagged ambiguity, not resolved here:** Phase 6A.5's own Asset ID Standard (`CHR-0001`, `ENV-0001`, `PROP-0001`, `VFX-0001` format) has never matched the `FPP-CHAR-001`/`FPP-ENV-001`/`FPP-TECH-001`/`FPP-VFX-001` format actually used throughout the Asset Registry and every Phase 6B–6J Production Package — a discrepancy first flagged at Phase 6B.0 and never reconciled. This is directly relevant to Concept Art, since every concept art asset will eventually require its own permanent Asset ID under one of these two competing standards. This document does not resolve the discrepancy; any future Concept Art asset ID must await a Founder decision reconciling it, consistent with how it has been carried forward, unresolved, since Phase 6B.
