## 1. Purpose

Phase 6J exists to govern the eventual selection and direction of voice performers for Kael, Cassian, Mira, and the Guardian. Per Phase 6G.0 §4's own already-Locked language, casting is explicitly a "separate Founder-directed process," distinct from Voice Performance Authority itself. Phase 6J.0 establishes only the rules that will govern that future process. **It authorizes no casting decision, selects no performer, conducts no audition, and produces no recording.**

---

## 2. Casting Authority

A casting decision (the selection of a specific performer for a specific character) is authorized only by:
1. A dedicated, future Founder Directive naming the character and confirming the decision — this document does not itself select or approve any performer.
2. Direct conformance to Character Voice Requirement Authority (Section 3, below) and Audition Standards (Section 4, below), verified before any candidate may be considered.

No casting process may begin, and no candidate may be evaluated, until such a Founder Directive exists. This governance document alone does not open casting.

---

## 3. Character Voice Requirement Authority

A performer's voice must satisfy the requirements already Locked in each character's own Dialogue/Voice Group Production Package (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6G2_Audio_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`, FPP-AUD-001 through 004) and Character Production Package. These requirements are cited here by reference, not restated as new:

- **Kael (FPP-AUD-001):** economical, controlled, disciplined baseline register; must be capable of three confirmed distinct vocal states (Episode 2 strain, Episode 7 rank-advancement, Episode 12 Senior Ward), not one generic "stressed" reading; facial/eye-glow-synchronized delivery tracking Signal-Sensitivity state, including within single scenes.
- **Mira (FPP-AUD-002):** practical, purposeful, unhurried baseline; open-by-default, guarding specifically in trust-tension scenes; no eye-glow/Emotion Modifier synchronization dependency.
- **Cassian (FPP-AUD-003):** measured, deliberate, economical by default; must fray specifically and only at his five schedule beats (Episodes 28/29/30/31/35) as distinguishable, escalating vocal states, never a single reused "distress" reading; his Episode 35 closing line carries Locked-canon-level textual precision.
- **The Guardian (FPP-AUD-004):** calm, certain, unremarkable baseline register; the Episode 8 "slightly longer pause before speaking" is its sole permitted vocal-timing deviation; no vocal delivery may hint at any of the Guardian's nine protected narrative facts (Phase 6B.1 Mystery Protection Rule).

Any voice requirement not already established in one of these sources does not exist for casting purposes — it must be flagged as missing, never invented at the casting stage. This includes, without limitation, age range, accent, vocal timbre/pitch, or any other physical vocal-quality specification: **no source in this production specifies any of these for any character**, and none is invented here.

---

## 4. Audition Standards

Where a future casting process evaluates a candidate performer, the audition material itself is governed as follows:
1. Any audition reading must use exact-text dialogue drawn directly from an Approved screenplay's own DIALOGUE block, per Phase 6G.0's already-Locked Dialogue Authority (exact-text lock, no line cut, paraphrased, or reordered). No audition line may be improvised, invented, or adapted from outside the 36 Approved screenplays.
2. A candidate's delivery must be evaluated against that character's own Character Voice Requirement Authority (Section 3), not against a casting director's independent judgment of "fit" untethered from the Locked specification.
3. **No specific audition sides package (a curated selection of scenes/lines for audition use) currently exists for any character.** This is flagged as missing, not created here — assembling one is a future, separate production deliverable, not authorized by this governance document.
4. **No audition format, medium (self-tape vs. live), union/non-union classification, or logistical standard is specified anywhere in this production's existing documentation.** Flagged as missing; any such standard requires its own future Founder Directive, not invention here.

---

## 5. Performance Direction Authority

Once a performer is cast under a future, separate Founder Directive, their recorded performance is governed by:
- Phase 6G.0 §4 (Voice Performance Authority) in full, cited by reference — this document does not restate it.
- Each character's own Dialogue/Voice Group package (Section 3, above), which remains the binding specification for delivery, register, and required vocal-state variants.
- Each screenplay's own parenthetical performance directions, already established at Phase 6G.0 §3 as "binding direction for Voice Performance Authority... not optional color."

No director, performer, or production stage may deviate from this already-Locked specification without a new Founder Directive amending the relevant Voice Performance Authority itself, per Phase 6G.0's own Audio Revision Rule.

---

## 6. Cross-Reference Authority

This framework sits entirely atop already-Locked authority and creates no independent vocal or dialogue specification of its own. Every rule above traces directly to one of: `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6G0_Audio_Production_Governance_v1.0.md`, `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6G2_Audio_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`, the four Character Production Packages, `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6B1_Visual_Manifestation_Canon_v1.0.md` (the Guardian's Mystery Protection Rule), or `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A_Master_Production_Roadmap_v1.0.md`. Where this framework is silent, the governing source is one of these documents, never this document's own independent judgment.

## 7. Revision Rules

Any revision to an Approved casting framework element must:
- Reopen the existing entry, never overwrite it silently — preserving all prior Founder Approval as historical record, per the reopen-and-append method already established throughout this production.
- Cite the specific new source that justifies the revision (an updated Character or Audio Production Package, or a new Founder Directive).
- Carry its own new Founder Review, Alignment Audit, and Founder Approval before superseding the prior version.
- Never delete a prior version's record — mark it Superseded, consistent with the standing "nothing disappears without a record" practice.

---

## Version Control

Every Casting production document follows the same versioning discipline standing across this entire production: `v1.0` on first compilation, incremented major versions on Founder-directed content changes, changelog entries preserved in full at every version, and git commit/push performed separately for content-compilation versus Founder-approval steps wherever both occur in the same session.

---

## 8. Conflict Resolution Rules

If any casting-framework work finds:
- A contradiction between two Locked Canon sources,
- A contradiction between a candidate's proposed vocal characteristic and a character's own Locked Voice Performance requirement,
- A casting or performance-direction decision with no citable source anywhere in Casting, Character Voice Requirement, Audition, or Performance Direction Authority (Sections 2–5), or
- Any indication of repository corruption or a missing Locked authority,

production on that specific casting matter stops, and the conflict is flagged for Founder decision — exactly as every prior Phase 6 Production Conflict Rule already requires in its own domain. No conflict of this kind may be silently resolved by casting-stage inference, convenience, or "reasonable" invention of a missing vocal specification. This includes, without exception, any apparent casting need for a vocal quality (age, accent, timbre) the governing sources do not confirm — such a need is always flagged, never self-authorized.
