4. **Eye-glow:** confirm the eye rendering is unambiguously "icy cyan" with a visible glow/luminance quality distinguishing it from an ordinary eye-color render, consistent with `color-language.md`'s Calm-state specification.
5. **Skin/weathering and hair:** flagged at medium/lower confidence — not mandatory corrections on their own, but worth deliberate attention on a re-generation pass: avoid a fully pristine, "hero" finish (Phase 6B.2 item 20's own Do Not Do), and keep hair styling toward "practical," not elaborately stylized.
