### B. Assembly Groups (16 — mapped 1:1 to the existing Storyboard Sequence Groups)

Per Phase 6I.0 §3 (Assembly Authority), assembly draws its reusable shot/scene material directly from the 16 Approved Storyboard Production Packages. No new Assembly Group or Asset ID is created here — each Editing Group above assembles from one or more of **FPP-SB-001 through FPP-SB-016** (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6E2_Storyboard_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`), per each episode's own Location List. Episodes spanning more than one environment (e.g., Episode 20: FPP-SB-003 + FPP-SB-004; Episode 23: FPP-SB-011 + institutional groups) draw from multiple Assembly Groups within a single Editing Group — this is expected and already governed by Assembly Authority, not a new finding.

### C. Transition Groups (1 confirmed — universal)

Direct re-search of all 36 Editing Direction sections (not merely the two instances previously cited at Phase 6H.1/Phase 6I.0) confirms and strengthens the existing finding: **zero** rendered dissolve, wipe, cross-dissolve, or fade transition exists anywhere in the 36 screenplays. Four additional explicit hard-cut instances are newly logged here beyond the two already on record (Episodes 7, 8): Episode 2 ("a hard tonal cut at Scene 5→6"), Episode 11 ("a hard cut to Mira in Scene 3"). No dissolve/wipe/fade is used anywhere; every fade-adjacent mention in the corpus explicitly *rejects* it ("not softened by a slow pull-out or fade," "no fade or gradual build"). There is exactly one Transition Group in this production: the **hard-cut tonal-break device**, already logged as **FPP-AUD-014**. No new Asset ID is created; this finding reconfirms, not duplicates, the existing entry, per Phase 6I.0 §5 (Transition Authority).

### D. Cross-Cut Groups — Confirmed Baseline + Flagged Discrepancy

**Confirmed baseline (matches existing FPP-SB-007):** Episodes 22, 24, 27, 34, 35, 36 — 6 episodes, 11 scenes, per Phase 6E.1's Cross-Cut/Convergence Sequences group. Reconfirmed directly: each of these 6 episodes' own Editing Direction explicitly uses "cross-cut," "cross-cutting," or "converge" language.

**Flagged discrepancy (Production Conflict, not silently resolved — Phase 6I.0 §9):** Direct search of all 36 Editing Direction sections finds **3 additional episodes** whose own text confirms cross-cutting/intercutting between multiple environments, none of which are captured in FPP-SB-007's scene list:
- **Episode 10, Scene 5** — confirmed intercut between the "operation site" (ENV-011) and "Ward Station Seven — communications context" (FPP-ENV-001), per both the Editing Direction ("Scene 5's intercut should end on the operation site, not the command context") and the episode's own Location List.
- **Episode 20, all 5 scenes** — confirmed "alternating structure between the two threads throughout," spanning the Reconstruction-Work Environment (FPP-ENV-004/FPP-SB-003) and the Guardian/Kael Partnership Environment (FPP-ENV-003/FPP-SB-004), per both the Editing Direction and the episode's own Location List (Scenes 1,3,5a vs. 2,4,5b).
- **Episode 23, Scenes 1–2** — confirmed "escalating cross-cutting," spanning the Populated Civilian Area (FPP-ENV-010) and "institutional spaces (reused from Episodes 13–22)," per both the Editing Direction and the episode's own Location List.

This discrepancy traces to Phase 6E.1's own disclosed grouping convention ("first-listed-location-as-primary for either/or scenes; separate Cross-Cut bucket for multi-environment scenes") — these three episodes' multi-environment scenes were evidently bucketed into their primary single-environment Storyboard groups (FPP-SB-002, FPP-SB-003/004, FPP-SB-011) rather than into FPP-SB-007, even though their own screenplay Editing Direction independently and explicitly confirms cross-cutting/intercutting. **No resolution is applied here** — Phase 6I.1 is inventory only and has no authority to revise an Approved Storyboard Production Package. This is flagged for Founder decision: whether Phase 6E.1/6E.2 should be reopened to fold these three episodes into FPP-SB-007 (or a new distinct "two-environment alternation" sub-type), or whether they are correctly treated as a distinct, narrower editing-stage technique that does not require Storyboard-level reclassification.

**Resolved 2026-07-18 per "Founder Directive — Phase 6I.1.5 Storyboard / Editing Cross-Cut Reconciliation (v1.0)":** re-verification confirmed FPP-SB-007 is correctly scoped and requires no expansion — none of the three episodes match its own self-cited "institutional convergence" definition, and the three are not even a shared pattern with each other. Each episode's own already-governing package (FPP-SB-002 for Episode 10; FPP-SB-003/FPP-SB-004 for Episode 20; FPP-SB-011 for Episode 23) received a narrow Transition Rules cross-reference instead. Full determination in `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Phase6I1_5_Storyboard_Editing_CrossCut_Reconciliation_v1.0.md`.

### E. Color Grade Groups (15 confirmed — environment-mapped)

Per Phase 6I.0 §6 (Color Consistency Rule), color grading is governed, not created, at the editing stage. One Color Grade Group exists per Environment Production Package's own Color Philosophy/Lighting Rules field — mapped 1:1 to the same 15 environments already established (FPP-ENV-001 through 010, ENV-011 through 015). No new Asset ID is created. Two cross-cutting constants apply to every group without exception, per the Locked permanent three-color system: the cyan/icy-blue reservation (never to be extended by grading onto any other asset) and the two confirmed VFX assets' own color dependency (**FPP-VFX-001** Kael's eye-glow, **FPP-VFX-002** the Breach-adjacent lineage), both already logged at Phase 6H.1/6H.2. Two environments carry a confirmed **Permanent Lighting/Production Canon** that directly bounds color grading across cuts: Night Haven (Phase 6C.7, cooler institutional vs. warmer resistance register by beat) and Cassian's Private Interior (Phase 6C.6, no invented architecture or palette beyond his Locked personal palette).

### G. Reusable Editing Patterns (4 confirmed)

1. **The held-beat / no-early-cutaway restraint pattern** — confirmed across at least 11 episodes (1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13, 16, 18, 21), each explicitly instructing a scene to be held rather than cut away from early. Parallels the series-wide music restraint baseline (FPP-AUD-006) as a cross-disciplinary "restraint over spectacle" production philosophy.
2. **The hard-cut tonal-break device, deployed at structural pivot points** — not merely the default absence of a transition (Section C above), but a specifically confirmed punctuation device at major tonal turns (Episode 2 Scene 5→6, Episode 7 Scene 5, Episode 8 Scene 4→5, Episode 11 Scene 3).
3. **The sequential-threads-into-convergence template** — confirmed self-cited across the 6 FPP-SB-007 episodes (22, 24, 27, 34, 35, 36): establish threads separately, converge without merging or resolving. A reusable structural device, not independently designed per episode.
4. **The camera/music self-cited escalation-echo lineage** — Episode 9's Editing Direction explicitly parallels Episode 2 ("mirroring but exceeding Episode 2's own escalation"), matching both FPP-SB-002's own self-cited camera-echo template (Episodes 1→2→9–11) and FPP-AUD-007's own self-cited music echo ("Episode 9 explicitly echoes Episode 2's musical shape without repeating it") — a single reusable escalation pattern confirmed simultaneously across camera, music, and editing disciplines.
