## 1. Purpose

Final Edit is the actual, produced output of the assembly discipline Phase 6I already fully governs — the difference between Phase 6I's own governance/inventory work (which authorized no edit) and an actual finished, Approved edited episode. Phase 6L exists to govern that execution and its approval, not to re-govern assembly itself. Phase 6L.0 establishes only the rules that will govern an actual Final Edit once produced. **It authorizes no editing, creates no final scene, and produces no video.**

**A structural prerequisite is recorded here rather than worked around:** per the Production Authority Hierarchy's own level order, Final Edit (level 12) sits above Character Assets, Environment Assets, Animation Assets, and Audio Assets (levels 8–11) — the actual produced art, motion, and audio a real edit assembles. Per the Phase 6 Lock Certification's own confirmed finding, **no actual production asset of any kind exists yet anywhere in this production.** This governance document may be compiled now, exactly as Phase 6I.0 was compiled before any Storyboard/Animation/Audio package existed — but no actual Final Edit work may begin until the assets it would assemble are produced, a dependency this document does not resolve or work around.

---

## 2. Final Edit Authority

A Final Edit may assemble only:
1. Material already Approved under Phase 6I.0's Editing Authority, Assembly Authority, and Transition Authority (§§2, 3, 5), cited by reference — this document restates none of it.
2. Material corresponding to one of the 36 Editing Groups already identified in Phase 6I.1's Master Editing Inventory (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6I1_Editing_Inventory_v1.0.md`, FPP-EDIT-001 through 036), including the Cross-Cut Groups reconciliation already closed at Phase 6I.1.5.
3. **Actual produced Character, Environment, Animation, and Audio Assets** (Production Authority Hierarchy levels 8–11) — not their governing Production Packages, which are reference documents, not assemblable footage or audio. No such asset currently exists (Section 1, above).

A Final Edit assembling any material outside these three categories, or assembling a placeholder/reference package as if it were produced content, is prohibited.

---

## 3. Editorial Approval Authority

A Final Edit becomes Approved only via the same discipline every other asset type in this production has followed:
- Technical Review, Alignment Audit, Founder Review, and Founder Approval, per `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6A5_Asset_Production_Standards_v1.0.md`'s own Asset Review Rule.
- Progression through the full Asset Lifecycle already Locked at Phase 6A.5 (Requested → In Progress → Review → Revision → Founder Review → Approved → Locked → Archived) — no stage may be skipped.
- Registration in `documentation/Asset_Registry.md` immediately upon Approval, per the Registry's own standing rule that no asset is "real" until logged there.

No Final Edit may be treated as canon or advanced toward Master Release until it has passed this full Approval chain.

---

## 4. Continuity Control

A Final Edit must preserve, without exception, every continuity rule already Locked upstream — cited by reference, not restated:
- Phase 6I.0's own Continuity Authority (§4) in full.
- All 7 Continuity Groups identified at Phase 6I.1 (Kael's eye-glow rendering; Cassian's five-stage unraveling schedule; the Breach-adjacent disturbance lineage; the series-wide music restraint baseline; Night Haven's Permanent Lighting Canon; Cassian's Private Interior Production Rule; the Dialogue exact-text lock).
- Every Storyboard, Animation, Audio, and Visual Effects Production Package's own Continuity field for any asset the edit assembles.

A Final Edit that would violate any of these already-Locked rules is prohibited, regardless of any perceived editorial benefit — exactly as Phase 6I.0 §4 already requires of assembly generally.

---

## 5. Revision Rules

Any revision to an Approved Final Edit must:
- Reopen the existing edit entry, never overwrite it silently — preserving all prior Founder Approval as historical record, per the reopen-and-append method already established throughout this production and restated at Phase 6I.0 §8 (Editing Revision Rule).
- State its Reason, Affected Episodes, and Affected Screenplays, and undergo Regression Review, per Phase 6A.5's own Asset Revision Rule.
- Cite the specific new source that justifies the revision (a corrected screenplay, an updated Production Package, or a new Founder Directive).
- Carry its own new Founder Review, Alignment Audit, and Founder Approval before superseding the prior version.
- Never delete a prior version's record — mark it Superseded.

---

## Version Control

Every Final Edit production document, and every individual Final Edit asset once produced, follows the same versioning discipline standing across this entire production: `v1.0` on first compilation/production, incremented major versions on Founder-directed content changes, changelog entries (or Version Standard fields, per Phase 6A.5) preserved in full at every version, and git commit/push performed separately for content-compilation versus Founder-approval steps wherever both occur in the same session.

---

## 6. Conflict Resolution

If any Final Edit work finds:
- A contradiction between two Locked Canon sources,
- A contradiction between a proposed edit and its own governing Storyboard, Animation, Audio, or Visual Effects Production Package, or Phase 6I.0/6I.1/6I.1.5,
- An edit, timing, or continuity choice with no citable source anywhere in Final Edit, Editorial Approval, or Continuity Control Authority (Sections 2–4), or
- Any indication of repository corruption or a missing Locked authority,

production on that specific Final Edit stops, and the conflict is flagged for Founder decision — exactly as Phase 6I.0 §9's own Production Conflict Rule already requires. No conflict of this kind may be silently resolved by editorial-stage inference, convenience, or pacing preference.

---

This framework sits entirely atop already-Locked authority and creates no independent editorial standard of its own.
