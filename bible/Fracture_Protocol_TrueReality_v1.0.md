# THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL
## True Reality Codex — "Layer Zero"
### Version 1.0

**Classification:** Internal — Meta-Canon (Confidential Within Universe)
**Companion documents:** `bible/Mythic_Bible_v3.0.md` (Layer One — the perceived/simulated reality this document sits underneath), `series/01-kael-the-shattered-realms/Series_Bible_v2.0.md` (governs when and how this surfaces in-story)

### What this document is

*The Shattered Realms* — the Seven Great Realms, the Aurothi, Essence, Sigils, Echoes, Relics, Kael's life as a Duskmarch cadet — all of it is real *to the people living inside it*. This document reveals what it actually is underneath: a controlled, simulated reality generated and maintained by a true, physical system called **the Fracture Protocol**, running inside a dystopian megacity in a reality outside the one the audience has spent Season One believing in.

**This is not a retcon that deletes anything.** Every section of `bible/Mythic_Bible_v3.0.md` remains true *as experienced*. This document is the layer underneath — what a character would find if they could ever step outside the simulation entirely. See `global-consistency-rules.md` (Art Bible) and Series Bible Section "Reveal Discipline" for the hard rule: **nothing in this document may be revealed to the audience or to Kael before it is earned through story**, per the Mythic Bible's own principle that every mystery eventually has an explanation, but "eventually" is never scheduled early (Bible Section 60).

## 1. Layer Zero Overview **[LOCKED]**

The true reality is a controlled futuristic megacity — sterile, layered, governed by a regulated, emotionless AI system called **Authority**. People in this reality are registered digital identities. Kael, in this true reality, is a teenage anomaly — engineered or hybrid in origin, cause unconfirmed even to himself — whose existence destabilizes the systems around him. Everything Season One depicts (Duskhollow, the Wardenate, Essence, Echoes) is a simulated, internally consistent reality generated by the Fracture Protocol system, and Kael's entire remembered life inside it is a persona the system either built for him or that grew around his anomaly, depending on which in-world faction you ask (this is deliberately unresolved — see Section 9).

## 2. The Megacity Structure **[LOCKED]**

| Level | Function | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 — Upper Grid** | Government / Authority control centers | Clean, minimal, sterile, emotionally empty |
| **Level 2 — Mid Zone** | Civilian society | Controlled illusion of normal life; regulated neon |
| **Level 3 — Lower Sector** | Restricted zones | System anomalies, broken architecture, truth-leakage areas |
| **Unknown Outside System** | Unmapped | Exists only in rumor and corrupted data; possible origin of Kael's truth |

## 3. Authority **[LOCKED]**

Authority is the system control force governing the true megacity — AI or hybrid AI/human governance, regulated, emotionless. It can modify registered identities' memory and access. It actively tracks anomalies. Authority is not a single character; it is an institution, and any individual "face" of Authority the audience meets is one instrument of it, not the whole of it.

## 4. System Rules **[LOCKED]**

1. **Identity is data.** People are registered digital identities; Authority can modify memory and access.
2. **Controlled AI system.** AI exists but is tightly regulated; governance is deliberately emotionless.
3. **Anomalies.** Kael is an anomaly. He causes system glitches, distortions, and alerts, and Authority actively tracks anomalies like him.
4. **Kael's power is not magic.** It is system interference, emotional overload, and identity instability affecting reality systems — full stop. Every "magical" thing Kael does inside the simulation (Layer One) is this, wearing a fantasy skin.

## 5. Character Archetype System **[LOCKED]**

These are recurring narrative *roles*, not fixed one-to-one character assignments — any given character may occupy one across their arc:

- **KAEL** — protagonist / anomaly / identity seeker.
- **AUTHORITY** — the system control force, in whatever local guise it takes.
- **GUIDE** — a mysterious partial-truth giver with inconsistent presence; never fully trustworthy, never fully false.
- **MIRROR** — a future version or alternate outcome of Kael himself.
- **CIVILIANS** — the controlled society population, largely unaware.

| Layer One (perceived, Mythic Bible v3.0) | Layer Zero (true reality) |

| Relics | Physical, hard-encoded root-access artifacts from the system's founding era |

| The Duskmarch Wardenate | A containment-monitoring institution whose true function — unknown to its own members — is watching for leakage between simulation-shards |

**Rule:** this table is the only place these equivalences are stated outright. No Layer One document should ever state them this plainly — that would collapse the mystery structure the entire franchise depends on.

## 7. Kael's True Nature **[LOCKED]**

Kael is an anomaly in the true megacity — engineered or hybrid, origin unconfirmed even within this document. His entire remembered life as a Duskmarch cadet (Series Bible v2.0) is a persona generated by, or grown within, the Fracture Protocol simulation. Whether the system built that life *for* him deliberately (to study or stabilize him) or whether it emerged as a side effect of his anomaly interacting with the simulation is an open question reserved for a later season (Bible Section 60) — do not resolve it prematurely, and do not let any single character state it as settled fact.

**Visual constant across both layers:** Kael's eyes glow cyan/icy-blue, with intensity tied to his emotional and system state (Art Bible `color-language.md`) — calm (soft, stable glow), conflict (flickering, unstable), activation (intense, sharp), breakdown (fragmented, unstable). This is true whether he is perceived inside the simulation or seen in the true megacity — it is the one constant that never changes registers, because it is not a costume, it is what he actually is.

## 8. Reveal Discipline **[LOCKED]**

- Nothing in this document is known to Kael, to any Series 01 supporting character, or to the audience during Season One. It is production-team meta-canon only.
- Any in-story hint must route through already-established Layer One mystery threads (Bible Section 60, Series Bible Section 15) — the audience should be able to *retroactively* recognize the clue once revealed, never feel like new information was invented on the spot.
- The first permitted crack in the simulation is the Season One finale's cross-Realm reaction (Series Bible Episode 19–20) — this is Layer Zero's system logging a real anomaly event, not a coincidence, and Season Two should build on it directly.

## 9. Open Questions This Raises **[LIVING SECTION — deliberately unresolved]**

- Did Authority build Kael's simulated life deliberately, or did it emerge from his anomaly?
- Is "Authority" itself an Architect-descended intelligence, or something that arose after the Fracture Protocol event independently of the original seven?
- Is the Reassembly (Layer One) aware they are, in truth, attempting a system-level reboot — or do they also believe their own fantasy-framed theory?
- Does Kael have a physical body in the true megacity separate from his simulated persona, and if so, what happens to one if the other is harmed?
- What is the "Unknown Outside System" in relation to the Aetherséa — the same unmapped void, or something even further out?
