### Adjacent Categories Also Checked (not directive-listed, but reasonably implied)

Gate, lift/elevator, window, and in-world/diegetic camera were also searched. Every hit found is a false positive from unrelated language: "Stage 6 gate" (a production-process term, Episode 1), "approved window" (a time-range metaphor, Episodes 35–36), and every "camera" reference is shot-direction language ("the camera stays with her," "the camera does not linger") — the production camera, never an in-story security or recording device.

### The Real Question: Was Anything Excluded *Because* It Was Generic?

This is the directive's actual concern, and it is answered directly: **no.** Phase 6D.1 did not encounter any generic terminal/console/workstation/furniture/security/environmental-technology reference and set it aside for lacking a unique name. It could not have — this second, independently-targeted search confirms none of these categories appear in the screenplay text at all, named or unnamed, specific or generic. The screenplays are simply silent on physical set-dressing objects of every kind, with the sole exception already logged (FPP-TECH-001, "the comm").

### What Phase 6D.1 Did Correctly Fold Into Environment Packages (Not Excluded, Correctly Placed)

These are **not excluded prop candidates** — they were never eligible for the Prop & Technology Inventory in the first place, and Phase 6D.1 was correct not to extract them. Each is a functional atmosphere note describing an environment's general technological *character* (e.g., "this room implies research activity happens here"), not a discrete, individually-interacted-with object the way FPP-TECH-001 (the comm) is ("Kael reads it" — a specific action on a specific, singular item). None names a form factor, a specific device type, or a repeatable, reusable design brief. Manufacturing a "generic terminal" or "generic console" design for any of these would be pure invention — exactly what Phase 6D.0's Asset Creation Rule prohibits ("visual specificity beyond the citation is an invention and is prohibited").
