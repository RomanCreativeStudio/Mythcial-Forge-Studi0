## 1. Review

**FPP-TECH-001 ("the comm"):** the sole confirmed Prop & Technology asset in the entire 36-episode production (Phase 6D.1, validated at Phase 6D.1.5 — no other candidate exists or was excluded improperly). Appears once, Episode 7, Scene 5: *"A comm chimes — brief, official. Kael reads it. The ease drops from his face instantly."* No further prop or technology asset requires review; the discipline's inventory is closed at exactly one item.

**Existing dependencies, verified directly:**
- **Audio:** `FPP-AUD-012` ("The Comm Chime," Sound Effect) already Approved and cross-cited from Kael's own Dialogue/Voice package (`FPP-AUD-001`) — "his reaction to FPP-AUD-012 (the comm chime, Episode 7) must be vocally timed to it."
- **Storyboard:** **zero citations.** Direct search of `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6E2_Storyboard_Production_Packages_v1.0.md` finds no reference to FPP-TECH-001 or "the comm" anywhere, in any of the 16 packages.
- **Animation:** **zero citations.** Direct search of `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6F2_Animation_Production_Packages_v1.0.md` finds no reference anywhere.
- **Environment:** the governing environment is a **confirmed, disclosed either/or**, not a genuine gap. Episode 7's own Location List states "Ward Station Seven (1–4, and 5, if selected)" / "Mid Zone (alternate for Scene 5)"; Scene 5's own Location field reads "Ward Station Seven or Mid Zone, informal space... Per production selection"; its own Directorial Interpretation states explicitly: *"Scene 5's specific informal location is left to production."* This is the same class of deliberate production-flexibility language already accepted elsewhere in this corpus without treating it as a blocker (e.g., the Populated Civilian Area's "Lower Sector or Mid Zone," Phase 6C.5) — it is intentional, not missing information.
- **Form factor / interface behavior:** genuinely unspecified. Episode 7's own Scene 5 gives no visual description of the comm beyond the word itself and the action "Kael reads it." Its own Asset Requirements table for Scene 5 lists only "Kael; Mira; the chosen environment" — **the comm itself is not listed as a required asset even in its own scene**, consistent with Phase 6D.1's original finding that it was never itemized anywhere.

## 2. Determination

**Existing documentation is sufficient and Phase 6D.2 production package planning may begin**, with two items carried forward as disclosed constraints rather than blockers:

1. **The environment either/or is not a blocker.** It is confirmed intentional production flexibility, per the screenplay's own explicit "left to production" language — the eventual package must disclose both candidate environments (`FPP-ENV-001`/`FPP-SB-001` Ward Station Seven; `ENV-012`/`FPP-SB-008` Mid Zone) rather than assume one, exactly as the Populated Civilian Area precedent already established.
2. **The comm's form factor and interface behavior are not a blocker to *planning*, but must remain flagged, not invented, in any package content.** Per Phase 6D.0's own Asset Creation Rule ("visual specificity beyond the citation is an invention and is prohibited"), a Production Package may define governance/authority/dependency fields around the comm without ever specifying its appearance.

**No Founder decision is required to proceed to planning.** Neither item rises to the level of a canon contradiction or missing Locked authority requiring escalation under the Production Conflict Rule — both are already-disclosed, already-precedented conditions.
