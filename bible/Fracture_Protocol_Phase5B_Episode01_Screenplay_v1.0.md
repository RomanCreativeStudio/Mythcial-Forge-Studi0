## 9. Full Screenplay

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: DUTY SHIFT

- **Scene Number:** 1
- **Scene Title:** Cold Open: Duty Shift
- **INT./EXT.:** EXT.
- **Location:** Lower Sector
- **Time of Day:** Morning
- **Story Purpose:** Establish Kael's competence and ordinary routine before any names or context are given (seed package, Scene 1, Purpose).
- **Emotional Purpose:** Focused, unremarkable calm.
- **Characters Present:** Kael.
- **Required Assets:** Kael (character); Lower Sector (environment) — both flagged Asset Creation Required, Section 5.
- **Required Audio:** Ambient Lower Sector soundscape; no dialogue required.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** None — this is the season's opening scene; nothing precedes it.

**ACTION**

The Lower Sector, early light. Ward Division patrol routes, quiet at this hour. KAEL moves through it with the unhurried economy of someone who has done this exact walk a hundred times — checking a junction, acknowledging a passing colleague with a nod rather than a word, correcting a piece of misaligned signage without breaking stride.

Nothing about this reads as significant. That is the point.

**DIALOGUE**

*(None. The scene is carried by behavior.)*

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Kael's competence should be legible in small, specific choices — not a display of skill, a display of habit. He is not performing for anyone, including the camera.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Wide establishing frame of the Lower Sector, then a sustained mid-distance follow on Kael — no close-up yet. Save intimacy for Scene 3.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Per Art Bible `lighting-system.md`, Lower Sector zone identity — flat, functional morning light, no dramatic contrast.

**SOUND NOTES**

Ambient soundscape only; no score cue yet, per Section 11.

**TRANSITION**

Cut on Kael passing through Ward Station Seven's entrance.

### SCENE 2 — WARD STATION SEVEN: DUAL STATUS

- **Scene Number:** 2
- **Scene Title:** Ward Station Seven: Dual Status
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Ward Station Seven
- **Time of Day:** Morning
- **Story Purpose:** Make Kael's simultaneous Cadet/Light 98th-affiliated status visible through ordinary interaction, not exposition (seed package, Scene 2, Purpose).
- **Emotional Purpose:** Familiar, at ease among people who know him.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; unnamed Light 98th and Ward Division colleagues.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Ward Station Seven (interior) — both flagged Asset Creation Required.
- **Required Audio:** Light dialogue, intent only, establishing routine not plot.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 1; same morning.

**ACTION**

Ward Station Seven's interior — a working space, not a ceremonial one. A WARD COLLEAGUE hands Kael a duty log without looking up. A LIGHT 98TH COLLEAGUE, passing the other way, redirects mid-stride to fall into step with him for a few paces.

**DIALOGUE**

WARD COLLEAGUE
*(not looking up)*
Seven's clear. Nine's asking for someone who actually reads the whole report this time.

KAEL
*(taking the log)*
Tell Nine I'm insulted, and also correct.

LIGHT 98TH COLLEAGUE
*(falling into step, brief)*
You're on the Seven rotation and the 98th's list this week. Somebody upstairs still hasn't fixed the schedule.

KAEL
*(easy, unbothered)*
Somebody upstairs likes it this way. Keeps me humble.

The Light 98th colleague peels off with a short laugh. Kael keeps walking, already reading the log.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Nothing here should read as a joke performed for an audience — this is the specific, dry rhythm of people who work together daily. Kael's dual status should land as an established, slightly amusing fact of his life, not a plot point being explained.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Handheld-adjacent, moving with Kael through the space — the environment should feel populated and functioning around him, not staged for this exchange.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Per Art Bible `lighting-system.md`, Ward Station Seven interior — warmer, more lived-in than the Lower Sector exterior.

**SOUND NOTES**

Ambient station activity under the dialogue; no score.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to Kael stepping out of the busier corridor into a quieter space.

### SCENE 3 — KAEL AND MIRA

- **Scene Number:** 3
- **Scene Title:** Kael and Mira
- **INT./EXT.:** INT./EXT. (adjacent civilian space)
- **Location:** Ward Station Seven or adjacent Lower Sector civilian space
- **Time of Day:** Midday
- **Story Purpose:** Show the earned-trust relationship already in progress (seed package, Scene 3, Purpose).
- **Emotional Purpose:** Warm, unguarded, comfortable silence as much as conversation.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, Mira.
- **Required Assets:** Kael, Mira (both flagged Asset Creation Required).
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue establishing the relationship's current stage — trust before friendship before love, not yet spoken aloud (Phase 2C, Kael §4; Mira §4).
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** No prior scene between these two characters exists to reference; this is their first appearance together, established as already in progress, not as a first meeting.

**ACTION**

A quieter corner — not private, just unbusy. MIRA is already there, mid-task (something practical, hands occupied, not posed). Kael arrives without announcement; neither of them treats it as an event.

He sits. She doesn't stop what she's doing. A beat of comfortable quiet passes before either speaks.

**DIALOGUE**

MIRA
*(not looking up)*
You're late for someone who was already here an hour ago in a different uniform.

KAEL
Nine wanted the report read twice. I told her once was generous.

MIRA
*(a small smile, still working)*
She's not wrong to ask twice. You skim.

KAEL
I read carefully. I just don't read *slowly.*

MIRA
*(finally looking up)*
There's a difference?

KAEL
There's a difference.

A pause. Comfortable, not weighted.

MIRA
*(quieter, genuine)*
Long shift?

KAEL
Same as always. Nothing happened. Which is the good kind of shift.

MIRA
Don't let anyone hear you say that. Sounds like tempting something.

KAEL
*(dry)*
Noted.

They sit together a moment longer, not filling the silence for its own sake.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

This is the episode's only scene with real dialogue weight. Nothing either character says should explain how they know each other or what they mean to each other — the ease itself is the information. Mira should read as an equal presence in this space, not someone waiting to be attended to.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Closer, more intimate framing than Scenes 1–2 — the first close-up coverage of the episode belongs here.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Per Art Bible `lighting-system.md` — warmest register of the episode.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet ambient bed; dialogue carries the scene without competing sound.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to Kael back among colleagues, later in the day.

### SCENE 4 — BASELINE BELIEF

- **Scene Number:** 4
- **Scene Title:** Baseline Belief
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Ward Station Seven
- **Time of Day:** Afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Establish, through action rather than statement, what Kael already believes — power as responsibility, not weapon (Phase 2A.2, Section 1).
- **Emotional Purpose:** Quiet conviction.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; unnamed colleagues or civilians.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Ward Station Seven (interior).
- **Required Audio:** Minimal dialogue; the beat should read visually wherever possible.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Same day, later than Scene 3.

**ACTION**

A small, unglamorous moment: a civilian or junior colleague hesitates over a decision within Kael's authority to simply override. He doesn't override it. He waits, lets them work it out, offers one quiet, specific piece of guidance rather than taking the choice from them.

Per Phase 2E's Emotional Continuity requirement, one brief, private beat here — Kael alone for a few seconds afterward, a flicker of something carried rather than shown — a note that this ease has a cost he doesn't examine here, not a crack, not commented on by any other character.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
*(to the junior colleague, calm)*
You already know what you'd tell someone else to do here.

JUNIOR COLLEAGUE (V.O., off-frame or brief on-frame)
That's different.

KAEL
It isn't. It just feels different when it's yours to decide.

*(The colleague makes the call themselves. Kael doesn't take credit for it.)*

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Kael's guidance should never read as a lesson being delivered to the audience — it's a small, specific interaction, not a thesis statement. The private beat afterward should be extremely brief and unremarked upon; no other character reacts to it or notices it.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Held, quiet framing; the private beat isolates Kael briefly in frame after the colleague exits.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Per Art Bible `lighting-system.md`, Ward Station Seven interior — same register as Scene 2, slightly cooler by end of scene to support the private beat without over-signaling it.

**SOUND NOTES**

Minimal; no score under the private beat — let it sit in near-silence.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to Kael back outside, Lower Sector, as the day shifts toward its close.

### SCENE 5 — THE FIRST SIGN

- **Scene Number:** 5
- **Scene Title:** The First Sign
- **INT./EXT.:** EXT.
- **Location:** Lower Sector
- **Time of Day:** Late afternoon/early evening
- **Story Purpose:** Deliver the episode's cliffhanger (seed package, Scene 5, Purpose).
- **Emotional Purpose:** Calm breaking into alertness — the episode's only shift in tone.
- **Characters Present:** Kael (Light 98th/Ward Division personnel may register the disturbance around him; no new named character).
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Lower Sector (environment); the Scene 5 disturbance effect (flagged Asset Creation Required).
- **Required Audio:** A single sound-design cue marking the shift; no music sting that overplays the moment.
- **Required Visual Effects:** One subtle disturbance effect, governed by Art Bible `system-interference-visual-rules.md` — not redefined here.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Closes directly into Episode 2's own Beginning State, per the Phase 4B package's own Cliffhanger field ("directly into Episode 2").

**ACTION**

Kael, walking a now-familiar route back through the Lower Sector. Around him, the ordinary rhythm of the day continues — until it doesn't, in one small, specific way: a subtle, unusual disturbance, Breach-adjacent in character, registers at the edge of the environment. Not an alarm. Not a crisis. A wrongness, small enough that only someone paying close attention would catch it.

Kael catches it.

He stops. Doesn't reach for anything, doesn't call it in yet — just watches, still, reading it the way he reads everything: carefully, not slowly.

**DIALOGUE**

*(None. The scene closes on Kael's reaction alone.)*

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

This is the episode's only tonal shift. Per the seed package's own Character Performance Planning, the shift should be a small, precise change in posture — not panic, not alarm played big. The stillness that follows should feel like attention sharpening, not fear arriving.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Hold on Kael's face and posture as the disturbance registers; do not cut away to show the disturbance itself in more detail than the Art Bible's own subtle-cue guidance allows.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Per Art Bible `lighting-system.md` and `system-interference-visual-rules.md` — the shift should be legible in the environment's light/interference cue, not overplayed.

**SOUND NOTES**

One clean, distinct sound-design cue; per Section 11, the score has not been signaling this moment in advance.

**TRANSITION**

Hold on Kael, then cut to black — directly into Episode 2's own Beginning State (Phase 4B, Episode 2, seed authority).
