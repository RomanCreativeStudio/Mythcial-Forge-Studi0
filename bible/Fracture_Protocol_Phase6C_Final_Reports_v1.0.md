## Master Environment Certification

# THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL — PHASE 6C — MASTER ENVIRONMENT CERTIFICATION

**Determination question, per the directive's own instruction:** "Determine whether READY FOR PHASE 6D — PROP & TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTION or NOT READY."

## Determination: NOT READY

This determination is reached deliberately and is not a default or conservative hedge — it reflects the actual state of the evidence gathered across all 10 named Environment Production Packages. Documenting every blocking issue, per the directive's own instruction for a NOT READY determination:

**Blocking issues:**

1. **Cassian's Private Interior (the Ascendant Core) — Blocked on architectural specification.** No architecture, construction, materials, scale, dimensions, or layer placement exists anywhere in Locked Canon beyond a single Faction Bible sentence establishing its narrative function. Concept art cannot begin.
2. **Guardian/Kael Partnership Environment — Blocked on architectural specification.** Same category of gap; a "Season One established asset" with no independent description anywhere in the Phase 5B screenplay corpus. Concept art cannot begin.
3. **Night Haven Institutional Space — Flagged for Founder Decision.** An unresolved, screenplay-internal lighting contradiction (Episodes 14/22 call it "cooler," Episode 27 calls it "warmer") must be adjudicated before a concept-art lighting brief can be finalized.
4. **Ascendant Network World-Scale Environment — Flagged for Founder Decision.** Poses a genuine, unresolved production-design question — how to visually represent a civilization-scale network with, per Locked Faction Bible canon, "no single headquarters" — that has no precedent among any other environment in this production.
5. **Doctrine Institutional Environment — Flagged for Founder Decision.** An unresolved tension between the Faction Bible's "no single headquarters" description and this environment's own staging as a specific, recurring gathering space.
6. **Populated Civilian Area — Flagged for Founder Decision.** An open Lower Sector-vs-Mid Zone layer choice, explicitly left undecided by the screenplay's own Production Notes, with real downstream material/color consequences.
7. **The ~18 remaining lower-frequency Season One/Arc Two environments have not been individually identified, let alone built.** This was a deliberate, explicitly-flagged deferral in Phase 6C's own governance document (consistent with Phase 6A's prior discipline against inventing IDs for unneeded locations) — but it remains a genuine, large-scale gap in overall Environment Production completeness, not a resolved item.

**What IS certified complete:**
- Environment Canon (Phase 6C's own governance document) is Locked, effective immediately.
- All 10 named Environment Production Packages are compiled, audited, and Approved — including 4 that are genuinely production-ready (Ward Station Seven, Reconstruction-Work, Formal Meeting, Neutral Private Meeting Environments).
- Production hierarchy remains intact — no Locked Canon, screenplay, or prior-phase document was altered or contradicted.
- Asset Registry has been updated to reflect all 10 named environments' individual, differentiated statuses.
- Five genuine discrepancies in Phase 6A's original episode-range data were found and corrected, each explicitly documented rather than silently inherited.

**What is NOT yet certified:** "Environment Production is complete" — six of ten named environments carry an explicit, unresolved blocker, and roughly two-thirds of the production's total named-environment count (the ~18 deferred locations) has not yet begun.

**Recommendation, not a self-authorized next step:** the four flagged-for-Founder-decision items (Night Haven's lighting contradiction, the Ascendant Network's representational design question, the Doctrine environment's headquarters tension, and the Populated Civilian Area's layer choice) are narrow, answerable questions that could plausibly be resolved in a single focused Founder decision pass, after which those four environments would likely convert to "In Progress" status. The two "Blocked on architectural specification" environments (Cassian's Private Interior, Guardian/Kael Partnership Environment) require more substantial new design input. The ~18 deferred environments require a dedicated identification pass before they can be built at all. These are offered as observations to inform the Founder's own next-step decision, not as a plan this document authorizes itself.

Per the directive's own instruction, this NOT READY determination documents every blocking issue rather than proceeding to Phase 6D. Founder Approval is still applied below to certify this document's own findings as accurate and complete, distinct from certifying environment production itself as complete.
