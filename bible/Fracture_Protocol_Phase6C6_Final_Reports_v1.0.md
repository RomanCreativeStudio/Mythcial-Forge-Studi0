## Step 2 Outcome — Night Haven Lighting

**Re-verification performed directly against every screenplay reference**, at the final Phase 5B level specifically (not merely the earlier Phase 4B planning stage already checked in Phase 6C.5):

- Episode 14: "A formal, cool register — distinct from Light Order's familiar warmth" (Action); "cooler, more formal register marking Night Haven as distinct" (Lighting Direction) — stated twice, as the episode's own settled formal instruction.
- Episode 22: silent on Night Haven-specific lighting; describes only the shared institutional/briefing register. Not a third data point.
- Episode 27: "Night Haven's warmer resistance tone" (Lighting Notes); "Night Haven's warmer resistance tone... carried forward" (Lighting Direction) — also stated twice, also the episode's own settled formal instruction.

**Determination: Contradictory, not contextual.** No time-of-day, story-mood, power-failure, or any other in-text narrative cause is stated anywhere to justify the difference. Episode 27's own "established... carried forward" phrasing rules out reading "warmer" as a situational deviation — it explicitly treats the warmer register as standing and continuous, directly conflicting with Episode 14's equally explicit "cooler" register.

**Exact conflict identified per the directive's instruction; no solution proposed.** This is a genuine, unresolved authorial contradiction between two Locked-Approved screenplays, requiring direct Founder adjudication.

**Production Readiness: BLOCKED** *(unchanged — confirmed, not newly discovered)*.
