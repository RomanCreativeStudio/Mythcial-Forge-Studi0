## 1. Episode Identity

- **Episode number:** 6
- **Episode title:** "The Man Before the Myth"
- **Core purpose:** Develop Titan's legacy, stage one — memory and Light 98th culture only (Phase 4A, Episode 6, Purpose).
- **Narrative objective:** Dramatize the gap between Titan the legend and Titan the ordinary, self-doubting man (Phase 2A.2, Section 2, item 6; Phase 2C, Titan Section 2) — and the specific risk that Kael, absorbing only the legend, places him on a pedestal too high to live up to (Phase 2C, Titan Section 3).
- **Emotional objective:** A quiet, unsettling recognition — Kael learns that the man he's spent this season measuring himself against carried the exact same flaw he does.
- **Audience experience goal:** This episode should feel like meeting someone through other people's memory of them — warm, incomplete, occasionally contradictory — and should leave the audience (and Kael) with more weight, not resolution.

---

## 2. Story Structure

- **Opening hook:** Kael engages with Titan's memory through Light 98th culture — not a flashback opening on Titan himself, but Kael entering a space or occasion where his memory is actively kept (Phase 4A, Episode 6, Beginning; Phase 2C, Titan Section 1).
- **Setup:** The Light 98th's cultural relationship to Titan is established — reverent, formal, legend-facing — before any more personal account appears.
- **Rising conflict:** The gap widens between the polished legend Kael has always known and a more human account someone who actually served with him offers, unprompted.
- **Character turning point:** Kael hears, specifically, that Titan's own defining flaw was carrying every burden alone rather than letting others share it (Character Bible, "The Man") — the same flaw the season has been building in Kael since Episode 2.
- **Major reveal or progression:** No new lore is introduced — this is Kael learning an already-Locked fact about Titan for the first time on screen, not a new fact being created (Phase 2C, Titan Section 1–2).
- **Climax:** Not a confrontation — the moment Kael fully registers what he's just learned, and what it might mean about the standard he's been holding himself to.
- **Closing emotional beat:** Kael's growing responsibility begins — a private, early resolve toward advancement, not framed as inspired confidence but as something more complicated (Phase 4A, Episode 6, Ending).
- **Cliffhanger / Transition:** Into Kael's formal advancement bid (Phase 4A, Episode 6, Transition) — tonal, not a plot cliffhanger.

## 3. Scene Breakdown

*Five scenes proposed, sized for Studio OS's Mainline Episode runtime target. Titan does not appear on screen in any scene — every account of him is delivered through other characters' memory, Light 98th cultural practice, or ambient environmental detail (a training space he once used, a marker, an object), never a live-action or clearly-dramatized flashback of Titan himself acting.*

**Scene 1 — Kept Memory**
- Purpose: Establish the Light 98th's ongoing cultural relationship to Titan's memory.
- Location: Mid Zone — Titan's own Locked Home Zone (Character Bible, "Mars to the Megacity") — or a Light 98th space within it.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed Light 98th personnel, some old enough to have served alongside Titan.
- Emotional state: Reverent, formal, a little distant — the legend's register.
- Conflict: None yet.
- Visual requirements: Mid Zone environment, deepening its established association with Titan's and Mira's shared history (Phase 4A, Episode 6, World Progression; Phase 2D, Section 2) — no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should establish the legend's formal shape — his reputation, not yet his person.
- Production needs: New Mid Zone dressing (a training space, memorial marker, or similar Light 98th-affiliated detail); checked against the Asset Registry before build.

**Scene 2 — The Legend**
- Purpose: Present the myth layer fully, before it's complicated.
- Location: Same as Scene 1.
- Characters present: Kael; a senior, unnamed Light 98th figure recounting Titan's reputation.
- Emotional state: Admiring, larger-than-life.
- Conflict: None yet — this scene exists to make the pedestal concrete before Scene 3 begins to trouble it.
- Visual requirements: Same environment.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should sound exactly like what a legend sounds like — accomplished, composed, without visible doubt.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 3 — The Man**
- Purpose: Introduce the human layer, per Phase 2E's explicit requirement that any memory sequence preserve the man, not only the legend.
- Location: Same as Scenes 1–2, or a more private adjacent space.
- Characters present: Kael; a different unnamed Light 98th figure — someone who served closer to Titan personally, not merely the institution's formal memory of him.
- Emotional state: Warmer, more candid — real humor and real fear described alongside real doubt (Character Bible, "The Man").
- Conflict: The gap between Scene 2's legend and this scene's person becomes explicit for the first time.
- Visual requirements: Same environment, closer framing per Art Bible `cinematic-mood-guide.md`.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should name specific, ordinary human qualities — self-doubt, humor, fear — never treated as diminishing him, consistent with Phase 2E's requirement that this material never collapse the man/myth distinction by omission.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 4 — What He Carried**
- Purpose: Land the episode's central beat — Titan's own defining flaw.
- Location: Same as Scene 3.
- Characters present: Kael; the same Light 98th figure from Scene 3.
- Emotional state: Kael — quiet, recognizing something in himself; the speaker — matter-of-fact, without judgment of Titan.
- Conflict: Kael learns Titan's own recorded, defining mistake was believing he had to carry every burden alone rather than let others share it (Character Bible, "The Man") — the same flaw the season has built in Kael since Episode 2.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; Kael's reaction should be readable but restrained.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should state the fact plainly, without editorializing that Kael shares it — the audience and Kael should make that connection themselves.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 5 — What He Decides**
- Purpose: Close the episode on Kael's private, complicated resolve.
- Location: A quieter moment, alone or nearly so.
- Characters present: Kael only, or Kael with minimal unnamed presence.
- Emotional state: Weighted, not inspired — a decision made under pressure of what he's just learned, not a clean epiphany.
- Conflict: None external — entirely internal, per the seed entry's own Ending note.
- Visual requirements: Same or adjacent environment; a quieter close.
- Audio requirements: Minimal dialogue or none.
- Production needs: No new asset.

## 4. Character Performance Planning

**Kael.**
- Motivations this episode: To understand the man behind the standard he's measured himself against since before the season began (Phase 2A.2, Section 1 — "Titan's death is already Kael's past when the audience meets him").
- Emotional progression: Reverent distance (Scenes 1–2) → growing unease as the legend complicates (Scene 3) → recognition of his own flaw mirrored (Scene 4) → weighted, private resolve (Scene 5).
- Dialogue intent, not lines: in Scenes 1–2, his questions should come from admiration, not skepticism. In Scene 4, his reaction to learning Titan's flaw should not be stated aloud as "that's my flaw too" — the recognition should register on him, not be explained by dialogue.
- Body language: composed and attentive through Scenes 1–2; a visible stillness or held breath at Scene 4's revelation; in Scene 5, per Phase 2E's Emotional Continuity requirement for Kael, his resolve must not read as his flaw resolved — it should read as him choosing to take on more, which the audience (per Episode 3's established pattern) should recognize as double-edged, not simply triumphant.
- Relationship dynamics: no core-cast relationship is active this episode beyond his relationship to Titan's legacy itself — Mira does not appear, consistent with Phase 4A's own Character Focus for this episode and with the reciprocal Kael/Mira mechanism being explicitly reserved for Arc Two or early Arc Three (Phase 2C, Titan Section 4).

**Titan's legacy (memory only — not a live-action character this episode).**
- Motivations: not applicable — Titan has no active present-tense arc, per his own Locked Canon Alignment Principle (Phase 2C, Titan Section 1).
- Presentation requirement: every scene referencing him must carry both the legend (accomplished, composed, second-in-command of an elite formation) and the man (self-doubting, capable of real humor and real fear, one recorded defining mistake) — Scene 2 alone would violate Phase 2E's requirement if the episode stopped there; Scenes 3–4 are structurally required, not optional texture.
- Dialogue intent (as reported by others, never as Titan speaking directly on screen): the Light 98th figures' accounts should sound like two people describing two real, compatible sides of the same person, not a contradiction to be resolved — consistent with "both are canon, neither replaces the other" (Phase 2E, Section 4, Titan's Production Guide).

**Unnamed Light 98th figures (background, non-canon-named).**
- Motivations: institutional pride (Scene 2 speaker) and personal, unguarded memory (Scene 3–4 speaker) — two distinct registers, deliberately not the same figure, so the episode doesn't collapse the legend and the man into one voice.
- No named character is created for either figure — both remain generic, reusable background roles, consistent with the pattern already established in Episodes 2–3.

## 5. Visual Production Planning

- **Required environments:** Mid Zone, with new set dressing establishing a Light 98th-affiliated space tied to Titan's memory (a training ground, memorial marker, or similar) — this deepens the existing Mid Zone asset rather than introducing a new location (Phase 2D, Section 2).
- **Character appearances:** Kael, per his existing reference sheet. Unnamed Light 98th figures, generic and reusable, consistent with prior episodes. **No Titan character asset is required or proposed** — he does not appear on screen.
- **Props:** Any memorial or training-space set dressing (per Scene 1) — checked against the Asset Registry before build; no new prop invents any fact about Titan beyond what's already Locked.
- **Effects:** None required.
- **Technology:** None required beyond what's already Locked.
- **Lighting:** Per Art Bible's `lighting-system.md` — warmer and more reverent for Scenes 1–2, closer and more intimate for Scenes 3–5, within existing zone-identity and mood rules.
- **Cinematic requirements:** Per Art Bible's `camera-language.md` and `cinematic-mood-guide.md` — the shift from formal, wider framing (legend) to closer, quieter framing (man) is a mood note tracking the episode's own structure, not a shot list.

---

## 6. Audio Planning

- **Narration needs:** None.
- **Dialogue priorities:** Scenes 2 through 4 carry this episode's dialogue weight; Scene 1 is largely ambient/atmospheric, Scene 5 minimal or silent.
- **Sound design requirements:** Formal, slightly reverent ambience for Scenes 1–2; quieter, more intimate ambience for Scenes 3–5 — per Phase 3A's routing to Production OS's Music Production SOP and Studio OS's Music & Audio Standards.
- **Music tone:** Warm but never triumphant — this is not a hero's-legacy montage; the score should leave room for Scene 4's discomfort to register without being underlined.
- **Emotional timing:** No musical cue should resolve Scene 4's recognition or Scene 5's weighted decision — the episode should end on genuine complexity, consistent with its own Central Conflict framing.

- **Characters:** Kael — already built. Two unnamed, generic, reusable Light 98th background figures — checked against the Asset Registry before build, scoped for potential reuse in future Light 98th culture scenes, not single-use.
- **Environments:** Mid Zone, reused, with new Light 98th-affiliated set dressing — checked against the Asset Registry before build.
- **Props:** Any memorial/training-space set dressing — checked against the Asset Registry before build.
- **Effects:** None required.
- **Reusable assets:** Both new items (the background figures, the Mid Zone dressing) are scoped explicitly for reuse in later Light-98th-culture scenes, not treated as one-off builds.
