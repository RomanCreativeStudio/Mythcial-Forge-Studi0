### What Changed Between Phase 6C.6 and Phase 6C.7

Phase 6C.6 correctly found no *in-episode* stated cause for the warm/cool difference — neither Episode 14 nor Episode 27 explicitly explains its own register or cross-references a reason. That finding is not overturned; it remains true that the episodes themselves do not narrate an explanation. What Phase 6C.7 adds is a check the prior two passes did not perform: whether an **already-Locked general rule**, applied to this environment's own confirmed facts, independently produces the observed pattern without requiring the episodes to state it themselves. Two such rules were found:

1. **`lighting-system.md`'s Emotional Lighting Modes rule [LOCKED]:** lighting modes are "chosen by story beat, never by scene convenience" — establishing as a matter of general Locked Canon that no environment is expected to carry one fixed lighting identity across every appearance.
2. **`lighting-system.md`'s Night Lighting rule [LOCKED]:** the megacity's base color-temperature grammar codes cool as institutional/systemic and warm as practical/human — a permanent, general vocabulary already governing every environment in the production.

Applied to Night Haven's own Locked Faction Bible identity (Belief: "cooperation, independence, and choice"; formative crisis: resisting "a power-grab against its independence"; Culture: "compassion... memory") and its own confirmed narrative arc (Episode 14/22's measured institutional engagement → Episode 27's active resistance under civilizational-scale pressure), these two Locked rules predict exactly the pattern observed: cooler in the institutional beats, warmer in the resistance beat. This is reconciliation — connecting existing Locked sources for the first time — not invention of new canon.

### Permanent Lighting Canon Established

Full text in the Night Haven package's own Phase 6C.7 Lighting Resolution Section. Summary: Night Haven's lighting is governed by beat-type, not a fixed signature — cooler/institutional for diplomatic beats, warmer/resistance for active-resistance beats, per the Locked cool=institutional/warm=human grammar and Night Haven's own Locked ideological identity. This rule is specific to Night Haven and does not set precedent for any other faction or environment without its own independent Locked-canon basis.
