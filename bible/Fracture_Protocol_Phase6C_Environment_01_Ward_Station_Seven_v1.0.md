## 2. Narrative Purpose

Ward Station Seven is Kael's home institution — the Ward Division post where he was "raised and trained as a Cadet" after being orphaned by a Breach (World Bible §5–6). It is the saga's establishing environment (Episode 1) and its most-reused location by a wide margin. Narratively, it functions as the fixed point Kael returns to across nearly all of Season One — the place that grounds his arc before the story's geography widens into Arc Two's institutional and civilian-scale locations.

## 3. Screenplay Appearances (Screenplay Validation)

**First Appearance:** Episode 1 (establishing episode).
**Final confirmed Appearance:** Episode 23.
**Recurring Episodes, confirmed by direct text verification:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 22, 23.

**Discrepancy flagged, not silently inherited:** Phase 6A's Master Asset Backlog lists Ward Station Seven's episode range as "Season One recurring (1–12), plus 22, 23, 27." Direct re-verification against the actual screenplay text found this is **not accurate for Episodes 6 and 27**: Episode 6's own Location List places its scenes in "Mid Zone — Light 98th-affiliated space," not Ward Station Seven; Episode 27 contains zero occurrences of "Ward Station Seven" anywhere in its text — its locations are the Light Order, Night Haven, and Doctrine institutional environments plus a Kael transitional space. This discrepancy is recorded here and will be carried into the Environment Production Report rather than silently correcting Phase 6A's own document.

**Scene References:** Episode 1, Scene 2 (establishing interior); Episode 2, Scene 1 (alarm/mobilization); Episode 3, Scene 1 (post-crisis quiet); Episode 7, Scene 1 (formal evaluation context); Episode 12, Scene 1 (review/ceremony/command dressing); Episodes 8–11 (lighting-register variation plus "communications context" and "reporting context" dressing variants); Episodes 22–23 (briefing-context reuse).

**Speaking Character Interaction:** Kael (primary, virtually every scene); unnamed Ward colleagues and evaluators (Episode 7); an unnamed Night Haven representative appears as a character present within this space in Episode 14 (per the Section 3 correction — Episode 14's own Location List places all five of its scenes at Ward Station Seven, not a distinct Night Haven environment; see Environment 6's package for the full cross-reference).

**Major Story Function:** establishes Kael's baseline world before the story's geography widens; hosts his routine, his crises (Episode 2's alarm), his formal evaluations (Episode 7, 12), and his institution's reaction to Arc Two developments (Episodes 22–23).
