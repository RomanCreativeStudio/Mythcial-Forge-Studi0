## Category Selection

**Character.**

Chosen over Environment, Prop, and VFX Asset on direct, existing evidence:
- The Production Authority Hierarchy (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase60_Production_Pipeline_Governance_v1.0.md`) itself orders **Character Assets (level 8) before Environment Assets (level 9)** — Character takes structural priority whenever both categories are ready.
- Props has exactly one candidate (FPP-TECH-001, "the comm"), which carries the most unresolved open items of any confirmed asset in the production (an unresolved governing-environment either/or, and a wholly unspecified form factor) and the lowest priority ranking (4, lowest) anywhere in Phase 6 — the weakest-supported starting point.
- VFX's strongest candidate (Kael's eye-glow, FPP-VFX-001) is a character-attached effect that cannot be meaningfully concept-arted independent of the character's own face — it is structurally downstream of Character concept art, not a parallel starting point.
- Environment's strongest candidate (Ward Station Seven) is genuinely excellent — its own package explicitly states it is "authoritative for all subsequent Ward Station Seven production (concept art, set/environment build...)" with only non-blocking open items (Scale, Dimensions, Weather Interaction, Traffic Behaviour, Soundscape) — but Character still precedes it per the Hierarchy's own level order, above.

## Selected Subject

**Kael Aurelian Veyr (FPP-CHAR-001).**

## Reasoning

- **Priority 1 in every Phase 6 discipline without exception** — Character (FPP-CHAR-001), Storyboard (FPP-SB-001, Ward Station Seven sequences, his most frequent setting), Animation (FPP-ANIM-001, "the production's only fully-confirmed character animation library"), Audio (FPP-AUD-001, "highest reuse in the production"), and Visual Effects (FPP-VFX-001, "highest-reuse VFX asset in the production"). No other single subject in this entire production carries this consistent a Priority 1 ranking across five independent disciplines.
- **Fewest open items of any character.** His Character Production Package's own Production status field confirms Costume Authority Complete with "only exact concept-art numeric/color values and the unconfirmed Formal Outfit scene" remaining — the first of these is not a pre-existing gap blocking concept art, it is the literal output concept art work itself produces; the second affects only his lowest-priority, least-confirmed outfit register, not his Primary Outfit (worn across all 36 episodes).
- **Appears in all 36 episodes**, the only character or environment in the entire production with universal reuse — maximal production value from the very first concept art asset.
- **Unblocks downstream work directly**: his own eye-glow VFX (FPP-VFX-001) cannot be meaningfully concept-arted without his face already established, so beginning with Kael is also the fastest path to the VFX category's own strongest candidate becoming viable.
