## 1. Episode Identity

- **Episode number:** 15
- **Episode title:** Placeholder only, not final — "How Far It Reaches" (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15 seed entry).
- **Core purpose:** Reveal the Ascendant Network's true scale before Cassian is personally introduced (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Purpose).
- **Narrative objective:** Establish, through institutional evidence rather than personal encounter, that the Doctrine is not a fringe threat but a distributed, civilization-spanning movement (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Central Conflict).
- **Emotional objective:** Quiet dread at scale — not fear of a person, but the unsettling realization of how large and diffuse the threat actually is.
- **Audience experience goal:** This episode should feel like watching a map unfold further than expected — the audience should end it understanding the size of what's coming before any character does.

---

## 2. Story Structure

- **Opening hook:** Evidence begins surfacing, through Ward Division/Light 98th institutional channels, that the Doctrine's presence extends further than previously understood.
- **Setup:** The Doctrine, known only as a threat in principle since Episode 12, has never had its actual size established (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Beginning State).
- **Rising conflict:** Evidence surfaces that the Ascendant Network is "a distributed, civilization-spanning movement, not a fringe group" (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Central Conflict; Phase 2A.2, Section 4, "Expansion of the world"; Faction Bible, "Operational Structure — The Ascendant Network").
- **Character turning point:** None — per the seed entry, no individual character development occurs this episode; it is world-scale.
- **Major reveal or progression:** The single largest world-fact expansion of Arc Two's opening movement — the Network's true scale — using only what the Faction Bible already states (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, World Progression).
- **Climax:** The moment the full scope of the evidence is assembled and understood by Kael's institution — not a confrontation, a realization.
- **Closing emotional beat:** The scale of what Kael, Light Order, and Night Haven are actually facing becomes visible for the first time (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Ending Beat).
- **Cliffhanger / Transition:** Into Movement II's revelation cluster, beginning with Cassian himself (Phase 4A.2, Episode 15, Transition; Episode 16's own seed entry).

## 3. Scene Breakdown

*Five scenes proposed, sized for Studio OS's Mainline Episode runtime target. This episode is deliberately institutional and evidentiary in structure — a mystery of scale being assembled, not a personal drama.*

**Scene 1 — Isolated Reports**
- Purpose: Open on scattered, seemingly unrelated evidence beginning to surface.
- Location: Ward Station Seven.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th intelligence personnel.
- Emotional state: Neutral, procedural — nothing yet suggests scale.
- Conflict: None yet — the scene establishes fragments, not conclusions.
- Visual requirements: Ward Station Seven interior, reused asset.
- Audio requirements: Procedural dialogue intent — intent only.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 2 — A Pattern Forms**
- Purpose: Show the fragments beginning to connect.
- Location: Ward Station Seven, an intelligence/analysis context.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th personnel.
- Emotional state: Growing unease as disparate reports start to align.
- Conflict: The pieces individually seemed minor; together, they suggest something larger.
- Visual requirements: Same environment category; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should convey mounting realization without naming a final conclusion yet.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 3 — Civilization-Spanning**
- Purpose: Deliver the episode's central revelation.
- Location: Ward Station Seven, a formal briefing context.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th leadership.
- Emotional state: Grave, sobered — the moment the scale becomes undeniable.
- Conflict: The evidence now clearly shows the Ascendant Network as "a distributed, civilization-spanning movement, not a fringe group" — a direct, institution-shaking realization.
- Visual requirements: Same environment category; no new location, no new Ascendant Network iconography beyond what's already Locked in the Faction Bible.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should stay strictly bounded by the Faction Bible's existing "Operational Structure" entry — no new organizational detail, leader, or historical claim.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 4 — What Night Haven Already Knew**
- Purpose: Establish that this scale realization reaches beyond Kael's own institution.
- Location: Ward Station Seven or an adjacent institutional space where political/administrative information circulates, consistent with the season's established pattern (Episodes 7, 13, 14).
- Characters present: Unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th personnel discussing the realization's reach; no named Night Haven individual.
- Emotional state: Procedural, grave — the scale is now shared knowledge, not isolated to Kael's own chain of command.
- Conflict: None direct — the scene exists to establish that Night Haven, too, is now facing this same realization, per the seed entry's own Ending Beat.
- Visual requirements: Same environment category; no new location, no Night Haven character design.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should confirm the realization's reach without inventing any new Night Haven detail beyond what Episode 14 already established.
- Production needs: No new asset.

**Scene 5 — The Size of It**
- Purpose: Close the episode on the world-scale realization, without personal reaction.
- Location: Ward Station Seven.
- Characters present: Kael; unnamed Ward Division/Light 98th personnel.
- Emotional state: Quiet, sobered — Kael as one witness among many to something far larger than himself.
- Conflict: None direct — the scene exists to let the scale of the revelation settle before the season moves toward its personal consequences in Movement II.
- Visual requirements: Same environment; no new location.
- Audio requirements: Dialogue intent should gesture toward what comes next without naming Cassian or revealing Episode 16's content.
- Production needs: No new asset.
