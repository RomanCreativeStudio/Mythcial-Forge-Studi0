## Screenplay Regression Rule

Before any screenplay may receive Founder Approval, it shall undergo a complete Regression Verification, comparing the screenplay against:
- its corresponding approved Phase 4B Production Development Package
- all higher-level Locked Canon
- the immediately preceding approved screenplay
- any earlier screenplay whose continuity directly affects the current episode

**First-episode note:** for Episode 1 (and for any future episode with no immediately preceding screenplay yet approved, or no earlier screenplay whose continuity directly affects it), the "preceding approved screenplay" and "earlier screenplay" checks are satisfied as **Not Applicable** rather than skipped — recorded explicitly in each screenplay's own Regression Verification section, not silently omitted.

---

## Mandatory Regression Verification

Verify that: every approved story beat is present; no approved story beat has been omitted; no new story beat has been introduced; no canon milestone has been moved; chronology remains correct; pacing preserves the approved episode structure; character progression follows the approved arc; emotional progression matches approved continuity; dialogue reflects only established characterization; visual interpretation does not create new canon; environmental storytelling does not imply unsupported lore; cinematography does not alter story meaning; audio direction does not imply unsupported narrative; editing choices do not change chronology; production notes remain subordinate to canon.

## Character Regression Verification

Confirm that: motivations remain unchanged; personalities remain unchanged; relationships remain unchanged; emotional state matches the previous approved screenplay; future development is not introduced early; protected mysteries remain protected; unresolved conflicts remain unresolved until their approved episode.

## Continuity Regression Verification

Confirm: scene order is correct; timeline remains consistent; transitions align with the previous screenplay; recurring locations remain visually consistent; recurring props remain consistent; recurring terminology remains consistent; no contradiction exists with earlier approved scripts.

## Canon Regression Verification

Confirm: every scene traces directly to approved canon; no screenplay expands lore; no screenplay replaces canon; no screenplay interprets uncertainty as fact; the Missing Canon Rule (Phase 5 Canon Implementation Lock) has been followed whenever applicable.

## Asset Regression Verification

Verify: all required assets exist in the Asset Registry, or any missing asset is explicitly marked Asset Creation Required. Screenplays shall never invent production assets in place of registry entries.

---

## Regression Audit Structure (required in every screenplay, in addition to Phase 5A's own Screenplay Audit)

1. Repository Verification
2. Canon Regression
3. Story Regression
4. Character Regression
5. Dialogue Regression
6. Visual Regression
7. Audio Regression
8. Asset Regression
9. Continuity Regression
10. Final Regression Certification

Each section concludes with **PASS / PASS WITH OBSERVATIONS / FAIL**.

---

## Blocking Conditions

Founder Approval shall not be granted if Regression Verification finds: canon contradiction; chronology conflict; missing approved story beat; unauthorized story addition; unsupported characterization; altered milestone placement; protected mystery violation; unsupported lore implication; repository integrity issue. Blocking issues must be resolved before approval.

---

## Automatic Workflow Integration (supersedes Phase 5A Section 10's nine-step list with this ten-step list)

For every screenplay:
1. Build screenplay.
2. Perform internal verification.
3. Run Founder Review & Alignment Audit (Phase 5A, Section 9).
4. Run Regression Verification (this document).
5. Resolve blocking issues.
6. Apply Founder Approval.
7. Commit.
8. Push.
9. Verify repository integrity.
10. Continue automatically to the next screenplay unless stopped by: a blocking canon issue, a repository integrity issue, a Missing Canon Rule trigger, or an explicit Founder Directive.

---

## Permanent Production Rule

Regression Verification is mandatory for every screenplay produced during Phase 5. No screenplay may bypass this process. This directive remains permanently in force unless explicitly superseded by a later Founder Directive.
