## Storyboard Plan

*Breaks all 36 episodes into a storyboard production order optimized for asset reuse, not necessarily broadcast order.*

**Recommended storyboard batching, grouped by shared-environment/shared-character clusters rather than strict episode-number sequence:**

1. **Batch A — Season One core (Episodes 1–12):** built in broadcast order; establishes Ward Station Seven, Kael, and (from Episode 5) Mira. Highest storyboard volume per new-asset-dollar since this batch establishes the saga's most-reused environment and its two most-used characters.
2. **Batch B — Arc Two opening (Episodes 13–15):** Night Haven and Ascendant Network environments introduced; no new character.
3. **Batch C — Cassian cluster (Episodes 16–18, 21):** Cassian and the formal meeting environment introduced and reused four times within a tight span — strong candidate for storyboarding as a contiguous block regardless of the episodes' narrative gaps, to maximize continuity of visual design decisions.
4. **Batch D — Guardian/Mira interior cluster (Episodes 19, 20, 24–26, 32–33):** the Guardian/Kael partnership environment and the reconstruction environment recur most heavily in this cluster; storyboarding these together supports maximum lighting/blocking reuse.
5. **Batch E — Civilian-scale crisis (Episodes 22–23):** the populated civilian area and disaster-response environments are unique to this two-episode span.
6. **Batch F — Movement II Cassian arc (Episodes 27–31):** the new Doctrine institutional environment (27) and neutral meeting environment (29) are introduced here, alongside continued reuse of Cassian's private interior.
7. **Batch G — Finale (Episodes 34–36):** cross-cutting, multi-environment episodes; storyboarded last since they depend on every environment already established in Batches A–F.

**Flagged:** this is a reuse-optimization recommendation, not a mandated order — actual storyboard sequencing remains a Founder/production-management decision.

## Animation Plan

Organized by the four axes specified in the directive:

- **By character:** Kael (present in all 36 episodes — the highest-volume animation load by a wide margin), then Cassian (16 episodes), then Mira (11 episodes), then the Guardian (9 episodes).
- **By environment:** Ward Station Seven and its variants carry the highest animation-background load (Season One's 12 episodes plus 3 Arc Two episodes); the four-location cluster (Cassian's interior, Guardian/Kael space, reconstruction environment, formal meeting environment) carries the second-highest combined load.
- **By episode order:** broadcast order remains the default for animation sequencing once assets exist, since it matches the Storyboard Plan's Batch A-first structure and the continuity chain validated in Phase 5C.
- **By shared assets:** the 10 core recurring locations identified in the Master Asset Backlog account for the substantial majority of all 175 scenes across the saga (per Phase 5C's Production Readiness Report) — animation production should prioritize these shared assets first, since their completion unlocks the largest number of subsequent scenes.
- **By animation complexity:** solo/interior two-character episodes (17, 19, 25, 26, 28–33) are lower complexity (contained blocking, minimal cross-cutting); institutional/multi-thread episodes (2, 12, 15, 16, 21–24, 27, 34–36) are higher complexity (cross-cutting between multiple simultaneous locations and larger background-character counts).

## Editing Plan

Standard pipeline stages, as no episode-specific editing detail exists in any screenplay (editing is Level 12 in the Phase 6.0 Production Authority Hierarchy, downstream of Animation and Audio):

1. **Assembly** — raw animated scenes assembled in screenplay scene order per episode.
2. **Rough Cut** — pacing pass against each screenplay's own Section 16 (Editing Direction) intent.
3. **Fine Cut** — refined against each screenplay's own Section 12 (Camera Direction) and Section 16 pacing notes.
4. **Sound Mix** — voice, music, and SFX layered per each screenplay's Section 10/11.
5. **Color** — per each screenplay's Section 14 (Lighting Direction) intent.
6. **Final Export** — per Studio OS's own delivery specification (not itemized in any screenplay; routed generically).
7. **Quality Control** — verification against the screenplay itself, per Phase 5B.5's Production Rule ("Every production department must build directly from the Approved Screenplay").

**Flagged:** no screenplay specifies exact runtime, only the 8–14 minute target band (Phase 5C, Deliverable 4) — exact runtime is confirmed as an Editing-stage determination, not fabricated here.

## Production Schedule

**Recommended production order, optimized for maximum reuse and minimum duplicate work:**

1. **Phase 6B (next phase) — Character Asset Production, Priority 1 characters first:** Kael, then Cassian, Mira, and the Guardian (order among these three flexible, but all three needed before Episode 16's cluster can be produced in full).
2. **Phase 6C (anticipated, not yet authorized) — Environment Asset Production, Priority 1–2 locations first:** Ward Station Seven, then the four-location recurring cluster (Cassian's private interior, Guardian/Kael partnership environment, reconstruction environment, formal meeting environment).
3. **Concept Art and Storyboarding** proceed in parallel with character/environment asset production, following the Batch A–G order defined in the Storyboard Plan above.
4. **Lower-priority, single-use environments** (neutral meeting space, populated civilian area, Doctrine institutional space, and the remaining low-frequency Season One/Arc Two locations) are produced on-demand as their specific episode's production is reached, rather than front-loaded.
5. **Voice casting** should begin once character visual assets are sufficiently advanced to support casting decisions (flagged dependency, per the Audio Production Plan above) — this is a Recommendation for sequencing, not a blocking requirement on Phase 6B beginning.

**This document does not authorize Phase 6B, 6C, or any subsequent phase to begin** — per Phase 6.0's own Asset Approval Rule, each requires its own Founder Approval before commencing, consistent with the Automatic Workflow's own explicit "Stop only if... Founder instruction" boundary.
