## A Resolution Applied Here, Disclosed Rather Than Silent

This document's core content — an interplanetary civilization spanning Earth, Mars, and the Moon — directly conflicted with the World Bible's existing Locked description of the setting as *"a single, controlled megacity — not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place."* This conflict was flagged explicitly, twice, across the two prior directives that introduced this material (once with three explicit resolution options offered, once again in the Phase 1A compilation's "What This Document Does Not Yet Resolve" section). Neither flag was directly answered, but three consecutive Founder directives have now built on the same interplanetary framework without walking it back, and this one instructs: *"Only request Founder clarification if an actual canon contradiction is discovered."*

Given that pattern, this compilation proceeds on the following resolution, applied to World Bible Section 1 (see that document's own changelog for the exact edit): **the megacity remains Kael's home and the entirety of Season One's story setting, unchanged — the Authority System's total, unremovable local control (System Rule 2) is untouched.** What changes is the universe's *stated scope* beyond that story: the megacity now exists within the larger interplanetary civilization this document describes, rather than being the literal totality of everything that exists. "Not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place" is preserved as a statement about the *story's* grounding, not the universe's outer size.

This is the one interpretive choice in this document that goes beyond pure compilation, and it is flagged here in full rather than folded silently into the text below.

## Phase 1B.2 — Political & Government Framework

### Government

The official interplanetary governing body is the **United Solar System Nations (USCN)**. It exists to preserve peace, cooperation, diplomacy, law, equality, and mutual prosperity throughout the Solar System. Each member world retains its own sovereign government while participating in the USCN.

### Executive Government

The executive leader of the USCN is the **Chancellor**, who:
- Serves a five-year term.
- Is elected by the USCN Assembly.
- Oversees implementation of Assembly decisions.
- May veto legislation.
- May not declare a Solar System emergency without the approval of the three planetary presidents.
- Serves as Commander-in-Chief of the Solar Peace Corps during declared emergencies.
- Is subject to elections, impeachment, and judicial review.

### Legislature

The **USCN Assembly** consists of 750 representatives: 250 from Earth, 250 from Mars, 250 from the Moon.

A Chancellor veto may be overridden only by an 80% Assembly vote, followed by review by the USCN Supreme Court.

**Flagged, not invented:** the source material states ordinary legislation "requires the required Assembly majority established in canon" — this is a self-referential placeholder in the Founder-provided text itself (it names a requirement without stating what that requirement actually is). No specific majority threshold is invented here to fill the gap; it remains open pending a specific number.

### Judiciary

The highest judicial authority is the **United Solar System Nations Supreme Court (USCN Supreme Court)**, responsible for:
- Constitutional review.
- Interplanetary legal disputes.
- Appeals from planetary courts.
- Judicial oversight of emergency powers.
- Protection of Charter rights.

### Emergency Government

Solar System emergencies require approval from the Chancellor, the President of Earth, the President of Mars, and the President of the Moon. Emergency authority automatically expires after 90 days unless renewed through constitutional procedure. Fundamental rights protected by the USCN Charter remain protected even during emergencies.

### Planetary Government

Each inhabited world operates as a Representative Democratic Republic, with a President, Vice President, and Secretary of State. Each retains authority over domestic law while recognizing USCN authority on interplanetary matters.

### Citizenship

Citizenship may be revoked only for the most serious crimes against civilization, including treason, espionage, and conspiracies that threaten governments or entire worlds. Dual planetary citizenship is legal. Naturalization is available to non-human species under USCN law (see Alien Civilization, above).

### Solar Peace Corps

The neutral interplanetary peacekeeping organization of the USCN. Its mission is to preserve peace, protect civilian life, defend the constitutional principles of the USCN, and maintain stability throughout the Solar System while remaining politically neutral. It answers directly to the USCN.

**Creed:** *"To be a peacekeeper means to first know the peace giver."*

### USCN Motto

*"Many Worlds. One Future."*

### USCN Core Values

Equality, Honor, Integrity, Justice, Compassion, Service, Peace, Cooperation, Hope.

### Political Philosophy, Diplomacy, Justice, Elections, Constitutional Framework

No content beyond what's captured in the sections above (Executive/Legislature/Judiciary/Emergency Government/Citizenship, and the Core Values list) has been provided for these as standalone topics. Not invented here; left as explicit gaps pending further Founder detail.
