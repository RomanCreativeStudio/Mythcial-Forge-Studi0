## A Resolution Applied Here, Disclosed Rather Than Silent

This document's core content — an interplanetary civilization spanning Earth, Mars, and the Moon — directly conflicted with the World Bible's existing Locked description of the setting as *"a single, controlled megacity — not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place."* This conflict was flagged explicitly, twice, across the two prior directives that introduced this material (once with three explicit resolution options offered, once again in the Phase 1A compilation's "What This Document Does Not Yet Resolve" section). Neither flag was directly answered, but three consecutive Founder directives have now built on the same interplanetary framework without walking it back, and this one instructs: *"Only request Founder clarification if an actual canon contradiction is discovered."*

Given that pattern, this compilation proceeds on the following resolution, applied to World Bible Section 1 (see that document's own changelog for the exact edit): **the megacity remains Kael's home and the entirety of Season One's story setting, unchanged — the Authority System's total, unremovable local control (System Rule 2) is untouched.** What changes is the universe's *stated scope* beyond that story: the megacity now exists within the larger interplanetary civilization this document describes, rather than being the literal totality of everything that exists. "Not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place" is preserved as a statement about the *story's* grounding, not the universe's outer size.

This is the one interpretive choice in this document that goes beyond pure compilation, and it is flagged here in full rather than folded silently into the text below.

## Phase 1B.1 — Human Civilization

**Civilization:** Human civilization exists across Earth, Mars, and the Moon, with additional military and research installations throughout the Solar System.

**Earth:** Humanity's historical home and the origin point every world still measures itself against. The Americas and Europe remain the major population hubs; Africa and Australia remain populated but resource-depressed by the war. The war has caused real environmental damage — depleting minerals and breaking down infrastructure — but the Amazon Jungle stands deliberately preserved and untouched, a standing symbol that restoration remains possible.

**Mars:** The primary economic and industrial center — mining, oil, water production, and agriculture. Its planetary conflict originated in elite corruption infiltrating the government from within ("old order groups"), later the trigger for the Light 98th Contubernium's Mars deployment (Phase 1A Production Bible §8).

**Moon:** The leading center for medicine, scientific research, and advanced development. It maintains an independent planetary military of its own in case of a coup, and its elected government is split along the same Light Order/Night Haven factional lines as Earth's.

**Flagged, not silently resolved:** a later Founder Discovery submission named Mars, rather than the Moon, as the scientific center. This directly conflicts with the Moon's role immediately above, which was compiled first and independently reconfirmed by a separate submission. Per the Canon Timeline Reconciliation Notice ("when a Final Canon Lock exists, that document takes priority... do not merge outdated concepts with locked canon"), the Moon remains the Locked scientific center; the conflicting claim is not incorporated here. It is logged, not discarded, in `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Proposal_Vault_v1.0.md`.

**Human Expansion:** Humanity has not expanded beyond the Solar System, due to ongoing political instability, war, corruption, and technological limitations — chief among the latter, unsolved long-term FTL travel (below).

**Population:** Two Founder Discovery submissions gave different, unreconciled totals for Earth/Mars/Moon population (approximately 3 billion / 7 billion / 7 billion in one, 3 billion / 7 billion / 9 billion in the other). Neither has been confirmed as final between the two, so no single figure is locked here; both remain logged in the Proposal Vault pending reconciliation.

**FTL Travel:** Confirmed to exist in limited form — short-range travel between worlds within the Solar System (on the order of days), achieved through a hyperspace/light-to-energy harness mechanism. It is energy-intensive, requires specialized infrastructure, and carries real risk over extended use (material degradation, explosion, radiation exposure). This is consistent with, not a contradiction of, the already-Locked Phase 1B.3 Technology Canon, which lists *"long-term FTL travel"* and *"materials capable of surviving extended FTL usage"* among humanity's greatest remaining unsolved challenges — what's confirmed here is exactly a technology that works for short local hops without having solved the long-term/extended-use problem. True interstellar-capable FTL remains unsolved, which is the technological half of why humanity hasn't expanded beyond the Solar System (see Human Expansion, above).

**Alien Civilization:** Confirmed to exist. Two native intelligent species share the Solar System alongside humanity — native Martians and native "Moon people" — both of whom lived in this system, in undetected contact with each other, for generations before humanity discovered them (first found while excavating cave systems into both worlds' interiors). Humanity has known of them for roughly 1,000 years. Relations are mixed but predominantly peaceful: most are allied with humanity, some remain wary or distrustful, some stay neutral. Interbreeding is confirmed — hybrid human-alien children exist and are recognized under law (see Citizenship, Phase 1B.2 below). This fills what was previously an explicit gap (only implied by the Citizenship framework's non-human naturalization clause) and does not contradict anything previously Locked. Their view of the Guardian: broadly regarded as useful, but approached with some caution, since they believe it may carry an unrecognized flaw of its own — not further defined, consistent with the Guardian's standing, deliberately preserved mystery (Phase 1A Production Bible §7).

**Government Foundations:** See Phase 1B.2 below in full.

**Medicine & Science:** Concentrated on the Moon (see above). Average human lifespan with proper care is approximately 250 years. Limb and organ regeneration and replacement are both possible, though imperfect compared to the original. Most genetic disease has been cured; Parkinson's disease and memory-loss conditions remain unsolved, the latter because intervention at the level of memory plasticity carries too much risk. Mental health strain from space travel and the ongoing war is an explicit, unresolved, and growing problem.

## Phase 1B.2 — Political & Government Framework

### Government

The official interplanetary governing body is the **United Solar System Nations (USCN)**. It exists to preserve peace, cooperation, diplomacy, law, equality, and mutual prosperity throughout the Solar System. Each member world retains its own sovereign government while participating in the USCN.

**Founding and headquarters:** the USCN was founded by the first three planetary presidents in the same year humanity first discovered intelligent alien life in this system (see Alien Civilization, Phase 1B.1 above) — Earth proposed a joint decision, and Mars and the Moon agreed it served peace and prosperity. Its headquarters is a station positioned in the gravitational middle of all three planets, with its own artificial gravitational field, chosen so no single world could be seen as more central than another.

### Executive Government

The executive leader of the USCN is the **Chancellor**, a primarily executive office (not a lawmaking one), who:
- Serves a five-year term.
- Is elected by the USCN Assembly.
- Oversees implementation of Assembly decisions and negotiates treaties with member-world presidents.
- May veto legislation.
- May not impeach a planetary president directly, but may subpoena a planetary government for review.
- May not declare a Solar System emergency without the approval of the three planetary presidents.
- Serves as Commander-in-Chief of the Solar Peace Corps during declared emergencies.
- Is subject to elections, impeachment, recall election, and judicial review — checks that exist specifically to prevent the office from drifting toward dictatorship.

### Legislature

The **USCN Assembly** consists of 750 representatives: 250 from Earth, 250 from Mars, 250 from the Moon. Representatives are elected by the citizens of their home world and require a nominating recommendation from their planetary government. The Assembly meets in continuous session through the year, with the full body convening twice yearly at halfway points for a status review.

A Chancellor veto may be overridden only by an 80% Assembly vote, followed by review by the USCN Supreme Court. Constitutional amendments require a 75% Assembly vote plus USCN Supreme Court approval, specifically to guard against any amendment disadvantaging a member world or species.

**Flagged, not invented — two unreconciled candidate answers, neither adopted:** the ordinary-legislation majority threshold remains an open placeholder in the source material itself. Two Founder Discovery submissions have since proposed different mechanisms — roughly a 500-of-750 Assembly vote followed by a 2-1 vote among the three planetary presidents, versus a 75% Assembly vote combined with the Chancellor's own vote — and neither has been confirmed over the other. No number is locked here; both remain logged in the Proposal Vault pending a single Founder-confirmed answer.

### Judiciary

The highest judicial authority is the **United Solar System Nations Supreme Court (USCN Supreme Court)** — also referred to informally as the Solar Supreme Court — responsible for:
- Constitutional review.
- Interplanetary legal disputes.
- Appeals from planetary courts, including citizen appeals of planetary rulings believed to be unfair.
- Judicial oversight of emergency powers, and of all trials to ensure they are conducted fairly.
- Protection of Charter rights.

### Emergency Government

Solar System emergencies require approval from the Chancellor, the President of Earth, the President of Mars, and the President of the Moon. Qualifying events include government or world-scale collapse, genocide, and large-scale war. Emergency authority automatically expires after 90 days unless the full Assembly chambers agree to renew it through constitutional procedure — a deliberate check to keep the Chancellor and the presidents from overstepping their power, or overstepping the people's. Planetary rights remain protected and cannot be suspended, even during a declared emergency. Anyone found abusing emergency powers faces impeachment and court-martial by the USCN, with full trial rights, including the right to counsel.

### Presidential Succession

If a planetary president dies or is incapacitated, the Vice President immediately assumes office; if the Vice President is also unavailable, the Secretary of State assumes office next. A new election must be held as quickly as possible, at minimum within roughly three weeks. The USCN may temporarily assist a planet through a leadership crisis to keep its government functioning until succession is resolved.

### Planetary Government

Each inhabited world operates as a Representative Democratic Republic, with a President, Vice President, and Secretary of State, each elected to serve up to three five-year terms. Each retains authority over domestic law and jurisdiction while recognizing USCN authority on interplanetary matters. A planetary leader may be removed through impeachment; the USCN reviews impeachments to guard against the process itself being abused for corrupt ends. If an entire planetary government becomes corrupt, its own citizens may stage a coup with USCN support, and the USCN may act as a temporary caretaker government (see Solar Peace Corps, below) until it is restored.

### Citizenship

Citizenship may be revoked only for the most serious crimes against civilization, including treason, espionage, and conspiracies that threaten governments or entire worlds. Revocation costs a person their voting rights and their right to bear arms, but citizenship can later be restored through a formal process of testing and instruction demonstrating renewed understanding. Dual planetary citizenship is legal, subject to periodic validation. Naturalization is available to non-human species under USCN law (see Alien Civilization, above), subject to additional proof of genuine intent. Family members are not punished for another citizen's crimes, though a family's own honor and standing can suffer reputational and financial consequences from a member's actions.

### Solar Peace Corps

The neutral interplanetary peacekeeping organization of the USCN — part peacekeeper, part military force, empowered to use military-grade force only when genuinely necessary. Its mission is to preserve peace, protect civilian life, defend the constitutional principles of the USCN, and maintain stability throughout the Solar System while remaining politically neutral. It is headquartered alongside the USCN itself, and answers directly to the USCN, which prevents it from overstepping its jurisdiction — it cannot make law or force decisions outside its own authority.

It intervenes in cases of corruption, government collapse, war, and genocide. During a total government collapse specifically, it may act as a planet's temporary caretaker leadership until civilian government is restored; in other crises, it holds a peacekeeping role rather than a governing one. It may detain and question government officials, but returns them to their own government's jurisdiction for prosecution rather than prosecuting them itself. Every officer is expected to uphold honesty, integrity, loyalty, respect, and a people-first ethic — a reputation that has earned it broad public trust and goodwill.

**Creed:** *"To be a peacekeeper means to first know the peace giver."*

### Law & Justice

Every citizen is guaranteed the right to vote, freedom of speech, the right to bear arms, freedom of religion, and the freedom to choose their own relationships, regardless of race or species. Human and non-human citizens are legally equal under USCN law, though enforcement is imperfect in practice — inequality is still sometimes exploited at the margins. Criminal matters are tried first through a planetary world government, with the USCN Supreme Court reviewing to ensure the process was fair; genocide against another species is regarded as the single worst crime in the Solar System. Crimes affecting the whole of civilization — hate crimes, government corruption, planetary destruction, genocide — fall under USCN authority; ordinary crimes such as robbery or murder remain under planetary jurisdiction, except where another species is involved. Citizens may appeal a planetary ruling to the USCN Supreme Court if they believe they were treated unfairly.

### Elections & Political Power

All citizens, of every recognized species, are entitled to vote. Full legal adulthood is reached at 18, but the voting age was lowered to 16 during the ongoing war specifically so soldiers deployed before turning 18 are not deprived of their vote until they return. Military personnel may hold public office only after their service has concluded, to avoid any conflict of interest. Wealthy families — including Light Order and Night Haven — hold enough influence to act as de facto lobbyists (through innovation, endorsements, and positioning their own people into government roles) without holding the legal power to write law themselves. Political parties are legal, though individual members within them are not immune from acting illegally. Lobbying and corporate influence are permitted but regulated, and prohibited from threatening harm or using blackmail to change a vote.

### Diplomatic Relations

Earth, Mars, and the Moon resolve disputes through the USCN's neutral platform, and treaties are negotiated the same way, to ensure every party is heard fairly. If a member world refuses to accept a USCN decision, the matter returns to committee for a revised proposal rather than being forced through. Disputes involving the native alien species are handled by a joint committee representing all parties together until resolved. The greatest political challenge facing the USCN at the start of the story is the Light Order/Night Haven conflict itself, and containing its effects before it spreads to draw in worlds that would otherwise stay uninvolved.

### USCN Motto

*"Many Worlds. One Future."*

**Reconfirmed 2026-07-11:** a later Founder Discovery submission answered this same question with the Solar Peace Corps' own Creed instead — read as a cross-reference mix-up, not an intentional change, and not adopted here. This Motto was independently reconfirmed as an official Canon Confirmation in the directive that authorized this compilation.

### USCN Core Values

Equality, Honor, Integrity, Justice, Compassion, Service, Peace, Cooperation, Hope.

**USCN Charter values** (a related but distinct list, added 2026-07-11): honor, integrity, care, and grace for those in need — compatible with, and a narrower emphasis within, the Core Values above.

### USCN Identity

**Emblem:** a fiery wing set against all three planets, with the sun shining above them.

**Promise to member worlds:** to uphold shared law and ethics so every world's people live in harmonious safety.

**Hoped-for legacy:** to stand, generations from now, as a light of peace, hope, and prosperity.

### Political Philosophy

Equality — between worlds, between species — is the ideal the USCN exists to protect above all else; it will never cross the line into treating any world or species as inferior. Its acknowledged internal weakness is the tension between that ideal and the reality that a true crisis sometimes forces hard, imperfect choices to save a world. Ordinary citizens continue believing in the USCN despite its flaws because it has held to being the one institution that stays neutral and tries to save everyone, not just one side.

### Constitutional Framework

No content beyond what's captured in the sections above (Executive/Legislature/Judiciary/Emergency Government/Citizenship/Elections, and the Core Values and Charter values lists) has been provided for this as a standalone topic. Not invented here; left as an explicit gap pending further Founder detail.
