## Methodology Disclosure (Flagged, Not Hidden)

Total scene count across all 36 episodes, summed directly from each screenplay's own Scene Count field: **175 scenes.** Each was assigned to its governing environment using the scene's own Location field, with two disclosed conventions:
1. Where a scene's Location field names more than one environment as an either/or build-stage choice (e.g., Episode 5: "Mid Zone or Ward Station Seven"), the first-listed option is used for grouping purposes — the underlying either/or itself is already documented in that scene's governing Environment Production Package, not re-litigated here.
2. Where a scene's own Action or Camera Notes explicitly describe cutting between two or more environments within that single scene (e.g., Episode 22 Scene 4's "Intercut: Light Order's briefing continues... Night Haven's review continues"), it is grouped separately under **Cross-Cut/Convergence Sequences**, not assigned to either individual environment, since its storyboard treatment is a distinct technique, not a single-location sequence.

This tally is a grouping convention for reuse-planning purposes, not a claim of shot-level precision — genuine shot decomposition is reserved for actual storyboard production (Phase 6E.2 or later), which this directive explicitly does not authorize.

## Missing Information — Flagged, Not Invented

- **No sequence group has a confirmed shot count.** Per Phase 6E.0's own Shot Authority, individual shots within a scene are traceable only where a scene's own Camera Notes describe a progression (as several episodes' Camera Direction sections do); this inventory counts scenes, not shots, and does not estimate shot counts where none is confirmed.
- **The Ward Station Seven sequence group has no single reusable camera template**, unlike every other group — its 49 scenes span the widest range of dressings and functions in the production (formal review, ceremony, briefing/command, informal), each with its own scene-specific Camera Notes rather than one confirmed general rule. This is disclosed as a genuine open item for Phase 6E.2 or later, not filled in with an invented general template.
- **FPP-TECH-001's (the comm) exact environment dependency is ambiguous** — Episode 7 Scene 5 offers "Ward Station Seven or Mid Zone, informal space" as a build-stage either/or, so the comm's own storyboard treatment cannot yet be assigned to a single sequence group with certainty.
