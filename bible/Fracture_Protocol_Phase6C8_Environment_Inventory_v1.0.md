Folding it in here is consistent with the standing production discipline (already applied throughout Phase 6C/6C.5/6C.6/6C.7) of not itemizing redress/scale variants of an existing asset as new environments.

No screenplay or production package states these are the same physical asset, so they are not merged here — but this is worth surfacing as a possible early narrative seed of her later role, for the Founder's own awareness.

### Coverage Verification

Every Location List entry across all 36 Approved Phase 5B screenplays was mapped to one of the 16 environments above (or, in Episode 4's case, confirmed as already covered by Environment 3's own package, which independently cites Episode 4's exact Location List — "Lower Sector (Scenes 1–2, and 3–5 if selected) / Ward Station Seven (alternate for 3–5)" — as its own establishing source). **Zero location strings remain unaccounted for.** This is a complete inventory of the production's environment landscape as written, not a partial pass.

### Reconciliation Note — Correcting the "~18 Additional Environments" Estimate

Phase 6A's original Master Asset Backlog (and every certification since, through Phase 6C.7) carried forward an explicit placeholder: "~18 additional locations... individually enumerable... not itemized further here to avoid inventing IDs for locations not yet needed." That figure was never derived from a direct count — it was a deliberately conservative, unverified placeholder, flagged as such at the time. Direct enumeration in this phase finds the true number is **6**, not ~18 (or **5** confirmed plus 1 flagged pending clarification). The gap between the estimate and the actual count is explained by two factors: (1) many scenes that could have read as distinct "new" locations are explicitly cited in their own screenplays as redress/scale variants of a single existing asset (the Lower Sector cluster alone accounts for what might have looked like 5–7 separate entries); (2) the original ~18 figure was a precautionary upper-bound estimate, not a count, made before any direct enumeration had been performed. This correction is surfaced plainly, consistent with the standing practice of flagging discrepancies rather than letting an unverified figure persist silently across multiple certifications.

## Report 3 — Updated Environment Certification

**Determination question:** READY FOR PHASE 6D or NOT READY.

## Determination: NOT READY

**What changed:** the single largest open item carried through every certification in this lineage (Phase 6C, 6C.5, 6C.6, 6C.7) — "~18 unidentified environments" — is now fully resolved into a specific, small, enumerated list. This is a material improvement in certainty, even though it does not by itself unblock production.

**What remains:**

1. **Six newly-identified environments (ENV-011 through ENV-016) have no Environment Production Package.** Per this directive's own explicit instruction ("Do not create Production Packages"), none was built in this pass — identification and production-readiness are two distinct steps, and only the first has occurred here. All six carry "Required — Not Yet Produced" status (one additionally flagged for clarification).
2. **ENV-016 (Private Kael/Mira Environment) carries an unresolved ambiguity** — possible duplicate of Environment 4, or a genuinely distinct asset — requiring a direct Founder decision before it can be classified either way.

**What IS certified complete:**
- All 10 previously-named environments remain fully unblocked (6 READY, 4 IN PROGRESS), unchanged from the Phase 6C.7 certification.
- The environment landscape across the entire 36-episode production is now completely and specifically enumerated — 16 total identified environments, zero unaccounted-for location strings, with the prior "~18" placeholder corrected to an actual count.
- No new canon was created; no name was invented; every entry traces directly to its own screenplay's exact language or an assigned temporary ENV-0## identifier where no name exists, exactly as this directive required.
- The Asset Registry and a new standing Environment Inventory document (`documentation/Environment_Inventory.md`) are both updated to reflect the full 16-environment landscape.

**Recommendation, not a self-authorized next step:** a future directive authorizing full Environment Production Packages for ENV-011 through ENV-015 (and a resolution of ENV-016's ambiguity first) would be the natural next step — structurally the same Phase 6C-style build-and-audit work already performed for the 10 named environments, now with a complete, bounded list to work from rather than an open-ended estimate.

Per this directive's own instruction, this NOT READY determination reflects the six environments still lacking Production Packages, not a defect in the identification work itself.
