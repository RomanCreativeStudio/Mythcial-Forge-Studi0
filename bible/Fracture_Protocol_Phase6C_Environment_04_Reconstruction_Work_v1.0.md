## 2. Narrative Purpose

Mira's civilian reconstruction work site — the physical expression of her Locked identity ("rebuilding what conflict destroys, not fighting the conflict itself," Character Bible). Explicitly coded, within the screenplay itself, as the visual and tonal opposite of the season's institutional interiors: Episode 19 states Kael comes here in part because it is somewhere that "wasn't a briefing room." It is the site of Episode 33 ("What She Builds"), her active-role beginning, whose underlying mechanism remains deliberately unspecified per Phase 5A.2 — a fact this package's Environmental Effects field addresses directly (Section 6).
