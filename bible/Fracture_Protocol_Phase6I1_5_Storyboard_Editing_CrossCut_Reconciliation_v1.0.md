## 1. Re-Verification

Each of the three flagged episodes' own Editing Direction (Section 16) and Location List (Section 7) was re-read directly, and FPP-SB-007's own defining fields (`Fracture_Protocol_Phase6E2_Storyboard_Production_Packages_v1.0.md`, lines 100–111) were re-read in full alongside them, rather than relying on Phase 6I.1's own summary.

**FPP-SB-007's own self-defined scope** (Authority and Camera Language fields) is specific, not general: "rapid cross-cutting between institutional spaces," explicitly self-cited from Episode 34's own Camera Notes ("rapid cross-cutting between institutional spaces, mirroring Episode 12's own convergence approach") and Episode 27's Scene 5 (Light Order / Night Haven / Doctrine convergence). Its own Authority line cites governing environments FPP-ENV-001, FPP-ENV-006, FPP-ENV-008, ENV-013, ENV-014, FPP-ENV-003, FPP-ENV-004 "as applicable per episode" — a defined set of institutional/political environments converging *with one another*, not a general "any two-location intercut" category.

**Episode 10** (Location List: "Operation site [ENV-011]," Scenes 1,3,5; "Civilian-support perimeter," Scenes 2,4; "Ward Station Seven — communications context," Scene 5): the confirmed Scene 5 intercut is between a field/crisis-response site (ENV-011) and a communications/coordination context at Ward Station Seven. This is a field-operation ↔ command-center intercut, structurally and narratively distinct from FPP-SB-007's institutional-*convergence* pattern (multiple political/factional bodies cross-cut against each other). ENV-011 is not among FPP-SB-007's own cited governing environments. **Does not match FPP-SB-007's own self-defined scope.**

**Episode 20** (Location List: "Reconstruction-work environment," Scenes 1,3,5a; "Guardian/Kael partnership environment," Scenes 2,4,5b): the confirmed alternation is between two personal/character-driven environments (FPP-ENV-004, FPP-ENV-003), explicitly described in the episode's own Editing Direction as "the two threads," not institutions. Neither environment is a political/factional body. **Does not match FPP-SB-007's own self-defined scope.**

**Episode 23** (Location List: "Populated civilian area [FPP-ENV-010]," Scenes 1,3; "Institutional spaces [reused from Episodes 13–22]," Scenes 2,4,5,6): the confirmed Scenes 1–2 cross-cut is between a civilian area and *a single* unspecified institutional space (which one is not itemized in Episode 23's own Location List) — a two-environment alternation, not a *convergence of multiple institutions with one another* as FPP-SB-007 requires. **Does not match FPP-SB-007's own self-defined scope**, though it is the closest of the three, since one side of its intercut genuinely is an institutional environment.

## 2. Determination

Per the three outcomes named in the directive:

- **"Existing FPP-SB-007 already covers them" — NO**, not as a blanket statement: none of the three episodes' confirmed intercuts match FPP-SB-007's own specific, self-cited "institutional convergence" definition.
- **"A new Cross-Cut Package is required" — NO.** The three episodes' intercuts are not a single shared pattern — Episode 10 is a field-ops/command-center intercut, Episode 20 is a personal two-thread alternation, and Episode 23 is a civilian/single-institution alternation. Building one new package to cover all three would misrepresent them as a shared technique they do not share, which would itself be an invention this directive expressly forbids. Each already has a correctly-governing package (FPP-SB-002; FPP-SB-003 and FPP-SB-004; FPP-SB-011, respectively).
- **"FPP-SB-007 requires amendment" — YES, but narrowly:** FPP-SB-007 itself requires only a **scope-clarification amendment** confirming it correctly excludes these three episodes and citing why, closing the Phase 6I.1 flag. The substantive gap is not in FPP-SB-007 — it is that Episodes 10, 20, and 23's own already-governing packages (FPP-SB-002, FPP-SB-003, FPP-SB-004, FPP-SB-011) had not yet cross-referenced these episodes' own confirmed intercuts in their Transition Rules fields. Each of those four packages therefore also receives a narrow, non-inventive amendment: a Transition Rules cross-reference citing facts already established in each episode's own Approved Location List and Editing Direction (which two locations intercut, in which scenes) — no new shot, camera angle, or storyboard content is added.

**No storyboard content is invented anywhere in this reconciliation.** Every fact cited above and applied in the amendments (Section 3) already exists in an Approved screenplay's own Location List or Editing Direction, or in an Approved Storyboard Production Package's own prior fields.
