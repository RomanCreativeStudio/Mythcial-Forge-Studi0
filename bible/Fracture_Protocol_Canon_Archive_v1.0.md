# THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL — CANON ARCHIVE
## Phase History Alignment

**Classification:** Internal — Canon Index
**Status:** Established 2026-07-11, per Founder Directive ("Canon Archive Reconciliation Directive — Founder Instruction, Phase History Alignment").
**Purpose:** This document performs the alignment and audit the directive requested — tracing each Phase from its original Discovery material, through any Refinement, to its current Final Canon Lock status — so that development material is never confused with locked canon. It does not redefine anything. Where this document and a source document disagree, the source document wins and this one is corrected.
**Distinct from:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Canon_Registry_v1.0.md`, which indexes canon *by entity* (organizations, doctrines, principles). This document indexes canon *by phase history* (discovery → refinement → lock). The two are complementary, not duplicates — the Registry answers "what is X and where is it defined," this document answers "how did Phase Y arrive at its current state, and what in it is still open."
**Governed by:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Master_System_Prompt_v1.2.md` Section 12 (Canon Governance System). No document was rewritten to produce this archive; no new lore was introduced. This is organization and verification only, per the directive's own "Required Action."

## Phase 1A — Foundation Framework

**Original Discovery:** Submitted across "Canon Consolidation — Phase 1A & Phase 1B" and confirmed by "Production Bible Compilation — Phase 1A (Core IP Foundation)," both 2026-07-09. Early material included the Guardian's core description and principle, Kael, Light Order/Night Haven as named factions, the seven Core Themes, and the sci-fi genre/manhwa-storytelling-approach confirmations.

**Refinements:** Four additional Guardian facts (cannot be controlled through force; evaluates by principle, not political affiliation; knowledge exceeds every AI humanity has created; true origins/purpose not fully understood) arrived later as part of the Phase 1B civilization material and were initially held back as out-of-scope, then folded into Phase 1A Section 7 on 2026-07-10 once the same directive was resent and reconfirmed.

**Final Canon:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Phase1A_Production_Bible_v1.0.md` — **Locked.**

**Audit:**
1. **Locked Canon:** Genre (sci-fi, non-negotiable), manhwa/manga as storytelling approach only, seven Core Themes, Light Order vs. Night Haven as the flagship Core Conflict, Kael as protagonist, the Light 98th Contubernium as his stated affiliation, the Guardian's description and Core Principle ("Humanity cannot be led into a future it has not chosen"), "The Fracture Protocol" as the historical-era name.
2. **Refined from earlier versions:** The Guardian's characterization — expanded from a single description/principle to include the four additional facts above, without contradiction.
3. **Contradictions already resolved:** None internal to Phase 1A itself; its own flagged tensions (below) point outward to later phases.
4. **Intentionally open:** (a) The Guardian vs. the Authority System — apparently opposed character, relationship never stated. (b) "The Fracture Protocol" now names three things (franchise title, megacity containment protocol, historical era) with no stated relationship between them. Both are carried forward, unresolved, into every later phase — see "Cross-Phase Open Items" below.
5. **Needs Founder clarification before future storytelling:** Both open items above, if a story ever needs to explicitly stage a scene where the Guardian and Authority System (or the historical-era vs. containment-protocol meanings of "The Fracture Protocol") would otherwise conflict on-screen. Not urgent for material already outlined.

## Phase 1B.1 — World & Civilization Framework

**Original Discovery:** Submitted alongside Phase 1A in "Canon Consolidation — Phase 1A & Phase 1B" (2026-07-09), confirmed by "Production Bible Compilation — Phase 1B (Universe Framework)" (2026-07-10). Introduced humanity's Earth/Mars/Moon civilization and the Human Expansion limit.

**Refinements:** The single largest refinement in the entire project so far — this material directly conflicted with World Bible Section 1's original Locked description of the setting as a single, total megacity ("not a scattered multiverse, not seven kingdoms, one place"). Flagged twice as a genuine contradiction (once with three explicit resolution options offered, once in Phase 1A's own "not yet resolved" section) before three consecutive Founder directives built on the interplanetary framework without retracting it. Resolved by a disclosed edit to World Bible Section 1 (v1.1 → v1.2, 2026-07-10): the megacity remains the entirety of Kael's Season One story and Authority's local control is unchanged; only the universe's *stated outer scope* expanded.

**Final Canon:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Phase1B_Production_Bible_v1.0.md` (Phase 1B.1 section) + `bible/Fracture_Protocol_World_Bible_v1.1.md` Section 1 (now at v1.3) — **Locked.**

**Audit:**
1. **Locked Canon:** Human civilization spans Earth (historical home), Mars (economic/industrial center), the Moon (medicine/science center); Human Expansion has not gone beyond the Solar System (political instability, war, technological limitations); the megacity exists within, not as, this wider civilization.
2. **Refined from earlier versions:** The universe's stated scope — from "one place, full stop" to "one place the story occupies, inside a larger civilization." The *story* did not change; the *stated boundary of the universe* did.
3. **Contradictions already resolved:** The single-megacity-vs-interplanetary-civilization conflict (see Refinements above) — the project's clearest example of a Major Canon Update correctly slowed down, flagged twice, and only applied once the Founder's sustained direction across multiple directives made intent unambiguous.
4. **Intentionally open:** Population figures, FTL travel mechanism (its *absence/limitation* is confirmed; the mechanism itself is not), and the existence/nature of alien civilizations (implied by the USCN citizenship framework's non-human naturalization clause, never directly described). None invented.
5. **Needs Founder clarification before future storytelling:** Any story material that would require a specific population figure, a stated FTL mechanism, or an on-screen alien character.

## Phase 1B.3 — Technology & Military Framework

**Original Discovery:** "Founder Canon Lock Directive — Phase 1B.3, Technology & Military Framework" (2026-07-10). Introduced Technology, Energy, AI, Guardian elaboration, Military, Helldiver Corps, Light 98th Contubernium structure, Ward Division, Warfare, Communications, and Final Philosophy canon.

**Refinements:** Ward Division was the one genuine contradiction in this phase — the submitted definition redefined an already-Locked institution's affiliation (Authority-local branch → Helldiver-connected foundation), purpose (Lower Sector containment → interplanetary civilization preservation), and Kael's rank (Cadet → "Vanguard Commander," a title absent from the existing rank ladder), with no reconciling explanation. Per the directive's own "do not alter existing organizations" instruction, this section was held out of the initial lock and reported rather than implemented. Resolved 2026-07-10 via the Founder's "Discovery Questionnaire — Phase 1B.3, Ward Division Canon Reconciliation": the conflict resolves by evolution, not replacement — the original containment mission stayed true and expanded into preservation; the Ward Division and Light 98th remain separate, operationally-partnered organizations; Kael starts as Cadet and earns Vanguard Commander later. Held at "Approved — Pending Final Lock" (honoring that questionnaire's own literal status field) until the Founder's "Phase 1B.3 — Ward Division Reconciliation Final Lock" directive (2026-07-11) applied it in full to World Bible Section 6 (v1.2 → v1.3) and locked it.

**Final Canon:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Phase1B3_Production_Bible_v1.0.md` + `bible/Fracture_Protocol_World_Bible_v1.1.md` Section 6 (now at v1.3) — **Locked, no sections pending.**

**Audit:**
1. **Locked Canon:** Technology philosophy ("Advancement means futuristic living, but evolution requires responsibility"); Energy (fusion-based, USCN-regulated); AI governance (advises, never controls elections/legislation/economics/healthcare/HR/critical decisions); the Guardian as sworn protector of the Solar System, commanded by no one ("Guidance builds civilization. Control destroys it."); Military doctrine (Constitution + USCN Charter + civilian-life-first); Helldiver Corps (36 Legions, 7 elite formations); Light 98th Contubernium (one of the 7, rapid-response, "They are not defined by the battles they win..."); Ward Division (Authority-origin, expanded into civilization preservation, separate-but-partnered with the Light 98th, Kael's starting institution); Warfare governance (civilian protection, no unnecessary-destruction weapons); Communications (CNAS/GHNS/MHNS); Final Philosophy ("Power without principle fractures civilizations; principle gives power a future").
2. **Refined from earlier versions:** Ward Division, in full (see Refinements above) — the clearest example in this phase of an evolution-not-replacement resolution.
3. **Contradictions already resolved:** The Ward Division institutional-identity/Kael's-rank contradiction — flagged 2026-07-10, reconciled 2026-07-10, finally locked 2026-07-11.
4. **Intentionally open:** Exactly how "Vanguard Commander" fits above the existing four-rank ladder (a tier above Division Director, a parallel honorific, or something else) — noted as minor and non-blocking, since it doesn't affect Kael's confirmed Season One starting state. The AI-governance principle's relationship to the Authority System's existing described behavior is also flagged as related-to, but distinct from, the Guardian/Authority tension carried from Phase 1A.
5. **Needs Founder clarification before future storytelling:** The Vanguard Commander rank placement, only once a story reaches that point in Kael's arc.

## Cross-Phase Open Items

Two items recur across multiple phases and are tracked once here rather than repeated per-phase:

1. **The Guardian vs. the Authority System.** First flagged Phase 1A (Section 7); referenced again in Phase 1B.3 (Sections 3 and 4) without resolution. The Guardian "does not rule," "cannot be controlled by any government or organization," and "refuses to force humanity's choices." The Authority System "cannot be removed, destroyed, or ignored" and treats every resident as a registered digital identity under its control. No directive received so far states whether these are the same entity at different scales, two distinct AIs, or a deliberate thematic contrast. **Still open.**
2. **"The Fracture Protocol" — three referents.** Franchise title (Master System Prompt), megacity containment protocol (World Bible Section 2), and interplanetary historical era (Phase 1A Section 6). No stated relationship between the three. **Still open.**

Neither item blocks any current Lock — both are carried forward as known, deliberately unresolved scaffolding, consistent with how the World Bible already treats other permanent ambiguities (e.g., Section 2's founding-event silence).
