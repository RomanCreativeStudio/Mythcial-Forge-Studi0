## 2. Determination

**Neither a uniform "discipline closure state" nor an entirely new discipline applies. The correct determination is: one already-scoped, already-authorized phase remains open — Phase 6D.2, Prop & Technology Production Packages.**

This is not an invented phase. It is the direct structural continuation of a pattern already established and Founder-Approved four separate times in this production (Storyboard 6E.2, Animation 6F.2, Audio 6G.2, Visual Effects 6H.2) — in every one of those cases, a discipline's Inventory phase (X.1) was followed by a Production Packages phase (X.2) building one governance/reference package per confirmed asset or group, regardless of how few assets existed (Visual Effects proceeded to 6H.2 with only 2 confirmed assets). Props & Technology confirmed exactly 1 asset at Phase 6D.1 (FPP-TECH-001) and validated that finding at Phase 6D.1.5, but was never carried the one remaining step every parallel discipline received.

No other undefined phase (no "Phase 6D.2" content, no new discipline, no Phase 7) is created or invented by this determination — this document identifies the gap and recommends the next phase; it does not build it.
