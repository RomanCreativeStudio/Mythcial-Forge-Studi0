No Locked source authorizes any specific rank-marker or insignia design — Phase 6B.3 §2 states this explicitly and directly: *"No specific rank-marker or insignia design is Locked anywhere... its exact visual form is an open concept-art item, not invented here."* This is the same category of violation already found and rejected twice in this production (the "VEYR"/"VEX"/chest-emblem images) — an unauthorized design invented into a space the Founder has not yet resolved. |
| **Boot markings** | **Flagged, moderate confidence.** Small triangle-shaped cyan marks appear on both boots. Given the shoulder emblem's own triangular/diamond geometry, this reads as a plausible recurrence of the same unauthorized mark rather than an unrelated coincidence — flagged for removal alongside the shoulder emblem rather than treated as a separate, independent finding. |
