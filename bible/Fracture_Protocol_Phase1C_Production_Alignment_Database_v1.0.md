### Section 1 — Identity Alignment

**Full Name:** Kael Aurelian Veyr

**Birthplace:** Mars — one of humanity's oldest sites of human-alien cooperation, consistent with the already-Locked Alien Civilization material (Phase 1B.1). Confirmed per Founder decision (Canon Alignment Patch v1.1).

**Relocation History:** Relocated from Mars to the megacity at a young age, growing up in the Lower Sector before the Breach that orphaned him. This is the explicit bridging fact required to hold his Mars birth and his Locked orphaning together — Breaches are Locked to the megacity's Lower Sector specifically (World Bible §10) — and it preserves all three facts simultaneously: Mars birthplace, Lower Sector childhood, Breach orphan origin.

**Current Location:** Ward Station Seven, Lower Sector.

**Age at Story Beginning:** 19 — within the already-Locked visual/identity range (17–20, Master Prompt §4).

**Civilization:** Humanity / United Solar System Nations sphere.

### Section 2 — Authority Identity Status

**Registration Status:** Registered human citizen.

**Authority Classification:** Citizen with recognized civilian identity records. Kael is not born into Authority leadership — his importance comes from his choices, his actions, his connection to the Light 98th, and his relationship to Titan's legacy, not from any inherited status.

**Political Status:** Not a political authority. He does not begin as a commander, a ruler, or a symbol. He begins as a person attempting to earn the right to carry responsibility.

**Military Classification:** Cadet, Ward Division. *(Corrected from the submitted "Helldiver Candidate" — see "Not Applied," below.)*

**Unit Affiliation:** Light 98th Contubernium — a separate, simultaneous affiliation, not a rank or classification within the Helldiver Corps itself (Phase 1B.3 §8, Final-Locked).

**Institutional Relationship:** Kael's Ward Division Cadet rank and his Light 98th affiliation are both Season One facts held at once, not sequential. He sits inside the same three-institution structure already defined for the Light 98th itself (Faction Bible): Light Order (legacy, responsibility, technology, civilization protection — cultural/political, not his chain of command), the Helldiver Corps (training, military discipline, combat operations — the Light 98th's actual operational command), and the Ward Division (civilian protection, local defense, the people he fights for — his own actual rank ladder).

### Section 5 — Character Engine

**Core Want:** To protect people from the forces threatening humanity's future — and beneath that, to prove that power can exist without losing compassion.

**Core Need:** To learn that protection doesn't mean carrying every burden alone. A true guardian doesn't stand above humanity — a true guardian stands with it.

**Greatest Strength:** Responsibility — he accepts the weight of decisions others avoid, combined with compassion, discipline, courage, and empathy.

**Greatest Flaw:** The Burden Complex — he believes responsibility means personally carrying every failure, every loss, and every impossible decision.

**Cost Already Paid:** A normal childhood, personal freedom, emotional simplicity, and the ability to view conflict from a distance. His greatest cost: he learned too early that protecting people means sometimes losing parts of yourself.

### Section 6 — Fracture Classification

**Fracture Anomaly Status:** Confirmed — **fracture anomaly subject**, the sole core/main fracture anomaly subject in this franchise (World Bible §5; Master Prompt System Rule 4). *(Corrected from the submitted "Not a Fracture anomaly," which directly reversed this Absolute-tier Locked fact — see "Not Applied," below.)*

**Relationship with Fractures:** His classification is not his identity. He is defined by his response to being a fracture anomaly subject, not reduced to it — his significance comes from choice, not destiny, the same standard held throughout his own Character Bible entry.

His story question: can humanity be protected without humanity losing itself?

### Not Applied — Flagged Rather Than Silently Corrected or Silently Accepted

Two fields were corrected, not transcribed as submitted:

- **"Fracture Anomaly Status: Not a Fracture anomaly"** — this reverses System Rule 4 and World Bible §5, the single most foundational fact about Kael's character in the entire franchise, and directly contradicts this same document's own Section 1 ("Breach orphan origin"). Read as a drafting error rather than an intended reversal, since nothing else in this submission argues for or acknowledges such a fundamental change. Corrected to the already-Locked classification; the thematic point underneath it ("defined by response, not by classification") was preserved, since it's a good point and doesn't depend on the field being wrong.
- **"Military Classification: Helldiver Candidate"** — the same framing excluded four times previously this session (Phase 1C.2's original submission, Titan's "Helldiver training system" detail, the "Rule 3" alignment-principle flag, and now here) because it conflicts with the Final-Locked Cadet-of-Ward-Division starting rank (Phase 1B.3 §8). Corrected to "Cadet, Ward Division," consistent with the rest of this same document's own Classification header, which already separately lists "Operational Association: Ward Division."

One field held, not written in either direction:

- **"Political Connection: Light Order Legacy Line"** — new; nothing previously compiled ties Kael's own family to Light Order specifically, and it sits close to softening the deliberately-built "Kael comes from nothing, Cassian comes from everything" contrast (Character Bible, Cassian's "Contrast with Kael"). Not accepted, not rejected — flagged for one direct answer before it's written in either way.
