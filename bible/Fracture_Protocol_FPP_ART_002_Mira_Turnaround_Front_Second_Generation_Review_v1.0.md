## Step 3 — Determination

**REVISION REQUIRED — SEVERE.** Three independent, critical failures are present, each sufficient on its own to reject this generation outright, and together strongly indicating this image was produced by a different, unvetted external tool rather than the established generation workflow:

1. **A visible "DeeVid AI" watermark is baked into the image** — an explicit, direct Forbidden Elements violation, and the same specific failure already encountered and rejected once during `FPP-ART-001`'s own production history.
2. **The rendering register is flat cel-shaded / vector illustration, not the Approved painterly semi-realistic anime cinematic hybrid** — a wholesale style mismatch, not a fixable detail, and again a repeat of a previously-rejected failure mode.
3. **The image is not full-body** — it is cropped at approximately upper-thigh height, with ankles, cuffs, and feet entirely absent, violating the Front View Modifier's and this asset's own brief's explicit "feet completely visible" / full-body requirement outright.

In addition, several construction deviations from the Locked reference persist or reappear:

4. **Belt buckle is tactical-style (black plastic side-release)**, not the reference's plain rectangular leather buckle — a regression of an issue already corrected once in `FPP-ART-001`'s own history.
5. **Jacket sleeves are long/cuffed to the wrist, not rolled** — a new construction deviation from the reference.
6. **Trouser color remains grayish/olive-leaning**, not the reference's warm neutral brown — unresolved from the first FPP-ART-002 candidate.
7. **Undershirt color/construction does not clearly match** the reference's plain gray-tan crew-neck.

Because the frame is incomplete, trouser cuff construction cannot be assessed at all in this submission — a full-body resubmission is required before that specific item can even be evaluated.
