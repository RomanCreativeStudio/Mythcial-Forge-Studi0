### D. Music Groups

Identified by cross-referencing every screenplay's own Music Direction section — the single most consistent, most-repeated instruction across the entire 36-episode corpus is a **restraint mandate**: no swelling score, no triumphant cue, no resolving cue, present in the Music Direction of the overwhelming majority of episodes checked directly. This is recorded here as the production's own de facto baseline register, not invented — it is the literal, repeated language of the screenplays themselves.

| Music Group | Episodes | Confirmed Character |
|---|---|---|
| Series-wide restraint baseline | Nearly all 36 episodes | "No swell," "no triumphant cue," "no resolving cue" — confirmed directly and repeatedly (Episodes 4, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, and others already cited in Phase 6F.1 research). |
| Crisis-escalation register | 2, 9, 10, 11, 23 | "Urgent but not chaotic," sustaining dread without resolving into relief; Episode 9 explicitly echoes Episode 2's "musical shape without repeating it." |
| Warm/intimate register | 5, 6, 7 (first half), 19, 26 | "Restrained and warm," "genuinely warm and earned" — the production's only confirmed instances of unambiguous positive emotional payoff in score. |
| Cassian's five-stage escalating register | 28, 29, 30, 31, 35 | Progresses from "quiet, unsettled, unresolved" (28) through "at its most exposed register for this character so far" (31) to "earned, weighted resolution" (35) — a confirmed, structured musical arc paralleling his animation schedule (FPP-ANIM-003). |
| Institutional convergence/cross-cut register | 12, 22, 27, 34 | "Mounting institutional tension throughout, unresolved at the close" — shared across every cross-cut sequence (FPP-SB-007). |
| Finale resolution register | 35, 36 | "Building... arriving at earned, weighted resolution" (35); "settled, earned, quietly hopeful... without triumphant simplicity" (36) — the production's only sanctioned full resolution, reserved for the finale alone. |
