## 3. Rendering Register

Semi-realistic anime cinematic hybrid — the single register governing every character in this production, Mira included, with no fidelity carve-out. Detailed, cinematically lit, high-detail facial focus. Never flat cel-shading, speed lines, chibi proportion breaks, or single-tone eye highlights (automatic QA failure on violation). *(`forbidden-elements.md`; `visual-development-guide.md`; Phase 6B.2 item 69's "applies to all of the above" rule.)*

---

## 4. Color Language

Warm, practical earth tones and work-wear neutrals dominate her entire visual identity — materially distinct from both Kael's muted-practical palette (grays, worn neutrals, muted non-cyan blues, earth tones, plus subtle cyan accents) and Cassian's cool formal palette. Cyan/icy-blue is never used anywhere in her design — that color is reserved exclusively to Kael's eye-glow and other Signal-linked phenomena (Cipher-work, Fragment apparitions, Breach residue) and "never used decoratively." No artificial glow, no Signal association, of any kind. *(Phase 6B.2 item 16; `color-language.md` palette table.)*

---

## 5. Forbidden Elements

**Universal blacklist** (`forbidden-elements.md`, applies to every character, Mira included): no fantasy-magic signifiers; no copied/reskinned existing published IP; no costless or infinite-looking system/particle effects; no modern real-world brand logos or real-world alphabet signage; no gratuitous or exploitative violence.

**Mira-specific Do Not Do rules** (Phase 6B.2 item 20; Phase 6B.3 §3; Visual Identity Spec §1F): never glamorize or combat-stylize her design; never assign her a Signal/eye-glow system — that remains exclusively Kael's; never render her hands as clean or unmarked — that would erase her single strongest distinguishing feature; never use the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue anywhere in her design; never reduce her Spanish heritage to a single narrow stereotype; no military/tactical redesign; no Authority System markings, faction symbols, logos, insignia, or branding of any kind.

---

## 6. Character Summary

Mira Solenne Valeris — a civilian reconstruction specialist, age 20, raised in the Moon's civilian districts, now resident in the megacity's Mid Zone; of Spanish heritage. Design philosophy: "quiet competence and warmth — a builder, not a fighter," deliberately contrasting with Kael's military practicality and Cassian's inherited polish. She was displaced when an Ascendant Doctrine occupation offered her home protection only in exchange for submission — the Light 98th arrived too late to stop it. Her internal conflict is rebuilding a life and deciding whether to trust a system, and a person, that arrived too late; she exists in this canon on her own terms, not only in relation to Kael. Her hands — visibly marked by hands-on reconstruction work — are her single strongest distinguishing visual feature, the direct equivalent of Kael's eye-glow: the one feature that visually encodes her entire identity. *(Character Bible v1.17; Phase 6B.2 items 1–2, 10, 15.)*
