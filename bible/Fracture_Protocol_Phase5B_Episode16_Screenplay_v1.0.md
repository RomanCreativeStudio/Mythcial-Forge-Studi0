### SCENE 1 — AN ARRANGED MEETING

- **Scene Number:** 1
- **Scene Title:** An Arranged Meeting
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Open on Cassian's first direct contact with Kael.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Guarded, formal, without hostility.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue establishing Cassian's total composure immediately.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Episode 15's closing transition; Cassian's first personal appearance in the story.

**ACTION**

The room is formal, neutral, arranged rather than ambushed — consistent with Cassian's standing as a former USCN Chief Strategic Futures Director rather than a fugitive. Kael enters to find Cassian already seated, composed, unhurried.

**DIALOGUE**

CASSIAN
*(rising, without theater)*
Thank you for coming. I know that wasn't a simple decision.

KAEL
*(guarded, professional)*
You arranged it easily enough.

CASSIAN
I've never hidden. I've only been unintroduced.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Cassian must not read as a monster — the tension is entirely in who he turns out to be: a person, not a myth. No aggression, no theatrical menace.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Formal, composed framing establishing both men at eye level — no visual coding tilts either man as dominant.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Controlled, composed, formal — per `lighting-system.md`.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet, formal ambience.

**TRANSITION**

Cut, continuous scene, into Scene 2.

### SCENE 2 — WHAT HE BELIEVES

- **Scene Number:** 2
- **Scene Title:** What He Believes
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Deliver Cassian's core philosophy directly.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Total conviction, no visible doubt; Kael genuinely engaged.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue carrying his belief sincerely and coherently.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Cassian's core belief drawn verbatim/near-verbatim from Character Bible, "Personal Philosophy."

**ACTION**

Cassian speaks plainly, without performance, laying out the belief that governs everything he's built.

**DIALOGUE**

CASSIAN
Survival has to come before principle. Sacrifice is justified, if it means we continue. I know how that sounds. I've made peace with how it sounds.

KAEL
*(not dismissive, testing)*
That's a belief that costs other people, not you.

CASSIAN
*(steady)*
It's cost me plenty. But you're right to press on it. That's exactly why I'm here instead of somewhere you'd never find me.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Per the seed package's explicit instruction, this must never simplify into cruelty or a strawman position — Cassian is genuinely persuasive, not theatrically villainous. Kael listens rather than reacts.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Tighter framing on Cassian as he speaks, holding on Kael's genuine attention rather than cutting away.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Controlled, formal, unchanged from Scene 1.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet, focused ambience; no score sting framing Cassian as villainous.

**TRANSITION**

Cut, continuous scene, into Scene 3.

### SCENE 3 — RESPECT WITHOUT AGREEMENT

- **Scene Number:** 3
- **Scene Title:** Respect Without Agreement
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Show Cassian's relationship to the Guardian's philosophy without the Guardian appearing.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Genuine, unforced respect coexisting with firm disagreement; Kael surprised.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue carrying both respect and disagreement with equal sincerity.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** No Guardian appearance or new characterization; drawn from Character Bible, "Personal Philosophy" and "Relationship with the Guardian."

**ACTION**

Cassian's tone shifts — not softer, but more careful, as he speaks of the Guardian.

**DIALOGUE**

CASSIAN
I worked alongside it once, before any of this. I still think it's one of the greatest protectors humanity has ever known.

KAEL
*(surprised)*
That's not what I expected you to say about it.

CASSIAN
Respecting something isn't the same as agreeing with it. It watches. It waits for us to choose. I call that a form of abandonment. It would call it faith. We've never resolved which of us is right.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Neither the respect nor the disagreement may undercut the other — both must land as equally sincere. No new Guardian origin, purpose, or destiny framing beyond Cassian's own already-Locked stated view.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Held two-shot, no cutaway visual reference to the Guardian itself.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Unchanged — controlled, formal.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet ambience, no shift signaling villainy or heroism to either position.

**TRANSITION**

Cut, continuous scene, into Scene 4.

### SCENE 4 — MORAL LIMITS

- **Scene Number:** 4
- **Scene Title:** Moral Limits
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Establish Cassian's boundaries, keeping him a genuine philosophical antagonist rather than a simple villain.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Firm, unwavering conviction; Kael probing for the line.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue making the limits feel like genuine conviction, not performance.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Moral limits drawn verbatim/near-verbatim from Character Bible, "Who He Is" and "Personal Philosophy."

**ACTION**

Kael presses. Cassian answers without flinching, and without softening.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
Where does it end, then? What wouldn't you do?

CASSIAN
I will never act from personal power. Not revenge, not cruelty — I have no use for either. I will never lie to people about my reasons. And I would not accept the end of humanity's capacity to choose for itself. If I'm wrong about all this, I'll be the one who dismantles it.

KAEL
*(quiet)*
You sound certain you won't be.

CASSIAN
I sound certain because I am. That's not the same as being right.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

These limits must read as genuine conviction, not a performance for Kael's benefit — firm, unwavering, but conceding real stakes rather than dismissing them.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Steady, unmoving framing on Cassian's stillness as he states his limits.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Graver, more formal, without becoming villainous or dramatic.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet, weighted ambience.

**TRANSITION**

Cut, continuous scene, into Scene 5.

### SCENE 5 — WHAT KAEL SEES

- **Scene Number:** 5
- **Scene Title:** What Kael Sees
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Late afternoon
- **Story Purpose:** Render the Contrast with Kael and the Kael/Cassian relationship textually; let Cassian's private fear surface as subtext.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Uncomfortable recognition (Kael); private, unstated cost beneath composure (Cassian).
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** Dialogue stays in philosophy and history; the private fear is carried by performance, not text.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Drawn from Character Bible, "Contrast with Kael" and "Kael & Cassian" (Relationship Dynamics); Cassian's private fear never confessed aloud.

**ACTION**

The conversation turns personal without becoming intimate. Kael studies Cassian and finds something uncomfortably familiar rather than purely alien. Cassian, for one unguarded beat, lets something pass behind his composure — never named, never spoken.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
You lost people. That's where this started for you.

CASSIAN
*(measured)*
Everyone in this conflict lost people. The difference is what we each decided to do about it.

KAEL
I decided people are worth protecting even when they choose wrong. You decided they can't be trusted to choose at all.

CASSIAN
*(a beat, not quite an admission)*
I decided the cost of trusting them was one I'd already paid once. I wasn't willing to pay it twice.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

*(Subtext, not dialogue — for the performer and director only, per the seed package's own explicit scope: Cassian's unspoken private fear is not that Kael will fail, but that Kael will succeed — because that would mean Cassian's life's work was built on grief rather than truth. Nothing above states this outright; it must be carried entirely through a visual or performance beat suggesting his composure has a private cost.)* Kael's recognition here draws on his own Locked fear of failing the people counting on him, carried further than he has let his own go — this should read on Kael's face as discomfort, not agreement.

**CAMERA NOTES**

A held close-up on Cassian in the beat after his last line — long enough for the audience to sense the private cost without any line naming it.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Late-afternoon light beginning to shift, without symbolic overstatement.

**SOUND NOTES**

Ambience drops slightly under the held beat — no score cue naming the emotion.

**TRANSITION**

Cut, continuous scene, into Scene 6.

### SCENE 6 — THE CHALLENGE

- **Scene Number:** 6
- **Scene Title:** The Challenge
- **INT./EXT.:** INT.
- **Location:** Formal meeting environment
- **Time of Day:** Evening
- **Story Purpose:** Close the episode on Cassian's direct challenge to Kael and the knowledge, not resolution, that results.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Serious, unresolved; parting without hostility, without agreement.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; Cassian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Cassian; formal meeting environment.
- **Required Audio:** No resolving musical cue — the question left audibly open.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Closes directly into Episode 17's own Beginning State; no final lesson delivered; no Arc Three material.

**ACTION**

Both men rise. Nothing here resolves. Cassian delivers his closing challenge, echoing the same question he has already asked the Guardian.

**DIALOGUE**

CASSIAN
Before you go — one question. Is restraint really faith in people? Or is it fear of becoming the thing you were made to stop?

KAEL
*(steady, not triumphant)*
Power exists to protect people's freedom to choose. Not to make sure they choose right. I'd rather risk that than take the choice away.

CASSIAN
*(not conceding, not dismissing)*
Ask me again after it costs you something.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Both men part serious and unresolved — no hostility, no agreement, no final lesson delivered. Kael's answer is his own Locked leadership philosophy offered as answer, not victory. Nothing here shows Cassian shaken or doubting; his stage-two total conviction remains fully intact.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Wide, formal framing as the two men separate — no visual suggesting either has won.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Evening light settling, consistent with the room's earlier formality.

**SOUND NOTES**

No resolving musical cue — ambience simply falls away.

**TRANSITION**

Hold on the empty room after both men have gone, then cut — directly into Episode 17's own Beginning State (Cassian's origin, the Sol Collapse).
