- **Climax:** Kael's flaw and synthesis resolving together; Cassian's self-dismantling delivered in full, using the Locked language exactly.
- **Closing emotional beat:** The saga's central conflicts resolve as Phase 2B's own "Which conflicts are resolved" specifies: Kael's personal flaw (balance over control), Cassian's certainty (his final break and self-dismantling), the Guardian's intervention-timing disagreement (already landed together, now fully realized), and Mira's trust (her civilian-reconstruction identity fully central, not adjacent).
