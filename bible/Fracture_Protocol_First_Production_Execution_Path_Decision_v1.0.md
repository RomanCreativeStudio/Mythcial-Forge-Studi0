## Selected Path

**Option B — Concept Art Execution.**

## Reasoning

Concept Art rates highest on every criterion evaluated except canon risk, where it is a well-bounded, already-disclosed moderate risk rather than a blocker. It is the literal next real step in the Production Authority Hierarchy's own order, the most production-ready of the three options (closed subject list, extensive Locked visual canon already available to cite), carries the lowest rework risk of the three (foundational work every later stage requires regardless), and — unlike Casting — can actually be executed within this production environment, as this session has already directly demonstrated with prior visual-asset work (the Brand Asset Production line). Master Release Governance (Option A) is rejected for now as premature: it has no downstream dependency to unlock, the weakest existing support of the three, and the highest risk of requiring substantial rework once real Final Edit/Master Release conditions exist. Casting Execution (Option C) remains a legitimate, well-supported parallel track, but is not selected as the *first* path because it cannot actually be executed inside this environment and does not advance the primary visual-production critical path the way Concept Art does.

This selection authorizes a **direction** only. No execution has begun, no asset has been created, and no specific asset (which character, environment, prop, or VFX asset to begin with) has been selected — that remains for the required next directive.

## Required Next Directive

A new Founder Directive is required to name the specific first Concept Art subject (one character, one environment, the confirmed prop, or one of the two confirmed VFX assets) and authorize its actual production, per Phase 6K.0's own Concept Art Authority, Visual Design Constraint Authority, and Approval Authority. Casting Execution and Phase 6M.0 Master Release Governance remain available as future Founder-directed paths; neither is foreclosed by this decision.
