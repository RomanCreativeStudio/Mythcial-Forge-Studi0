## 9. Full Screenplay

### SCENE 1 — WHAT PEOPLE SAY

- **Scene Number:** 1
- **Scene Title:** What People Say
- **INT./EXT.:** EXT.
- **Location:** Lower Sector, ordinary civilian space
- **Time of Day:** Midday
- **Story Purpose:** Establish the Guardian as ambient civilizational presence before any personal contact.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Ordinary, unremarkable.
- **Characters Present:** Kael; unnamed civilians.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; Lower Sector.
- **Required Audio:** Ambient dialogue carrying reverence and mild unease in equal measure.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Opens directly from Episode 3's Scene 5.

**ACTION**

Ordinary Lower Sector life. Kael moves through it, still carrying the quiet aftermath of Episode 3's uncomplicated pride. Around him, fragments of conversation — civilians speaking of the Guardian the way people speak of something larger than any government, half-reverent, half-uneasy.

**DIALOGUE**

CIVILIAN 1 (background, passing)
—said it was seen near the old transit line again. Just standing there.

CIVILIAN 2 (background)
It's always "just standing there." Nobody knows what that means.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Kael registers this only peripherally — the Guardian is, for him right now, still an abstraction like it is for everyone else in the scene.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Wide, ambient framing — the Guardian is talked about, not shown.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Ordinary midday Lower Sector light, per `lighting-system.md`.

**SOUND NOTES**

Layered ambient conversation, no single voice dominant.

**TRANSITION**

Cut as Kael turns a corner — and stops.

### SCENE 2 — PRESENCE

- **Scene Number:** 2
- **Scene Title:** Presence
- **INT./EXT.:** EXT.
- **Location:** Lower Sector
- **Time of Day:** Midday
- **Story Purpose:** Make the Guardian concrete for the first time this season.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Kael startled, wary; the Guardian calm, unhurried.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, the Guardian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; the Guardian (first build).
- **Required Audio:** Minimal dialogue; the Guardian's first words carry calm directness, not exposition about itself.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 1.

**ACTION**

The Guardian, simply present — no dramatic entrance, no effect announcing it. It is there the way a landmark is there. Kael stops short, genuinely caught off guard.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
*(startled, recovering fast)*
I didn't expect—

THE GUARDIAN
*(calm, direct, no preamble)*
Most people don't. I try not to make an announcement of it.

KAEL
*(wary, polite)*
Is there something you need?

THE GUARDIAN
Not from you. I came to see how you were.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

The Guardian's directness should feel disarming precisely because it offers no exposition about itself — it simply states its purpose in being here, nothing more. No line hints at where it came from or what it is.

**CAMERA NOTES**

A held two-shot once the Guardian speaks — no cutting away to "reveal" it dramatically; its presence is simply established, not unveiled.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Same Lower Sector light as Scene 1, unshifted — the Guardian's presence is not marked by any lighting change.

**SOUND NOTES**

Ambient recedes as the two speak; no musical sting announces the Guardian.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to the conversation continuing, more privately.

### SCENE 3 — RESISTANCE

- **Scene Number:** 3
- **Scene Title:** Resistance
- **INT./EXT.:** Per production selection
- **Location:** Lower Sector or Ward Station Seven
- **Time of Day:** Same day
- **Story Purpose:** Dramatize Kael's instinct to deflect what happened rather than be examined.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Kael defensive, minimizing; the Guardian patient.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, the Guardian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; the Guardian.
- **Required Audio:** Kael's deflection against the Guardian's calm refusal to accept it.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 2.

**ACTION**

Kael, given room to explain himself, chooses instead to minimize. The Guardian doesn't press — just doesn't let the deflection close the conversation either.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
It went the way it needed to. Everyone's safe. That's the job.

THE GUARDIAN
*(no judgment, simply not moving on)*
That's what happened. I asked how you were.

KAEL
*(a beat, guarded)*
I'm fine.

THE GUARDIAN
*(patient, unhurried)*
You could tell me that and it wouldn't cost you anything. I'd still be here either way.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

The Guardian never accuses, never pushes past a gentle refusal to be deflected — its patience itself is the pressure, not confrontation.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Tighter than Scene 2 — the conversation narrowing toward something more personal.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Consistent with the scene's chosen location's established register.

**SOUND NOTES**

Quiet, focused — no ambient distraction competing with the exchange.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to the conversation's turning point.

### SCENE 4 — RECOGNITION

- **Scene Number:** 4
- **Scene Title:** Recognition
- **INT./EXT.:** Same as Scene 3
- **Location:** Same as Scene 3
- **Time of Day:** Continuous
- **Story Purpose:** Land the episode's central beat.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Kael's first real crack in isolation; the Guardian steady, without triumph or pity.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, the Guardian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; the Guardian.
- **Required Audio:** The Guardian names the pattern without any origin-adjacent reference; recognition framed as principle-based only.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Direct continuation from Scene 3.

**ACTION**

A quieter, closer framing than the two prior scenes. The Guardian, without pressing further, simply names what it's actually seen — not the outcome, the pattern underneath it.

**DIALOGUE**

THE GUARDIAN
*(quiet, direct)*
You didn't wait for backup two days ago. You're doing the same thing right now, in a smaller way — deciding on your own that this is something you carry alone.

KAEL
*(genuinely caught, a beat of real silence)*
...That's not—
*(stops himself; doesn't finish the denial)*

THE GUARDIAN
I'm not telling you that to correct you. I noticed it, and it mattered enough to come find you.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

This is the episode's central beat — Kael's unfinished denial is the crack, not a confession. He does not have a realization or a resolution here; he simply stops defending himself for one moment. The Guardian's "it mattered enough to come find you" is the full extent of the causal-outreach motivation being stated; nothing further about why it noticed, or what that noticing means about it, is ever said.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Closest framing of the episode — per `cinematic-mood-guide.md`, this is the scene that earns intimacy.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Quieter, more focused treatment than Scenes 1–3, per the seed package's own note.

**SOUND NOTES**

Near-silence around the dialogue — the words should carry the full weight.

**TRANSITION**

Cut to the conversation's close.

### SCENE 5 — THE QUESTION

- **Scene Number:** 5
- **Scene Title:** The Question
- **INT./EXT.:** Same as Scenes 3–4
- **Location:** Same as Scenes 3–4
- **Time of Day:** Continuous
- **Story Purpose:** Close the episode on genuine, unresolved tension.
- **Emotional Purpose:** Quiet, unresolved.
- **Characters Present:** Kael, the Guardian.
- **Required Assets:** Kael; the Guardian.
- **Required Audio:** No line may resolve the tension raised.
- **Required Visual Effects:** None.
- **Continuity Requirements:** Closes directly into Episode 5's own Beginning State (Mira's backstory), per both packages' tonal Transition.

**ACTION**

A quiet after the crack in Scene 4. Kael, still unsettled, finds his own way to something that's been sitting with him — not about himself this time, but about the Guardian.

**DIALOGUE**

KAEL
*(quiet, genuinely asking)*
You could have stepped in two days ago. Before it got as far as it did.

THE GUARDIAN
*(a beat — not evasive, simply weighing it honestly)*
I could have.

KAEL
Why didn't you?

THE GUARDIAN
*(no easy answer offered)*
I don't have a clean answer for you. I have a reason I believe in, most days. Today isn't one of the days I'm certain of it.

Neither of them resolves it. The moment holds, unanswered.

**PERFORMANCE NOTES**

Per the seed package's own explicit instruction, this must end without resolution — no line may tie off the tension. The Guardian's admission of uncertainty must land as genuinely earned discomfort, not false humility, and must never gesture toward what it is or where its restraint comes from — only toward the fact that the question costs it something to sit with honestly.

**CAMERA NOTES**

Held, unresolved framing — no cut that implies closure.

**LIGHTING NOTES**

Same quiet, close treatment as Scene 4.

**SOUND NOTES**

The episode should end in near-silence — no musical resolution.

**TRANSITION**

Hold on the unresolved quiet, then cut — tonally, into Episode 5's own Beginning State (Mira's backstory). No plot cliffhanger.
