**Classification:** Internal — New Locked Production Canon Document (**LOCKED CANON, effective immediately**)

## 3. Kael Aurelian Veyr — Visual Canon

**Psychological anchor (Locked, Character Bible):** integrity, honesty, care, non-discriminating compassion; self-doubt and fear of failure beneath conviction; power as responsibility, never privilege; orphaned by a Breach, raised as a Ward Division Cadet at Ward Station Seven; Mars-born, relocated to the megacity as a child; Titan's protégé; the Guardian's partner. Every visual choice below reinforces "ordinary person, extraordinary responsibility" — never "chosen one" glamour.

1. **Visual Philosophy.** Groundedness over spectacle. Kael's design must never read as innately special — his one visual exception (the eye-glow) is a diagnostic burden, not a decoration, exactly as already Locked. Everything else about his design should look earned, practical, and ordinary.
2. **Narrative Function.** Protagonist and convergence point of the Three-Way Conflict; the audience's entry point into "carrying more than one person should have to carry."
3. **Overall Silhouette.** Lean-athletic, Cadet-trained; practical proportions that read as capable without reading as superhuman.
4. **Height Range.** Approximately 178–185 cm (5'10"–6'1") — a credible young-adult Cadet range; tall enough to carry command presence, not exaggerated into hero-cliché scale.
5. **Body Proportions.** Athletic-functional, consistent with Ward Division training and endurance-based conditioning — not stylized hypermasculine bulk. His body reads as trained for duty, not for spectacle.
6. **Facial Structure.** Open, direct, sincere features. Youthful (age 19, Locked) but visibly carrying strain and maturity beyond his years — Titan's death and his orphaning should be legible in subtle, not dramatic, ways.
7. **Eyes.** **Locked, unchanged.** Icy blue-cyan baseline, intensifying with Signal-Sensitivity (Master System Prompt Section 4). Shape and gaze: attentive, direct, unwavering — consistent with his refusal to look away from what needs doing. Emotion Modifiers (Locked): Calm = soft, stable glow; Conflict = flickering, unstable glow; Activation = intense, sharp glow; Breakdown = fragmented, unstable glow.
8. **Hair.** Dark, short, practical — no vanity styling. A cut that survives fieldwork without maintenance, consistent with someone who prioritizes others over his own image.
9. **Skin.** Practical field-Cadet texture — light-to-medium tone with visible, plausible wear (minor scarring, weathering) rather than pristine "hero" rendering. Exact tone value is a concept-art-stage decision within this texture direction.
10. **Hands.** Capable and marked by training — calluses and small scars consistent with combat and technical Cadet duties; never soft or manicured.
11. **Movement Style.** Economical, controlled, disciplined — Ward Division training shows in every motion. Steady under pressure; motion sharpens and tightens specifically during his Locked Activation eye-glow state, never independently of it.
12. **Posture.** Upright but not rigid; visible tension carried through the shoulders as the physical trace of his burden. Opens into a more relaxed stance only in his rare private/vulnerable moments — a deliberate, meaningful contrast, not an inconsistency.
13. **Facial Expressions.** The already-Locked 10-expression Expression Library (Character Production Package, Section 5) — each cross-linked to the Locked eye-glow Emotion Modifier state; no new expressions added or altered here.
14. **Emotional Presence.** Sincere, present, engaged — never detached or performative. His self-doubt should be legible as a flicker beneath otherwise-steady Calm-state composure, not hidden entirely.
15. **Visual Contrast.** The most grounded, practical register of the visually-canonized cast — contrasts with Mira's civilian warmth and Cassian's Upper Grid polish. He is the only principal character with an active, Locked visual-signal system; that system (the eye-glow) is his defining distinguishing feature and must never be echoed, approximated, or partially replicated on Mira, Cassian, or the Guardian.
16. **Color Philosophy.** Practical, muted tones — grays, worn neutrals, muted non-cyan blues, earth tones — for wardrobe and general palette. Cyan/icy-blue remains reserved exclusively for his eyes, per the already-Locked reservation (`color-language.md`); never used decoratively anywhere else in his design. **[Superseded in part by Amendment v1.3, below — original text preserved here as historical record.]**
17. **Lighting Behavior.** Scene-level lighting follows the Locked Emotional Lighting Modes (Grounded/Charged/Wounded, `lighting-system.md`). His personal eye-glow Emotion Modifier system operates independently, as his own character-specific diagnostic overlay — the two systems are never conflated; a Grounded-lit scene can still show a Conflict-state eye-glow, and vice versa.
18. **Animation Notes.** Idle, walk, run, turn, look, conversation, combat, reaction, and facial animation all required (per his Character Production Package, Section 7). Facial animation must synchronize with his Locked eye-glow Emotion Modifier transitions, including mid-scene state changes within a single confrontation.
19. **Camera Language.** Standard Lens Philosophy (35–50mm equivalent) for his dialogue and character work, per `camera-language.md`. Negative space is used deliberately during his early-season isolation beats (already cross-referenced in `camera-language.md`). Handheld instability is reserved exclusively for Breach/Fracture/Burnout sequences, never as a generic intensity device even in his most extreme emotional scenes.
20. **Do Not Do.** Never render the eye-glow at Activation intensity unless Signal-Sensitivity is actually active in-scene. Never use flat cel-shading, speed lines, or single-tone eye highlights (`forbidden-elements.md`). Never give him idealized, hypermasculine hero proportions. Never use cyan decoratively anywhere but his eyes. Never render him as physically flawless or unweathered. **[Superseded in part by Amendment v1.3, below — original text preserved here as historical record.]**
21. **Production Notes.** Genuinely open items, not fabricated here: exact hair-color value, exact skin-tone value, exact height within the stated range. These remain concept-art-stage decisions within the direction this document establishes.
22. **Visual Consistency Rules.** Every rule above must render identically across all 36 episodes and all promotional/marketing assets — the same standard already Locked for his eye-glow specification, now extended to his full visual design.

## 4. Mira Solenne Valeris — Visual Canon

**Psychological anchor (Locked, Character Bible):** civilian reconstruction specialist, age 20; raised in the Moon's civilian districts, now resident in the megacity's Mid Zone; displaced by an Ascendant Doctrine occupation that offered protection only in exchange for submission; her internal conflict is rebuilding a life and deciding whether to trust a system — and a person — that arrived too late; she exists on her own terms, not only in relation to Kael. Every visual choice below reinforces "a builder, not a fighter" and quiet, earned resilience.

1. **Visual Philosophy.** Quiet competence and warmth. A "builder," not a "fighter" — her visual identity communicates hands-on capability and grounded resilience, deliberately contrasting with Kael's military practicality and Cassian's inherited polish.
2. **Narrative Function.** The civilian counterweight to the saga's military and ideological conflicts; individual-scale proof of what protection is actually for; Kael's earned relationship, not his reward.
3. **Overall Silhouette.** Practical civilian-worker silhouette — unglamorous, functional, immediately legible as someone who works with her hands rather than someone dressed for spectacle.
4. **Height Range.** Approximately 163–170 cm (5'4"–5'7") — an ordinary civilian adult range, deliberately unremarkable; her narrative weight is never carried by her physical scale.
5. **Body Proportions.** Ordinary, capable civilian build — no stylized idealization or combat/glamour archetype; she reads as a real working adult.
6. **Facial Structure.** Warm, open, approachable features — trustworthy and steady rather than dramatic, consistent with her quiet, internally-carried resilience.
7. **Eyes.** No Locked eye-glow or Signal-linked system applies to Mira (confirmed absent in her Character Production Package, Section 3) — ordinary human eyes, expressive and steady, consistent with her deciding-whether-to-trust internal conflict. No color is Locked; left to concept-art finalization within this "warm, steady" direction.
8. **Hair.** Practical, work-appropriate styling — kept out of the way for hands-on reconstruction work (e.g., tied back), no elaborate or vanity styling.
9. **Skin.** Ordinary civilian texture consistent with outdoor, hands-on reconstruction fieldwork — plausible weather exposure, not idealized flawlessness. Exact tone value left to concept-art finalization.
10. **Hands.** **Her single strongest distinguishing visual feature.** Visibly marked by hands-on reconstruction work — calluses, small working scars, practical short unpolished nails. Her hands should read on-screen as a career history, directly reinforcing her Locked identity ("rebuilding what conflict destroys") more concretely than any other available visual choice.
11. **Movement Style.** Practical, purposeful, unhurried — the movement of someone competent at physical work, not combat-trained; steady rather than sharp.
12. **Posture.** Open and grounded by default; visibly closes or guards slightly in scenes touching her displacement backstory or trust-related tension, consistent with her Locked internal conflict.
13. **Facial Expressions.** The already-Locked 10-expression Expression Library (Character Production Package, Section 5). Anger, previously flagged there as narratively thin, is confirmed here as a low-priority, rarely-used expression rather than a core one — consistent with that flag, not overriding it.
14. **Emotional Presence.** Warm and steady by default, quiet rather than performative. Her unresolved trust-related tension should read as restraint, consistent with her Voice Requirements guidance ("carried, not cured").
15. **Visual Contrast.** The civilian register of the principal cast — deliberately unarmed, unarmored, and un-uniformed in every scene, standing apart from Kael's Cadet practicality and Cassian's Upper Grid formality. Her hands (Section 10) are her equivalent of Kael's eye-glow: the one feature that visually encodes her entire identity.
16. **Color Philosophy.** Warm, practical earth tones and work-wear neutrals — never the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue. Materially distinct from both Kael's muted-practical palette and Cassian's cool formal palette.
17. **Lighting Behavior.** Follows the Locked Emotional Lighting Modes (Grounded/Charged/Wounded) exactly as any human character; no special or signature lighting system, consistent with her non-Signal, non-eye-glow status (Section 7, above).
18. **Animation Notes.** Idle, conversation, observation, reaction, and facial animation required. Walk cycle required but secondary to her default stationary/working staging. Run and combat animation not required, consistent with her Locked non-combat role.
19. **Camera Language.** Standard Lens Philosophy for her dialogue and character scenes; no reserved centered-symmetry, negative-space, or handheld treatment is specifically assigned to her — she is filmed with the same grounded, motivated grammar as any civilian-register scene.
20. **Do Not Do.** Never glamorize or combat-stylize her design. Never assign her a Signal/eye-glow system — that remains exclusively Kael's. Never render her hands as clean or unmarked — that would erase her single strongest distinguishing feature. Never use the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue anywhere in her design.
21. **Production Notes.** Genuinely open items, not fabricated here: exact hair color, eye color, precise skin tone, and exact height within the stated range. This document resolves her design *direction*, not final exact values.
22. **Visual Consistency Rules.** Every rule above must render identically across all her episode appearances and all promotional/marketing assets.

## 5. Cassian Veyrion Aurelian — Visual Canon

**Psychological anchor (Locked, Character Bible):** former USCN Chief Strategic Futures Director, Ascendant Doctrine founder/leader; Aurelian family, Upper Grid origin; age late 30s–40s; radicalized by the Sol Collapse; controlled, articulate, genuinely sincere — never acting from personal power, revenge, or cruelty; his tragedy is that a genuine desire to protect humanity became, one compromise at a time, a justification for controlling it; his exact five-stage arc (Episodes 28 First Doubt / 29 Kael's Challenge / 30 Extremist Incident / 31 Shaken / 35 complete self-dismantling) is Locked and precise. Every visual choice below reinforces controlled authority that must crack believably, on schedule, not gradually.

1. **Visual Philosophy.** Controlled elegance and inherited authority that must crack believably by Episode 35. His polish is itself characterization — Upper Grid education and power, not villainy — designed specifically so its visible unraveling reads as tragedy.
2. **Narrative Function.** The saga's philosophical antagonist, "protection through control" — a genuine, sincere position the story challenges rather than a villain the story defeats.
3. **Overall Silhouette.** Composed, formal, precise — an Upper Grid strategist's silhouette; upright and contained. Never disheveled or menacing in his default state; his control is the entire point of the design.
4. **Height Range.** Approximately 178–188 cm (5'10"–6'2") — an authoritative adult range consistent with a former Chief Strategic Futures Director's public command presence.
5. **Body Proportions.** Lean, composed, non-combat proportions — a strategist's maintained conditioning, not a soldier's athletic build.
6. **Facial Structure.** Refined, controlled features consistent with Upper Grid origin and his Locked age range. A face built for public composure, deliberately designed to leave visible room for that composure to fracture at Episodes 30–31 and dismantle fully at Episode 35, per his Locked schedule.
7. **Eyes.** No Locked eye-glow or Signal-linked system applies to Cassian (confirmed absent in his Character Production Package, Section 3) — ordinary human eyes, deliberately controlled and measured in his default expression; should visibly lose that composure only at his Locked schedule beats.
8. **Hair.** Precise, maintained, formal styling — consistent with Upper Grid origin and his former public role. Never disheveled by default, so any disruption during Episodes 30–31 or 35 is immediately legible as character breakdown, not styling inconsistency.
9. **Skin.** Composed, well-maintained texture consistent with Upper Grid resources and his Locked age range — the visible toll of the Sol Collapse and his private fear should read as controlled fatigue, not physical hardship, distinct from Kael's fieldwork weathering and Mira's outdoor weathering. Exact tone value left to concept-art finalization.
10. **Hands.** Precise, controlled, economical gestures — a strategist's hands, never wasted motion. Should visibly shake or lose precision specifically and only during Episode 31 and Episode 35, per his Locked schedule — his hands are his second-clearest distinguishing feature after his controlled facial composure.
11. **Movement Style.** Measured, deliberate, economical — never rushed, never wasted; the movement of someone who has spent a career controlling how he is perceived. Frays only at his Locked schedule beats.
12. **Posture.** Upright, composed, formal by default — the posture of inherited authority. Visibly loosens or collapses specifically at Episodes 30–31 and completely at Episode 35, never gradually eroding across unrelated episodes; his schedule is discrete stages, not a slow drift.
13. **Facial Expressions.** The already-Locked 10-expression Expression Library (Character Production Package, Section 5). Anger remains flagged there as requiring careful handling to avoid contradicting his "not a villain" Canon Alignment Principle — restated, not overridden, here. His controlled-composure baseline makes Grief and Reflection (his Episode 35 states) the most visually significant departures in his entire expression range.
14. **Emotional Presence.** Sincere and persuasive, never menacing — genuine conviction, consistent with his Locked moral limits. His tragedy must be legible in his default presence, not held back for a single reveal at Episode 35.
15. **Visual Contrast.** The formal, composed register of the principal cast — Upper Grid polish contrasting deliberately with Kael's Lower-Sector practicality and Mira's civilian practicality. His five-stage emotional arc is his equivalent of Kael's eye-glow and Mira's hands: the one visual thread required to track a Locked, exact schedule rather than drift freely.
16. **Color Philosophy.** Cool, precise, formal tones — structured neutrals, deep non-cyan colors — reflecting Upper Grid control and resources; deliberately distinct from Kael's muted practicality and Mira's warm earth tones. Never the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue.
17. **Lighting Behavior.** Follows the Locked Emotional Lighting Modes (Grounded/Charged/Wounded) exactly as any human character. His Episode 35 self-dismantling is the strongest candidate in his arc for Wounded-mode lighting per its own Locked definition ("desaturated, harsh single-source or top-down light... loss, trauma beats") — noted here as a natural fit, not fixed as a mandatory scene-level choice, which remains a screenplay-implementation decision.
18. **Animation Notes.** Idle, conversation, turn, reaction, and facial animation required. Walk cycle required but secondary. Run and combat animation not required, consistent with his Locked non-combat role. Reaction and facial animation must be capable of tracking his exact five-stage schedule as distinguishable, escalating states — never a single generic "distress" animation reused across all five beats.
19. **Camera Language.** Standard Lens Philosophy for his dialogue and strategic scenes. A push-in is a natural fit for landing his Episode 35 self-dismantling line, consistent with the Locked "no more than one big camera move per scene" rule and push-in's existing use case (landing an emotional beat or twist reveal) — noted as a scene-level implementation fit, not a new camera rule.
20. **Do Not Do.** Never render him as visibly villainous, menacing, or unstable outside his Locked schedule beats. Never assign him a Signal/eye-glow system. Never let his composure "leak" gradually across unrelated episodes — his unraveling is discrete and scheduled. Never use the Locked-reserved cyan/icy-blue anywhere in his design.
21. **Production Notes.** Genuinely open items, not fabricated here: exact hair color, eye color, precise skin tone, and exact height within the stated range.
22. **Visual Consistency Rules.** Every rule above must render identically across all appearances, except where this document itself specifies a Locked, scheduled departure (his five-stage arc) — that departure is itself part of his visual consistency rule, not an exception to it.

## Amendment v1.3 — Kael Outfit Cyan-Accent Restoration (Master System Prompt Reconciliation)

**Reopened 2026-07-18** per `Fracture_Protocol_Kael_Prompt_Costume_Canon_Reconciliation_v1.0.md`, which found that `Fracture_Protocol_Master_System_Prompt_v1.2.md`, Section 4 (Core Character System — Kael, `[LOCKED]`), explicitly and unconditionally includes "subtle cyan accents" as part of Kael's outfit — "core design — never change." Items 16 and 20 above, as originally written, excluded cyan from the costume entirely without cross-checking this document against the Master System Prompt, contradicting the single highest-authority document in the production. Both original items are preserved unmodified above, marked superseded-in-part; this amendment states the corrected rule going forward.

**Item 16 (Color Philosophy) — corrected:** Practical, muted tones — grays, worn neutrals, muted non-cyan blues, earth tones — dominate his wardrobe and general palette. Per Master System Prompt Section 4 (`[LOCKED]`, "core design — never change"), the outfit itself also carries **subtle cyan accents** as a static, structural design element. The eye-glow remains cyan/icy-blue's *primary, dynamic, diagnostic* use — his defining, Locked signal system, governed by the full Emotion Modifier table — and nothing in this correction alters, dilutes, or competes with that role. No exact placement, shade, or extent of the outfit's cyan accents is specified here; that remains a concept-art-stage decision.

**Item 20 (Do Not Do) — corrected:** Never render the eye-glow at Activation intensity unless Signal-Sensitivity is actually active in-scene. Never use flat cel-shading, speed lines, or single-tone eye highlights (`forbidden-elements.md`). Never give him idealized, hypermasculine hero proportions. Never let the outfit's subtle cyan accents (Master System Prompt Section 4) rival, echo, or approach the eye-glow's own visual prominence — the eye-glow must always read as the singular, primary cyan signal; the outfit's accents are secondary, static, and subordinate. Never render him as physically flawless or unweathered.
