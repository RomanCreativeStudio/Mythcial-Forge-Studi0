## 2. Narrative Purpose

Per the Faction Bible, the Ascendant Core is Cassian's personal command center and a symbol of his belief that humanity's future can be calculated and guided — the physical expression of "protection through control." Narratively, it is where his composure is allowed to drop when no one is watching: Episode 17's establishing scene exists, in the screenplay's own words, "only to let the audience see the cost his account never showed." It is explicitly not the Ascendant Doctrine's institutional source — losing it costs Cassian a headquarters, not the movement itself, which matters for its Environment Classification (below).

## 5. Environment Classification

**Secondary Location · Recurring · Restricted.** Not classified as Government or Institutional — the Faction Bible is explicit that this is Cassian's *personal* facility, distinct from the Doctrine's own institutional presence (see Environment 8, the Doctrine Institutional Environment, for the public counterpart). Not classified as Historical despite housing "a repository of historical and civilization-scale research" — that category is reserved for sites that are themselves historically significant, not sites that merely contain historical materials; this distinction is stated explicitly rather than silently assumed either way.
