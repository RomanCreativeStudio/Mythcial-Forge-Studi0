# THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL — PHASE 6D.0: PROP & TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTION GOVERNANCE

**Status:** Compiled 2026-07-15 per "Founder Directive — Phase 6D.0 Prop & Technology Production Governance (v1.0)." Environment Production is certified READY (per `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Phase6C9_Environment_Completion_v1.0.md`, unaffected by the subsequent ENV-016 Repository Reconciliation); this document opens Phase 6D on that foundation.

## 1. Purpose

Phase 6C established that every environment in the production is identified, reconciled, and Approved. Phase 6D exists to do the same for props and technology — every physical object, device, tool, vehicle, or piece of equipment that appears, or is referenced as required, across the 36 Approved screenplays and their governing production packages. Phase 6D.0 establishes only the governance rules this work must follow. It authorizes no asset creation itself.

---

## 2. Source Authority — Every Prop and Technology Asset Must Derive Only From

Per the Founder Directive's own explicit instruction, every prop and technology asset produced under Phase 6D — at every subsequent sub-phase — must be derived exclusively from:

1. **Locked Canon** — the World Bible, Character Bible, Faction Bible, and any other document carrying Locked status, including the Production Bible where it supplies binding production rules.
2. **Approved Screenplays** — the 36 Phase 5B screenplays, their own Asset Requirements tables, Location Lists, Scene Action text, and Production Notes, and their governing Phase 4B Episode Production Packages.
3. **Approved Environment Packages** — the 15 independent Environment Production Packages (Phase 6C, reconciled through Phase 6C.9 and the ENV-016 Repository Reconciliation), specifically their own Asset Dependencies, Recurring Asset References, and Technology Presence fields.

**Never from assumption or memory.** A prop or technology item with no citable source in one of these three categories does not exist for production purposes — it must be flagged as missing, never invented, exactly as the same discipline has governed every phase of this production since Phase 6A. This mirrors, and is subordinate to, the Master System Prompt's own foundational Canon Governance rule.

---

## 3. Prop Authority

A **prop** is any physical, non-technological object referenced in a screenplay or environment package as present in a scene — carried, used, worn (excluding costume, which remains governed by Phase 6B.3), or set-dressed — that is not itself an environment or a character.

- Prop Authority is established only by direct citation: a screenplay's own Asset Requirements table, Location List annotation, or Scene Action text; or an Environment Production Package's own Asset Dependencies field.
- Where a prop is described functionally but not visually (e.g., "evidentiary/briefing materials already implied by existing institutional dressing," per several Environment Production Packages already on record), Phase 6D production must treat this as a flagged, functionally-scoped item — not an invitation to design freely beyond the confirmed function.
- Props already confirmed, across this production's own prior research, to require **zero new asset creation** (the repeated, cross-verified "none newly required" finding present in nearly every Environment Production Package to date) are not to be re-opened as production items absent new Locked or Approved evidence.

---

## 4. Technology Authority

**Technology** is any device, system, tool, or piece of equipment implying a functional mechanism — communications infrastructure, research/archive systems, response equipment, vehicles, or any Cipher/Fragment/Breach-adjacent apparatus.

- Technology Authority is established only by direct citation, exactly as Prop Authority above, with one additional constraint: any technology item touching Cipher-work, Fragment phenomena, or Breach-adjacent effects must additionally be checked against `mythic-forge-art-bible/system-interference-visual-rules.md` and the Locked permanent three-color system (cyan/icy-blue reserved exclusively for Fracture-anomaly phenomena, never decorative, never assigned to a prop or device as mere styling).
- Per Phase 5A.2's own standing lock, any technology whose underlying mechanism is deliberately unspecified in Locked Canon (most notably Mira's Episode 33 mechanism) must remain unspecified in Phase 6D production as well — this governance document extends, and does not override, that mystery-protection rule.
- Technology confirmed absent from the production (Phase 6A's own standing "zero specific props, weapons, vehicles, technology items, or new visual effects are named in any of the 36 Approved screenplays" finding, reconfirmed at every subsequent phase through Phase 6C.9) remains the accurate baseline until a specific citation is found to the contrary.

---

## 5. Asset Creation Rule

No prop or technology asset may be created — conceptually, visually, or in any production document — without a citation satisfying Section 2 above. Where a screenplay or environment package implies a prop or technology item functionally (e.g., "response equipment," "evidentiary materials," "research/archive technology") without describing it, Phase 6D production may proceed only as far as that functional description reaches; visual specificity beyond the citation is an invention and is prohibited. This rule mirrors the Environment Production Canon's own governing principle (Phase 6C, Section 1: "Do not invent canon... Never from memory. Never from inference. Never from assumption"), applied here to props and technology.

---

## 6. Asset Approval Rule

Every prop or technology asset requires:
1. A compiled Asset entry citing its source(s) directly (screenplay, Phase 4B package, or Environment Production Package).
2. A Founder Review confirming source citation is genuine and complete.
3. An Alignment Audit confirming no Locked Canon, screenplay, or Environment Package is contradicted.
4. Explicit Founder Approval before the asset is marked Approved or Locked in the Asset Registry.

No prop or technology asset may be marked Approved on the strength of a compiled entry alone — Founder Approval is required at the individual-asset level, exactly as it has been required at the individual-environment level throughout Phase 6C and its full reconciliation lineage.

---

## 7. Asset Revision Rule

Any revision to an Approved or Locked prop/technology asset must:
- Reopen the existing asset entry, never overwrite it silently — preserving all prior Founder Approval as historical record, per the reopen-and-append method already established and consistently applied throughout the Phase 6C.5–6C.9 and ENV-016 reconciliation lineage.
- Cite the specific new source (a newly Approved screenplay, a corrected Environment Package citation, or a new Founder Directive) that justifies the revision.
- Carry its own new Founder Review, Alignment Audit, and Founder Approval before superseding the prior version.
- Never delete a prior version's record — mark it Superseded, consistent with the standing "nothing disappears without a record" practice already applied to ENV-016's own package.

---

## 8. Dependency Rule

Every prop and technology asset must document:
- Which character(s) use or interact with it.
- Which environment(s) it appears in, cited by that environment's own Environment Production Package.
- Which episode(s) it appears in, cited directly.
- Any other asset (prop, technology, character, or environment) its existence depends on or is bounded by.

An asset with no confirmed character, environment, or episode dependency is not a production item — it should not be created. This mirrors the Dependency Validation structure already required by the Environment Production Canon (Phase 6C, Section 6) and the equivalent structure in the Phase 6B Character Production line.

---

## 9. Asset Registry Rule

Every prop and technology asset must be logged in `documentation/Asset_Registry.md`, under a new Props/Technology section (replacing the current "No entries. Zero specific props, weapons, vehicles, technology items, or new visual effects are named in any of the 36 Approved screenplays" placeholder once any asset is actually confirmed), using the same schema already established for Character and Environment entries: Asset ID, Asset Name, Category, Status, Priority, Episodes Used, Notes. No prop or technology asset exists for production purposes until it is logged in the Registry with a citable source.

---

## 10. Version Control Rule

Every prop/technology production document follows the same versioning discipline already standing across this entire production: `v1.0` on first compilation, incremented major versions on Founder-directed content changes, changelog entries preserved in full at every version, and git commit/push performed separately for content-compilation versus Founder-approval steps wherever both occur in the same session, consistent with the standing practice established since Phase 1A.

---

## 11. Production Conflict Rule

If any prop or technology research finds:
- A contradiction between two Locked Canon sources,
- A contradiction between a screenplay and an Environment Production Package,
- A prop or technology requirement with no citable source anywhere in Locked Canon, Approved Screenplays, or Approved Environment Packages, or
- Any indication of repository corruption or a missing Locked authority,

production on that specific asset stops, and the conflict is flagged for Founder decision — exactly as the Lighting Canon rule (Phase 6C.5, Step 3) required for environment lighting contradictions, and as the STOP CONDITIONS list has governed every directive in this production since Phase 6.0. No conflict of this kind may be silently resolved by inference, convenience, or default.
