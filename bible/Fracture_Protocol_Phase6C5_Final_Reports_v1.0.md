**Finding:** every priority tier now contains at least one READY or IN PROGRESS environment, and only Priority 2 and Priority 3 still carry a BLOCKED item each (one per tier, down from Phase 6C's near-uniform blocking). Priority order continues to govern build sequence; the two remaining BLOCKED items (Cassian's Private Interior, Night Haven) should be the focus of any further Founder decision-making, since every other flagged item from Phase 6C has now converted to READY or IN PROGRESS.

**BLOCKED item detail (Step 6 requirement — exact blocker, severity, required Founder decision, recommended resolution):**

1. **Cassian's Private Interior (the Ascendant Core).**
   - *Exact blocker:* No architecture, construction, materials, scale, dimensions, or layer placement exists anywhere in Locked Canon or the Phase 4B corpus beyond the Faction Bible's single sentence establishing its narrative function and repeated confirmations across five separate Phase 4B episodes (17, 24, 25, 28, 30, 31) that it is deliberately reused rather than newly described.
   - *Severity:* Moderate-high — blocks concept art entirely, though the character (Cassian) and narrative function are fully specified.
   - *Required Founder decision:* Supply a base architectural/material direction for this space, or explicitly authorize a minimal, deliberately-underdesigned treatment consistent with its established narrative minimalism.
   - *Recommended resolution (offered only):* Given that every Phase 4B reference to this environment explicitly frames its minimalism as intentional restraint rather than a gap to fill, a deliberately spare, cool-toned, precise interior — extending Cassian's own Locked personal visual register (Phase 6B.2/6B.3) rather than inventing new architectural language — is offered as one plausible direction; a second option is to treat it as genuinely "off-screen enough" that no full architectural build is needed beyond what any given scene's blocking requires. Neither option is self-authorized here.

2. **Night Haven Institutional Space.**
   - *Exact blocker:* The lighting register is genuinely contradictory across confirmed appearances (Episode 14/22's "cooler, more formal" vs. Episode 27's "warmer resistance tone"), independently reproduced at both the Phase 4B and Phase 5B production stages.
   - *Severity:* Moderate — does not block story, casting, or asset-dependency work, but blocks any concept-art lighting brief and color-palette finalization.
   - *Required Founder decision:* Select a single baseline lighting register as permanent Locked canon, or supply an in-story justification for treating the difference as intentional (which would convert this into a Lighting Canon Determination A rule instead).
   - *Recommended resolution (offered only):* Night Haven's Locked "freedom-first resistance" philosophy plausibly supports "warmer resistance tone" as the more thematically consistent register (particularly for Episode 27's active-resistance content), but this package does not adopt it, since no screenplay text states this reasoning explicitly.

## THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL — ENVIRONMENT PRODUCTION CERTIFICATION

**Determination question, per the directive's own Step 8 instruction:** "Determine READY FOR PHASE 6D or NOT READY."

## Determination: NOT READY

This determination is reached deliberately, not as a conservative default. The reconciliation phase achieved substantial, evidence-grounded progress — 8 of 10 named environments are now READY or IN PROGRESS, up from 4 of 10 under Phase 6C — but two conditions from the directive's own certification standard are not yet met, and a READY determination would misrepresent the actual state of the evidence:

**Remaining blocking issues:**

1. **Cassian's Private Interior (the Ascendant Core) — BLOCKED.** No architecture exists to build from anywhere in Locked Canon or the full Phase 4B/Phase 5B corpus. Full blocker detail in Report 4, above.
2. **Night Haven Institutional Space — BLOCKED.** A genuine, unresolved lighting contradiction, independently confirmed reproduced at two separate production stages, requires Founder adjudication before a concept-art lighting brief can be finalized. Full blocker detail in Report 4, above.
3. **The ~18 remaining lower-frequency Season One/Arc Two environments remain entirely unidentified**, exactly as under Phase 6C — this reconciliation's own scope (per its governing document, `Fracture_Protocol_Phase6C5_Environment_Canon_Reconciliation_v1.0.md`) was reconciliation of the 10 already-named environments, not identification of new ones. This gap is unchanged and remains the single largest item standing between the current state and genuine Environment Production completeness — roughly two-thirds of the production's total named-environment count has not yet been individually identified, let alone built.

**What IS certified complete:**
- The Phase 6C.5 reconciliation method (reopen, preserve, append — never overwrite) is Locked Canon, applied consistently across all 10 packages.
- All 10 named Environment Production Packages now carry a complete Reconciliation Section: direct re-verification against the full required source set (Master System Prompt, Production Bible, World Bible, Character Bible, Faction Bible, Art Bible, Phase 4B, Approved Screenplays), a Lighting Canon determination, a 13-field Environment Identity block, Environment Consistency findings, and a three-state Production Readiness determination.
- One further discrepancy (Environment 8's Episode 21 addition) was found to be a Phase 6C research error and self-corrected, consistent with the standing practice of flagging every discrepancy including the reconciling authority's own prior mistakes.
- Four environments previously blocked or flagged for Founder decision (Guardian/Kael Partnership, Ascendant Network, Doctrine Institutional Environment, Populated Civilian Area) were upgraded to IN PROGRESS on the strength of concrete, evidence-based findings in the Phase 4B corpus — none invented, all traceable to specific quoted source text.
- Three environments previously "In Progress" (Formal Meeting, Neutral Private Meeting) or newly reconciled (Reconstruction-Work, Ward Station Seven) are now confirmed READY with no blocker.
- The Asset Registry, Environment Reports, Environment Dependency Report, and Production Readiness Report (this document) are all updated per Step 7.
- No screenplay was rewritten, no environment was redesigned, and no new asset was created anywhere in this reconciliation phase — consistent with the directive's own explicit scope limits.

**What is NOT yet certified:** "Environment Production is complete." Two of ten named environments still carry a genuine, documented blocker, and the ~18 deferred environments — the majority of the production's actual total environment count — remain entirely outside this or any prior phase's scope.

**Recommendation, not a self-authorized next step:** the two remaining BLOCKED items are narrow enough that a single focused Founder decision pass (architecture direction for Cassian's Private Interior; lighting register for Night Haven) would likely be sufficient to convert both to IN PROGRESS or READY. The ~18 deferred environments require a dedicated identification pass — structurally the same kind of work this reconciliation phase itself performed for the 10 named environments, but not yet begun for the remainder — before Environment Production as a whole can be certified complete. These are offered as observations to inform the Founder's own next-step decision, not as a plan this document authorizes itself.

Per the directive's own Step 8 instruction, this NOT READY determination lists every remaining blocker rather than proceeding to certify Phase 6D readiness. Founder Approval is still applied below to certify this document's own findings as accurate and complete, distinct from certifying environment production itself as complete.
