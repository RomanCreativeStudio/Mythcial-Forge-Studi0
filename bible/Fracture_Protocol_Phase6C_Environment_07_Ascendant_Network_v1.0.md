**Scene References:** Episode 22, Scene 3 — the sole ACTION text describing this environment in the entire screenplay corpus: "Within the Network's own operation, something moves forward as simple continuation — not remarked as anything unusual by those carrying it out."
**Speaking Character Interaction:** no named character is confirmed present in the one physical scene; it is staged as an anonymous operational continuation.
**Major Story Function:** confirms the Ascendant Doctrine's reach is active and ongoing at a systemic level, in parallel with the Light Order and Night Haven institutional scenes occurring in the same episode.
