## 3. Screenplay Appearances (Screenplay Validation)

**Two discrepancies flagged, not silently inherited — the most significant correction found among the 10 named environments:**

1. **Episode 14 does not actually contain a physical Night Haven environment**, despite being listed in Phase 6A's aggregate range. Episode 14's own Location List places all five of its scenes at Ward Station Seven; Night Haven appears in that episode only as an unnamed **character** (a Night Haven representative) present within Ward Station Seven's own space. There is no physical Night Haven space rendered on-screen in Episode 14 at all.
2. **Episode 27 does contain a confirmed physical Night Haven scene** ("A separate room, a separate philosophy holding firm under the same widening pressure") but is **absent from Phase 6A's original episode range** for this environment. This appears to be an omission in Phase 6A's own aggregate list, corrected here.

**First true physical Appearance:** Episode 22 (the first episode where "Night Haven institutional space" is an actual named Location List entry, not merely a character's institutional affiliation).
**Final confirmed Appearance:** Episode 27.
**Recurring Episodes (corrected):** 22, 27. Episode 14 is retained in this package's citation record only as the source of the Night Haven representative *character* who appears there, not as a physical-environment appearance.
**Scene References:** Episode 22, Scene 2 ("A separate room, a separate institution. Night Haven representatives review their own fragment of the same pattern"); Episode 27, Scene 3 ("A separate room, a separate philosophy holding firm under the same widening pressure").
**Speaking Character Interaction:** unnamed Night Haven representative(s) only, in both confirmed physical appearances — no core cast character (Kael, Mira, Cassian, the Guardian) is confirmed present in this environment.
**Major Story Function:** shows Night Haven's own institutional response to the saga's widening Arc Two pressure, parallel to and separate from Light Order's and the Doctrine's own institutional scenes in the same episodes (per Episode 34's cross-cutting structure, see Environment 8).
