## 2. Character Overview

**Identity.** Mira Solenne Valeris — a civilian reconstruction specialist, age 20, raised in the Moon's civilian districts, now resident in the megacity's Mid Zone. The first named individual supporting character introduced in this canon, and the first-by-name fulfillment of Kael's own Locked personal want to build a family with the person he loves.

**Narrative purpose.** She exists on her own terms, not only to explain, praise, or challenge Kael — her backstory, philosophy, and internal conflict predate her relationship with him and would remain true without it. She was displaced when an Ascendant Doctrine occupation offered her home protection only in exchange for submission; the Light 98th arrived too late to stop it. She is compiled as thematic proof of Cassian's own worst fear (a movement built to protect people can still cost someone everything) and as a living instance of the Guardian's own stated limitation (it cannot fully weigh why one life holds immeasurable meaning to another).

**Emotional role.** Her internal conflict is her own: rebuilding a life after displacement, and deciding whether to trust a system — and a person — that arrived, in her own experience, too late. Her relationship with Kael carries the specific, unresolved weight of his own defining fear (failing the people counting on him) made personal; this is carried, not cured, consistent with how Titan's and the Guardian's relationships with Kael already treat unresolved pain.

**Thematic role.** She works in civilian reconstruction — rebuilding what conflict destroys, not fighting the conflict itself — the practical, individual-scale counterpart to the Guardian's civilizational-scale perspective. Her Legacy: she hopes to be remembered not as someone who was rescued, but as someone who rebuilt.

No physical, costume, or purely-visual content appears anywhere in this section's source material.
