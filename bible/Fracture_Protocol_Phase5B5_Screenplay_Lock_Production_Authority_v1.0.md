---

## Purpose

This document permanently establishes the Approved Screenplay as the authoritative production document for every department. This is not a story document. It is a permanent production governance document.

---

## Screenplay Authority

After Founder Approval, every screenplay becomes **Locked Production Canon**.

The screenplay becomes the authoritative source for:
- Dialogue
- Scene Order
- Emotional Beats
- Character Actions
- Character Intent
- Scene Transitions
- Pacing
- Cinematography Intent
- Production Notes

No production department may alter screenplay content without Founder authorization.

---

## Production Authority Hierarchy

The permanent hierarchy becomes:

1. Master System Prompt
2. Locked Canon
3. Production Bible
4. Approved Production Packages
5. Approved Screenplays
6. Storyboards
7. Asset Production
8. Animation
9. Editing
10. Final Delivery

Higher authority always overrides lower authority.

---

## Screenplay Lock Rule

After approval, the following become permanently locked:
- Dialogue
- Pacing
- Scene Order
- Emotional Beats
- Scene Transitions
- Cinematography Intent
- Production Notes

No silent edits are permitted.

---

## Screenplay Revision Rule

Any future screenplay revision requires:
- Canon Justification
- Founder Review
- Regression Verification
- Appropriate Specialty Verification
- Founder Approval

No exception.

---

## Production Rule

Every production department must build directly from the Approved Screenplay.

Never from:
- Memory
- Production Packages
- Character Bible
- Previous Drafts

Those documents explain intent. The screenplay governs production.

---

## Canon Conflict Rule

If production discovers:
- Continuity conflict
- Dialogue conflict
- Missing scene
- Missing transition
- Production contradiction
- Governance conflict

Production stops immediately. No workaround may be invented.

---

## Founder Override

Only the Founder may authorize:
- Screenplay revisions
- Dialogue changes
- Pacing changes
- Emotional changes
- Scene additions
- Scene removals

---

## Applicability

This document applies to all 36 Approved Phase 5B screenplays (Episodes 1–36), effective immediately upon this document's own Founder Approval, and to every screenplay produced under any future phase of this production.
