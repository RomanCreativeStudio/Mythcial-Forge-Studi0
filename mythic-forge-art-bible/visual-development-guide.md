# THE FRACTURE PROTOCOL — ART BIBLE
## Visual Development Guide
### Version 3.0 — Master Index

**Classification:** Internal — Visual Law
**Scope:** Governs the visual rendering of The Fracture Protocol, starting with Series 01.
**Absolute authority:** `bible/Fracture_Protocol_Master_System_Prompt_v1.2.md` — this Art Bible expands on it and must never contradict it.
**Relationship to other documents:** The World Bible (`bible/Fracture_Protocol_World_Bible_v1.1.md`) and Series Bible are authoritative on *what things mean*. This Art Bible is authoritative on *how everything looks*. Where a production decision is purely visual, this document outranks personal taste — "what looks cool" loses to "what this document specifies" (see `global-consistency-rules.md`).

---

### Core Visual Identity — One Register

**Register: Semi-Realistic Anime Cinematic Hybrid** (Master System Prompt, Section 8). High-detail facial focus, emotional lighting-driven storytelling, cinematic depth-of-field framing. This is the **only** sanctioned register — it governs both the animated footage and all promotional stills (thumbnails, concept art, social/marketing images). No shot or still may shift to a different register (flat cel-shading, photoreal hyperrealism, painterly-fantasy) without a documented, founder-approved exception (`global-consistency-rules.md`).

### The Five Pillars

1. **Cost is visible.** Every use of Cipher-work or Fragment-Sync must look like it costs something — strain, glow dimming under exertion, fatigue — never a free, decorative flourish (`system-interference-visual-rules.md`).
2. **Realism grounds the fiction.** Materials, light, and physics behave plausibly; stylization lives in color and mood, never in broken material logic (`materials-and-textures.md`, `lighting-system.md`).
3. **Each layer of the megacity has a distinct visual signature.** Upper Grid, Mid Zone, Lower Sector, and the Unknown Layer never share a dominant palette or architectural language (`color-language.md`, `environment-design-rules.md`).
4. **Light carries emotion.** Lighting mode is chosen by story beat, not by scene convenience (`lighting-system.md`, `cinematic-mood-guide.md`).
5. **No magic, ever.** Every phenomenon reads as system interference, data corruption, or perception/emotional instability — never as fantasy magic (`forbidden-elements.md`, `system-interference-visual-rules.md`).

This Art Bible follows the studio-wide `v{major}.{minor}` convention (Studio OS Section 30). A **[LOCKED]** rule requires founder/Creative Director sign-off to change; a **[LIVING]** rule may be expanded within its existing framework via the same Review Workflow used for world canon (Studio OS Section 18).

All character designs — including Kael and the full Series 01 cast — must obey every file in this Art Bible, and the Master System Prompt, without exception.
