# Lighting System
### Mythic Forge Art Bible v1.0

**Governs:** natural light, night lighting, magical lighting, and the emotional lighting-mode system used across all production.

---

## Natural Light **[LOCKED]**

Sun/sky light is warm-neutral and soft by default, always motivated by a visible or clearly implied source. Flat, directionless ambient fill is forbidden — every lit surface should be able to point back to where its light came from.

## Night Lighting **[LOCKED]**

Default night language is a **cool ambient base** (moon/starlight) contrasted against **warm practical sources** (torches, forge-light, Sigil-lanterns). This warm/cool contrast is the default grammar for every night scene, across every Realm — Realm identity is carried by *which* warm sources appear (a forge vs. a Sigil-lantern vs. bioluminescent Verdant growth), not by breaking the underlying contrast rule.

## Magical Lighting **[LOCKED framework, LIVING per-Aspect detail]**

Essence-based light sources cast light consistent with their Aspect's color identity (exact values in `color-language.md`). Rules that apply to all seven:

- Magical light obeys physical falloff, occlusion, and shadow-casting exactly like practical light — it is never a flat, unshadowed glow laid over a scene.
- Intensity and steadiness scale with the caster's skill and the cost being paid (Bible Sections 20–21): a strained caster's light should visibly flicker, dim, or waver. A glowing Sigil at full, steady brightness reads as *effortless* — reserve that for high-tier, well-earned displays only.
- Echo apparitions (Bible Section 18) are never lit as solid character models — they are translucent, light-distorting presences that bend and dim practical light passing through them rather than emitting flat glow.

## Emotional Lighting Modes **[LOCKED]**

Three lighting modes are chosen by story beat, never by scene convenience:

| Mode | Use | Visual Language |
|---|---|---|
| **Grounded** | Everyday scenes, establishing shots | Naturalistic, motivated, low contrast |
| **Charged** | Confrontation, revelation, rising tension | Heightened contrast, strong directional key light |
| **Wounded** | Rift/Fracture-adjacent horror, loss, trauma beats | Desaturated, harsh single-source or top-down light |

**Rule:** even in a Wounded-lit scene, at least one warm light source should remain visible somewhere in frame — this is the visual expression of the brand's "hopeful" tone pillar (Studio OS Section 4) and must never be sacrificed for a fully nihilistic look.
