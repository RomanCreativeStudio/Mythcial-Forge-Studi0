# Global Consistency Rules
### Mythic Forge Art Bible v2.0

**Governs:** the master rules that apply across every file in this Art Bible, and how conflicts between documents (or between a document and a production impulse) are resolved.

---

## What Must Never Change **[LOCKED]**

1. **Render registers.** Exactly two are sanctioned (`visual-development-guide.md`): Painterly Cinematic 3D for all animated footage, and Semi-Realistic Cinematic (anime-influenced, still painterly-lit) for promotional stills only (`prompt-library.md`). No shot of actual animated footage may shift to the Promotional register or any other medium (e.g., a flat cel-shaded dream sequence) without a documented, deliberate, founder-approved exception logged here. No promotional still may be mistaken for in-show footage or vice versa.
2. **Realm-to-palette assignment.** Once a Realm's anchor hue family is used in production (`color-language.md`), it is permanent. Realms may never be reassigned or share a dominant hue family.
3. **Magic always costs something visible.** This is enforced simultaneously by `magic-visual-rules.md` (the visual rule) and Bible Section 21 (the narrative/mechanical rule) — the two must never drift out of sync.
4. **The Relic Exception is the only realism exception.** No other object category may claim "too-perfect" material treatment (`materials-and-textures.md`) — if it isn't a Relic, it shows wear.
5. **Character Reference Sheets are the single source of truth.** Once a named character's Reference Sheet exists (Production OS Section 11), no shot may reinterpret their color, proportions, or material spec. A needed change goes through the sheet first, never through a one-off shot decision.

## Conflict Resolution **[LOCKED]**

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| This Art Bible vs. a production impulse ("it would look cooler if...") | This Art Bible wins, unless changed through the Review Workflow below |
| Two files within this Art Bible appear to disagree | Treat as a documentation bug — flag immediately, do not guess which one is "more right" |
| This Art Bible vs. Mythic Bible / Series Bible on a point of *meaning* (not visuals) | The Bible/Series Bible wins — this document only governs *how* canon is rendered, never *what* it means |
| This Art Bible vs. Studio OS or Production OS on a *process* question | Studio OS / Production OS win — this document is not a process document |

## Cross-Document Enforcement **[LOCKED]**

A change to any **[LOCKED]** rule anywhere in this Art Bible requires the same Canon Change process used for world canon (Bible Section 56, Knowledge Base Section 34): proposed as a draft, checked against the Originality Framework and all other Locked rules for contradiction, and signed off by the Creative Director before it takes effect. A version bump and changelog entry (per file) follows automatically once approved.
