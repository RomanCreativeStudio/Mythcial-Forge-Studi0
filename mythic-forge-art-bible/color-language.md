# Color Language
### Mythic Forge Art Bible v3.0

**Governs:** the megacity's core palette assignment, saturation and tone restrictions, and Kael's fixed eye-glow specification.

---

## Core Palette Rule **[LOCKED — Master System Prompt Section 8]**

The Fracture Protocol's color language is deliberately simple and load-bearing — each color means something specific, and that meaning never shifts:

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Black / charcoal | Identity / system reality — the baseline visual register for the megacity itself: architecture, Ward Division uniforms, ordinary technology |
| Cyan / icy blue | Fracture anomalies — Kael's eye-glow, Cipher-work, Fragment apparitions, Breach residue. Never used decoratively; its presence always means something anomalous or system-significant is active |
| White | Authority system control — Upper Grid architecture, official Authority signage and interfaces, the visual "voice" of the governing system |

**Rule:** no other zone-specific palette exists. Do not invent per-district or per-character color systems beyond this table without founder sign-off (Master System Prompt, Consistency Enforcement System).

## Saturation and Tone Restrictions **[LOCKED]**

- The baseline palette across the megacity leans **desaturated-naturalistic**, dominated by black/charcoal. Full saturation is not a default — it is an earned accent.
- Saturation is reserved for cyan fracture-anomaly moments and white Authority-control moments. A saturated color appearing on screen should always mean *something significant is active here* — never decorative background color.
- This restriction is the direct visual expression of "power has a cost" (World Bible Section 7): color intensity is rationed the same way Signal is.

## Kael Eye-Glow Specification **[LOCKED — Master System Prompt Section 4]**

Kael's eyes are a fixed physical signature — icy blue-cyan at baseline, intensifying with his Signal-Sensitivity state:

| State | Glow Behavior | Color Value |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | Stable glow | Muted icy cyan, low intensity |
| Conflict | Flickering, unstable glow | Icy cyan with irregular flicker, medium intensity |
| Activation (Cipher-work or Fragment-Sync in use) | Intense, sharp glow | Saturated icy cyan-white, high intensity |
| Breakdown (bond fraying, Burnout onset) | Fragmented glitch glow | Icy cyan breaking into visible discontinuous fragments, with faint desaturation at the edges |

**Rule:** never render Kael's eyes at Activation intensity in a scene where his Signal-Sensitivity isn't actually active — this glow is a diagnostic readout of his internal state, not a stylistic default. This must render identically across every episode and every promotional asset (Master System Prompt, Consistency Enforcement System).
